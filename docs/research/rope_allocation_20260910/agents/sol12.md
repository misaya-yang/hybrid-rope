# Sol12 — failure transcript audit and corrected allocation principle

## Result

The recurring mistake was more specific than “using a proxy”: the work repeatedly erased the label that distinguishes **useful matched signal** from **content-confusable interference**, optimized their pooled geometric response, and only afterward asked generation to decide whether the response was useful. This is why a smoother or lower-energy table could be mathematically successful and behaviorally worse. The clearest counterexample is Smooth MrBudget: it improves every audited unsigned geometry family yet is worse than MrPro on the existing 128K development panel (`.agents/rope_unification_20260910/reports/astra02.md:137`; `.agents/rope_unification_20260910/reports/astra03.md:12-20`).

The strongest rule consistent with the transcript is therefore:

> Allocate frequencies to maximize a certified **signed relevant-versus-distractor margin**, including its covariance and distractor multiplicity, while protecting already-useful computations. Which coefficients may co-adapt is part of the problem definition.

This gives one framework for scratch, adapted, and frozen regimes without pretending they have one optimizer. EVQ is recovered only under an exchangeable scratch/co-adaptation model in which useful signal is allocation-constant and allocation changes only interference covariance. Frozen deployment is a labeled finite-slot transport problem in which the useful mean changes with the table. MrRoPE is an evidenced structured reference and feasible initialization, not a theorem that high frequencies must remain native or slow frequencies must be divided by exactly the scale.

No new Qwen table follows from the evidence currently available. The missing object is not another geometry statistic but content-conditioned, source-labeled mean/covariance of the actual relevant-versus-hard-distractor contrasts.

## Non-redundant failure ledger

The dialogue first reduced the problem to zero-training deployment, then scheduled full fine-tuning arms for YaRN/MrRoPE/Z. The user corrected both substitutions: the requested contribution was a three-regime story (scratch, adapter, frozen), while YaRN and MrRoPE were zero-training references and adaptation belonged to the proposed method. The corrected constraint is not “all regimes share one table”; it is “all regimes share one allocation question and clearly state what may co-adapt.”

Later, a near-native/far-P2 length-dependent operator was proposed. The user correctly observed that it changed the operator class, required pairwise attention machinery rather than a single RoPE table, introduced an unsupported 512 boundary, and overlapped SelfExtend/Jet-Long. It was withdrawn before implementation. This rules out escaping an allocation failure by silently introducing distance routing, multiple attention branches, or dynamic remapping.

### 2. Design choices were repeatedly promoted into laws

“High frequency unchanged,” “low frequency `/s`,” strict frequency monotonicity, and a particular MrPro transition were each temporarily treated as required. The transcript then corrected each: they are motivated design choices, not optimality conditions. The claimed 44.8% limit on raising the first slow slot was only a consequence of holding the preceding slot fixed and imposing strict order; an earlier useful table was nonmonotone. Conversely, dropping all structure is also unsupported: unordered/permuted deployments can catastrophically fail even when support is unchanged (`paper-2027/research/attention-aware-retrofit/results/operator-analysis/SCALE_ORBIT_BOUNDARY_VALIDATION_RESULT_20260904.md:65-69,88-99`).

The correct invariant is labeled computation, not an aesthetically preferred curve. A joint permutation of frequency and learned coefficient slots is equivalent; changing the table alone is not.

### 3. Correct geometry was mistaken for a causal bridge

Several candidates were justified by phase coverage, first-zero horizons, scale transport, weak-subspace energy, Q/K projection norms, or smoother transitions. These quantities can diagnose how a table differs. They do not determine whether a response belongs to the answer source or a distractor. The project already contains direct counterexamples:

- useful p2 and a failed exact-chain table can match coarse scale-orbit quantities while differing sharply in behavior (`SCALE_ORBIT_BOUNDARY_VALIDATION_RESULT_20260904.md:67-73,139-150`);
- free headwise gain obtains much lower teacher-forced loss yet is a resolved negative against frequency-only on the downstream contrast (`HEADWISE_FACTORIZED_Z_AND_SCALE_FLOW_RESULT_20260902.md:137-167,274-306`);
- the 128K cross-cache diagnostic lets BM reading retrieve the correct answer from a MrPro-formed prefix, while a BM-formed prefix remains wrong under MrPro reading (`docs/research/ROPE_BM_CROSS_CACHE_20260908.md:32-62`). Thus prefix formation, reading, and final output cannot be collapsed into one “positional quality” number;
- more literal record coverage can coexist with worse generation: target-record layer/head coverage rose from 29.75 to 67.125 while prose exact answers fell from 7/8 to 6/8 (`docs/research/TWO_CORE_CONTINUATION_RESULTS_20260910.md:129-134`).

### 5. Candidate generation substituted for resolving the unknown

The transcript contains repeated cycles of a new curve, several arms, gates, and a promised follow-up. The user repeatedly objected that the method itself had not advanced. ### 6. Direct gradients and generic QPs already failed the relevant test

The archived 64-dimensional behavior-gradient route used too few heterogeneous samples and failed its unopened holdout. Renaming it “signed gradient,” MGDA, common descent, or a constrained QP does not repair the missing population definition. A useful optimizer must be downstream of a defensible statistical object. The mean/covariance rule below supplies such an object conditionally; it does not make sparse calibration sufficient by itself.

### 7. Gain, table, model, and length were repeatedly mixed

Historical successes often bind a complete table×gain operating point. The zero-training summary explicitly says missing raw ownership prevents attributing natural NLL to table or excluding gain (`ZERO_TRAINING_TWO_DAY_EXPERIMENT_SUMMARY_20260902.md:182-186`). These are conditional observations, not contradictions to be averaged away.

The corrected object must index regime, checkpoint, table, gain, length, task operation, decoder, and scorer. Pooling is allowed only under a stated exchangeability or transport assumption.

### 8. Cosh, exact EVQ, and Z are not interchangeable

The transcript correctly records that support retargeting reversed a Cosh gain and that Z was repeatedly mislabeled as Cosh. Astra02 adds a harder obstruction: for the exact finite-window collision kernel, the unique measure optimum is finite atomic, not a positive Cosh density (`.agents/rope_unification_20260910/reports/astra02.md:1-73`). Cosh follows from the delta-plus-min approximation with an anti-concentration term; it is not the exact universal allocation law. Therefore neither a Cosh win nor an exact collision optimum may be installed in frozen Qwen merely because it lowers the declared geometry objective.

## Corrected mathematical rule

For operation cell `r`, let `+` denote its lawful source and `h=1,...,H_r` its content-confusable distractors. For labeled rotary slot `j`, deployment frequency is

\[
\nu_j(x)=\omega_j e^{-x_j}.
\]

With the actual pre-RoPE query/key pair, each source-versus-distractor contrast has the exact form

\[
D_{rh}(x)=\sum_j c_{rhj}^{\top} f_{rhj}(x)+b_{rh},
\]

where `f` contains the relevant and distractor sine/cosine phases at their signed lags, and `c` contains the corresponding signed Q/K bilinear coefficients. Let its transported mean and variance be

\[
m_{rh}(x)=\mu_{rh}^{\top}f_{rh}(x)+b_{rh},\qquad
s_{rh}^2(x)=f_{rh}(x)^\top\Sigma_{rh}f_{rh}(x).
\]

If the centered contrasts are sub-Gaussian under the declared operation population, then for required pairwise margin `eta_r`,

\[
\Pr[\exists h:D_{rh}(x)\le\eta_r]
\le \sum_{h=1}^{H_r}
\exp\!\left[-\frac{(m_{rh}(x)-\eta_r)^2}{2s_{rh}^2(x)}\right].
\]

For only finite second moments, replace the exponential term by the one-sided Cantelli bound. For desired source attention mass `1-a_r`, a sufficient common pairwise requirement is

\[
\eta_r=\log\frac{H_r(1-a_r)}{a_r}.
\]

This explicitly includes the softmax crowd penalty that phase or pairwise-cosine proxies omit.

The allocation rule is

\[
\max_{x\in\mathcal F}\ \min_{r\in R_{target},h}
\frac{m_{rh}(x)-\eta_r}{s_{rh}(x)}
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
\frac{m_{rh}(x)-\eta_r}{s_{rh}(x)}\ge \bar z_{rh}
\ \forall r\in R_{protected}.
\tag{SRI}
\]

`F` contains only architectural requirements and the validity region of the measured moment envelope. A local trust box is justified when it is needed to control upstream-statistic drift or Taylor remainder; it is not a generic preference for small changes. Frequency order, endpoints, high/native blocks, or `/s` tails belong in `F` only when the chosen operator or retained evidence requires them. MrPro supplies the initial labeled table and a strong observed baseline.

### Scratch

When channels and weights co-adapt and the useful normalized signal is exchangeable and allocation-constant, maximizing SRI reduces to minimizing interference covariance. With independent loading noise plus nested slow-tail noise this takes the EVQ form

\[
\alpha\int\rho^2+\beta\int S_\rho(t)^2dt.
\]

The Cosh density follows only for that approximate continuum model. Under the exact finite-window kernel the optimum is atomic, and under finite equal-weight channels an additional quantization problem remains. Scratch training can learn slot associations after the allocation is chosen; this is the assumption that frozen deployment lacks.

### Adapter

An adapter changes both the allocation and the coefficient population. Alternate or jointly optimize `(x,adapter)` against the same SRI target, while measuring the new `mu,Sigma` after adaptation and retaining the native protected set. This is not “give our arm more budget and call it a frequency win.” The attributable claim is a co-adapted allocation method unless matched adaptation controls are run.

### Frozen

Freeze the checkpoint and estimate labeled `mu,Sigma` from qualified source-dependent operations with actual hard distractors, prefix states, GQA sharing, gain, and signed lags. Solve SRI locally from MrPro with finite-change certification, then evaluate exact trigonometric margins. If Smooth still ranks better on the very rows where it generates worse output, the selected operation level is insufficient; the next missing object is upstream state or value/readout behavior, not another geometry penalty.

## Counterexample checks

1. **Zero mean.** If all useful signed means vanish, covariance minimization cannot create a positive selection guarantee. This blocks a common EVQ-to-frozen extrapolation.
2. **Isotropic independent nuisance.** With isotropic independent sine/cosine noise, rotating frequency changes no projected variance. A collision benefit requires coherent lag- or cross-slot-conditioned nuisance, which must be measured.
3. **Signal/interference reversal.** A frequency response can be desirable for matched content and harmful for coherent distractors. Any unsigned response objective necessarily aliases these two cases.
4. **Many distractors.** Positive average or pairwise margin is insufficient at 128K; `eta_r` grows with distractor count. This explains why replaying a 64K phase does not replay its selection probability.
5. **Downstream readout.** Source selection does not prove answer generation. A generation guarantee additionally needs the correct value to be written and the decoder/EOS trajectory to preserve the target token at every required step.
6. **Upstream drift.** Frozen-Q/K replay is conditional. If changing the table alters previous-layer activations outside the stated moment envelope, the bound no longer applies.

## Decision consequence

Do not derive another frequency curve from Cosh, first-zero, spectral smoothness, weak-subspace energy, or an undifferentiated behavior gradient. Before emitting one Qwen candidate, calculate the signed source-versus-hard-distractor margin mean and covariance for existing MrPro, Smooth, E1, and P2 on the same qualified operations. The rule earns a candidate only if it explains the Smooth reversal and preserves the observed E1/P2 conditional directions without looking at held-out generation labels. If it cannot, report which layer of the causal chain is missing: source labeling, prefix formation, value transport, or decoder trajectory.

This is the narrow constructive improvement over the historical loop: it tells us what evidence is required, why scratch and frozen differ, how distractor count enters, and when the optimizer must return “not identified” instead of manufacturing another table.

## Findings added by the complete source pass

### A test can faithfully certify an invalid scientific premise

The old remediation handoff declared the generalized three-fiber non-identifiability theorem “fully remediated,” and its test harness verifies the report's own matrix and labels (`.agents/worker_remediation_1/handoff.md`; `tests/test_challenger_remediation_verification.py`). Likewise the equality of head-first and token-first energies is a reordering of the same finite sum, not a new physical selector. This is an unusually clean process counterexample: passing code can reproduce algebra and still fail to validate the scientific quantifiers. Tests should check derivations and implementations; theorem scope requires an independent proof and counterexample audit.

### Heterogeneous crossings do not create a universal crossover law

The external optimization plan tries to combine seven protocols into a universal 1x–2x “exchange-rate law” (`/Users/misaya.yanghejazfs.com.au/Downloads/ICLR2027_EVQ_优化执行方案.md`). The full corpus shows why that is not yet admissible: the rows mix architectures, training histories, metrics, gains, physical windows, and adaptation regimes; Video-DiT is already an exception; and support retargeting reverses the scratch ordering. Phase17B is explicitly a staged continuation rather than an equal-budget comparison (`docs/exp/2026-03/2026-03-10_phase17b_1024_continue_vs_512_baseline.md`). A common sign-change interval is a descriptive pattern. It cannot determine a frozen allocation without a common estimand or prospective prediction.

### Scratch evidence supports allocation activity, not the historical universal story

The L=256 report records strong three-seed raw-PPL improvement for tau=4 outside the training window, but its claims that advantage grows monotonically and that tau=4 verifies the theoretical optimum are stronger than the table: the 32x relative gain is slightly smaller than 16x, and the sweep only compares tau 2 and 4 (`docs/exp/2026-03/2026-03-04_phase11_L256_results.md`). The curated 454M table cleanly shows that EVQ and YaRN can interact on teacher-forced passkey NLL-gap, while also documenting different absolute-PPL conventions (`data/curated/table2_evq_yarn_454m_passkey_10pct.json`). These results support scratch/co-adapted allocation as active. They do not identify the constant-signal covariance model or transfer its optimum to a frozen checkpoint.

### Smooth BM provides the cross-checkpoint counterexample the rule must predict

The same boundary-matched construction is very strong on OLMo: seven additional 16K RULER tasks average 41.67% versus MrPro's 7.09% (`docs/research/ROPE_OLMO_BM_EXTRA_RULER_RESULT_20260908.md`). On Qwen7B it has no wins, three losses, and fifteen ties, with 128K QA falling from 50% to 0 (`docs/research/ROPE_QWEN7_BM_TRANSFER_20260908.md`). On Qwen3B natural NLL, BM-minus-MrPro is +.00425 at 16K with a positive paired interval, but is unresolved near zero at 8K/32K (`docs/research/ROPE_QWEN3_BM_NLL_RESULT_20260908.json`). This rules out both “smoothing is universally beneficial” and “BM failed.” It demands checkpoint- and operation-conditioned signal statistics.

### Exact EVQ and continuum Cosh separate at the optimizer, not only at deployment

Astra02 proves that the exact bounded finite-window cosine-kernel problem has a unique finite atomic probability-measure optimum. The Cosh density instead belongs to the delta-plus-min covariance approximation, whose identity term adds an anti-concentration cost. At finite equal-weight K, unrestricted atom masses also require quantization. This closes a hidden escape route in which one merely “corrects the EVQ kernel” and expects another smooth universal density: exact collision geometry changes the optimization class and still remains unlabeled with respect to task signal.

### Existing code often encodes a narrower metric than its surrounding narrative

The passkey evaluators explicitly separate teacher-forced NLL-gap from autoregressive exact match (`scripts/supporting_eval/eval_passkey_scratch.py`), and several result owners warn that the two diverge. The zero-training tournament optimizes teacher-forced Native-prefix, dense, and tail NLL rather than generated source binding (`scripts/eval/eval_zero_training_tournament.py`). The sparse-memory experiment's extensive execution safeguards could not repair a task that first had a query-blind shortcut and later lacked baseline query learnability (`docs/research/SPARSE_MEMORY_INTERFACE_PILOT_20260908.md`). These are not reasons to discard the code. They require naming the estimand before treating a successful run as evidence for allocation.
