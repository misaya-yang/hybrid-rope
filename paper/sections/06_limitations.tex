\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

This mechanism study isolates training-time allocation and claims PE diagnostics, not universal accuracy. Theory is exact only for the surrogate: realistic priors and $L_{\mathrm{eff}}^{J}$ are unmeasured; $\tau$ is a basin default; MLA/per-head choices remain unablated. Composition is YaRN-specific: EVQ4+NTK loses to Geo+NTK at $L{=}256$, $32\times$ ($331.4$ vs. $198.1$; Appendix Table~\ref{tab:phase11-leverage}). Video/LoRA/progressive rows are supporting. EVQ adds no inference operations but reduces neither attention/KV-cache complexity nor training FLOPs; energy and deployment gains require direct validation.
