## (f) Where I think the transcript's own stated reason is wrong or under-determined

1. **F2's mechanism claim is under-determined by its own design (agent partially acknowledges this).** The doc at L356 says "成败/召回均跟随前缀来源；这排除了'只切换最后query和生成期的全局读取表…就能修复这两例'的方案". But the intervention also changed the rotation applied to the *final prompt query and every generated token*, so the "read table" factor was never manipulated alone. The doc does state this limit ("改变读取表也改变最后query和生成轨迹各层状态…本实验不是纯key效应的完整识别"), so the *doc* is honest — but the one-line summaries (L319/L320: "仅切换读取表不足以修复失败", and the HANDOFF line at L356) do read as a cleaner causal claim than the design supports. Also: n=2, both retrospective cases whose outputs were already seen. **The rule it licenses is narrower than the summary implies: "do not expect a decode-side table swap to repair these two specific cases", not "the failure lives in prefill state".**

2. **F3's stated reason is a list of candidates, not a reason.** The result doc says the candidate "还压缩了过渡区、增加了大gap的数量及粗糙度，改变了长距联合相位关系；不能将这些几何事实任一项单独指定为失败的唯一原因" — i.e. it explicitly declines to name a cause. That is honest, but it means the Gap-capped failure did *not* refute any specific hypothesis; it only refuted "these two geometry objectives are sufficient". I would record the reason as **"reason not identified"** rather than as the three-item list. Note this is the *stronger* reading for future work: the shard has no explanation for why a lower-native-displacement, gap-respecting table loses 15.97pp at 128K.

3. **F1's "不能单独归因于模型容量" is correct, but the alternative offered is weak.** The 3B smoke doc (L167) says the result "不能单独归因于模型容量" and the follow-up diagnosis attributes the failure to prefill-formed state. Given F2's under-determination (point 1), the chain "3B fails → because of prefill-formed KV state" rests on one 2×2 experiment with n=2 retrospective cases, plus the C/P vs L contrast. The C/P vs L contrast (P7: P=100%/100% vs L=0%/60% with the same visible token/position set) is the genuinely load-bearing evidence, and it is stronger than the cross-cache result. **I would weight P7 over F2 as the mechanism evidence, and the transcript largely does the opposite in its summaries.**
