Thank you. Your review isolates the three questions that matter most, and we have treated them in that order. The new evidence materially addresses the first and third; for the YaRN comparison it supports a precise but narrower claim than the submission implied.

**1. Evidence breadth and endpoint type.** Your concern has two parts: the endpoints were PE-dominated and teacher-forced, and the models were small rather than pretrained LLMs. We agree that extrapolated perplexity and teacher-forced NLL-gap retrieval cannot establish usable context. The new evidence changes both: free-running generation instead of teacher-forced scoring, and mature pretrained models instead of small ones.

On OLMo-2 (1.485B actual parameters), with every LoRA backward pass physically capped at 4K, a matched Native/EVQ pair scores 0/100 versus 69/100 on strict autoregressive first-number exact at 8K on the official niah_single_1 task; an independently trained EVQ seed scores 67/100 on the same rows. The Native arm is matched rather than untouched, the evaluation rows and needle values are freshly generated and disjoint from training, and the endpoint is free-running generation rather than teacher-forced scoring. On a further 100-row set in which every generation-boundary gap — the distance from the source answer to the point where generation begins — exceeds the largest such gap available anywhere in 4K routing training (3,933 tokens; realized gaps 3,959–8,013), with zero query, source, value or answer overlap with training, calibration or prior evaluation rows, Native remains 0/100 while the two EVQ adapters score 49/100 and 48/100. That evaluation is pure inference with frozen adapters; the paired exact McNemar values are 3.6e-15 and 7.1e-15.

On LLaMA-3-8B, under identical physical-8K supervision over the same 13 RULER families, EVQ raises 16K official macro from 0.295% to 14.03%: at 2× the training length Native-LoRA is at essentially zero while EVQ retains nontrivial capability. Two of those 13 families are built from real-document QA datasets, SQuAD v2 and HotpotQA distractor, with human-written questions over real passages. At 16K, Native-LoRA's normalized-exact score is zero on every one of the 13 tasks, while EVQ-LoRA has normalized-exact successes including the HotpotQA task, so the effect reaches real-document multi-hop QA and not needle retrieval alone. At 8K — the training length itself, so in-window rather than extrapolation — the two metrics disagree and we report both: Native leads official macro (94.44% versus 77.60%), which credits partial and substring matches, while EVQ leads normalized exact (21.54% versus 17.69%).

Together these move the evidence off perplexity and teacher-forced scoring: the effect now appears in free-running generation, on a standard effective-context benchmark, and on real-document multi-hop QA, at pretrained 1.485B and 8B scale. Under identical physical-4K supervision over all 13 RULER families, OLMo-2 Native/EVQ official macro is 82.16%/37.51% at 4K, 0.08%/21.29% at 8K, and 0%/6.13% at 16K. Native learns the in-window distribution more strongly; EVQ supplies the 2×/4× length transfer, including 8K HotpotQA of 0% versus 25%. A matched 8B adaptation also speaks to whether the model is genuinely using distant content rather than reconstructing it locally. Native-LoRA/EVQ-LoRA NLL is 1.919/2.309 at 8K, 4.691/3.181 at 16K and 6.899/4.851 at 32K, with the 16K and 32K direction holding in all 24 evaluation packs and all three domains. At true 16K, median target-block hit@16 rises from 18.75% to 64.06%, and deleting the remote gold block from every head worsens EVQ NLL by 1.506 while leaving the Native control essentially unchanged. Top-1 generation is not solved at that scale, so this is probability and causal-routing evidence rather than capability. The submission already contained a 750M continuation (8K strict autoregressive exact 0% → 77.5%, PPL@16K 45.1 → 24.4) and a 432M scarce-channel MLA study over three seeds (PPL@16K 138.8±5.5 → 95.6±4.1 with only 16 rotary channels), alongside the 129M/382M video-DiT experiments.

**2. Theory-to-practice separation.** We will keep four levels explicitly distinct: (i) the conditional theorem, exact only for the stated convex surrogate C_app; (ii) exact-kernel diagnostics, which are empirical checks rather than proofs; (iii) trained-model results, which test the predicted direction without validating the surrogate itself; and (iv) the τ rule, where the derivation motivates τ ∝ d_eff / √L_train while setting the proportionality constant to one is an empirical operating convention rather than a theorem or a point optimum.

The strongest trained-model connection we have is a repeated direction rather than a confirmation of the surrogate. Reallocating a finite channel budget toward long range should incur a small in-window cost and produce a larger extrapolation gain. In the 1.485B from-scratch branch the EVQ−Geo NLL delta moves monotonically with the extrapolation ratio: +0.0724 at 2K, +0.0381 at 4K, −0.0437 at 8K, −0.1351 at 16K, with paired document-level bootstrap 95% intervals excluding zero at every length. The same ordering reappears in the 1.485B adaptation (4K 2.235 → 2.548 against 16K 4.851 → 2.925) and at 8B (8K +0.390 against 32K −2.048). Several independent trained models could have contradicted that prediction and did not. We present this as directional consistency, not as evidence that the surrogate is correct: it supplies the direction, not the crossover length.

**3. YaRN.** We agree that a common fixed scale does not rule out a better-tuned Geo+YaRN or a channel-wise search, and we did not run that joint sweep. Submitted Table 3 applies the same fixed s = 8 transform to both substrates across three training seeds: Geo+YaRN versus EVQ+YaRN obtains 61±3% versus 100±0% teacher-forced NLL-gap retrieval at 8K, and PPL 82.9 versus 70.9. The supported conclusion is substrate dependence — the training-time frequency table changes what the same inference-time transform can recover — and we will state it that way rather than as complementarity, which would suggest a stable additive gain we have not established. Settling the tuned comparison would require a joint sweep over s, β_fast, β_slow and mscale for both substrates at matched budget.

We would add one clarification, since this is the item that remains open. The unresolved tuned comparison bounds what we may claim about EVQ×YaRN. It does not bear on the standalone allocation result, which rests on fixed zero-parameter schedule controls in which the positional operator, training protocol, parameter count and tuning budget are identical across arms and only the frequency table differs.

These are task-family-adapted results: the training and evaluation rows are disjoint, but the generator families are shared, so this is length transfer within a task family. Each matched comparison uses one training seed per arm; on OLMo a second independently trained EVQ seed reproduces both the 8K result (67/100) and the beyond-gap result (48/100) on the same evaluation rows.
