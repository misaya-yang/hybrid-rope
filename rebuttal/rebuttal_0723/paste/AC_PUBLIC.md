We thank the reviewers and the AC. We address the three conditions in the metareview in the order stated there.

**1. Technical distinction.** We should have cited Oka et al. (FMRoPE) and the revision will. We do not claim novelty for the observation that some RoPE channels are ineffective; §2 of the submission already credits prior work on channel inequality, including Barbero et al. (2025) and Resonance RoPE.

Our claim is about what is optimized and at what stage, and it is easiest to read off the frequency-table formula. In ω_i = b^(-u_i) with u_i = 2i/d we distinguish three parameterization levels. Transport methods optimize ω → g_T(ω) on an already-pretrained spectrum, possibly per-frequency (PI, YaRN, LongRoPE). Base methods optimize the scalar b → b(T) for a declared context, preserving the normalized geometric order (NTK-aware, ABF, FMRoPE). EVQ-Cosh optimizes the third: u_i → φ_τ(u_i), the choice of training grid at fixed nominal base, fixed before the model learns anything, with φ_τ the closed-form inverse CDF of the stationary density of an explicit variational surrogate.

Most prior RoPE extrapolation work acts through the realized spectrum or the base/range; our starting question was whether the exponents should be spaced uniformly at all. The contribution we defend is confined to that level: posing the finite training-time grid as an explicit variational object with a closed-form, zero-learned-parameter realization. We do not claim to be first to modify RoPE frequencies, and the levels are distinguished by what is parameterized and at what stage, not by what is numerically achievable.

**2. Direct controlled comparison.** We ran the control that distinction implies. The FMRoPE rule evaluated here follows §6.1 of the paper (θ_train = L_train, θ_infer = L_target, ω_i(θ) = θ^(-2i/d)); we did not identify a public author implementation as of 23 July, so this is a paper-faithful reimplementation of that rule rather than an official-code reproduction, and we will correct it if the authors specify otherwise.

With the highest sampled frequency, the lowest sampled frequency, the log span, the initialization, the token order, the optimizer, the budget and the 32 evaluation anchors all held identical, and only the 30 interior frequencies changed, the endpoint-normalized Cosh interior allocation improves fixed-range OOD NLL by 0.478/0.205/0.113 at 512/1K/2K, winning 32/32, 27/32 and 22/32 anchors. A separate three-seed factorial reproduces the direction under the same exact-range constraint across bases 500K/1M, training lengths 256/1024 and head dimensions 32/64/128: the preregistered 1.25× Cosh and deformation-matched exponential arms favor the non-geometric allocation in 10/12 and 9/12 structural configurations respectively.

We report the constraining results too. In the retargeted control the ordering reverses (+0.061/+0.182/+0.279): the evaluated uniform-FMR rule obtains lower NLL than endpoint-normalized Cosh at these lengths, consistent with target-aware range selection becoming the larger factor in that condition. Within our own family, a deformation-matched exponential is statistically indistinguishable from Cosh. So the claim is an identifiable allocation axis, not a better method.

**3. Stronger evaluation.** The clearest form of the scale evidence is a trend. The 1.485B from-scratch branch shows a strict monotone relationship between relative gain and extrapolation ratio — the EVQ−Geo NLL delta is +0.0724/+0.0381 at 2K/4K against −0.0437/−0.1351 at 8K/16K, with paired document bootstrap intervals excluding zero at every length. The same in-window-cost / long-range-gain ordering reappears in the 1.485B and 8B adaptation studies, and is directionally consistent with the submitted 150M–750M evidence.

On capability: with 4K-capped backward passes a matched OLMo-2 pair scored 0/100 versus 69/100 strict autoregressive exact at 8K, a second independently trained EVQ seed 67/100, and 49/100 and 48/100 on a set where every source-to-answer gap exceeds training support. On LLaMA-3-8B, 13-family RULER macro at 16K goes from 0.295% to 14.03%. The submission already contained an 8B LoRA scale check, and the 1.485B experiments reproduce it under matched controls.

**4. Attribution.** An independent τ sweep, a 99-run fallibility audit, fixed zero-parameter analytic schedules under an unchanged operator, and the exact-range factorial separate the operating rule, learned capacity and allocation shape. We restate the τ rule as an empirical operating convention: the derivation motivates the scaling τ ∝ d_eff / √L_train, and setting the proportionality constant to one is a calibrated default, not a theorem.

**What we ask.** We ask that EVQ-Cosh be assessed as a simple, zero-parameter, training-time allocation axis with bounded mature-model evidence. We do not claim universal long-context superiority, replacement of target-aware scaling, optimality of Cosh or of the τ rule, unseen-task transfer, or state of the art in any setting. The revision states each of these limits explicitly, adds the Oka et al. citation and comparison, relabels the learned-frequency comparator in the PE-dominant table, and reports the new controls in full.
