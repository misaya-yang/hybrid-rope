We thank the reviewers and the AC. The metareview names three conditions that could change the recommendation — clear technical novelty over FMRoPE, a direct controlled comparison, and stronger evaluation. We have evidence on all three, and we address them in that order.

**1. Technical distinction.** We should have cited Oka et al. (FMRoPE) and the revision will. We do not claim novelty for the observation that some RoPE channels are ineffective; §2 of the submission already credits prior work on channel inequality, including Barbero et al. (2025) and Resonance RoPE.

The distinction is read off the frequency-table formula. In ω_i = b^(-u_i) with u_i = 2i/d, a method can parameterize the realized vector, the base, or the exponent. FMRoPE selects the scalar base/range b → b(T) for a declared context, preserving the normalized geometric order. EVQ-Cosh treats the fixed-base training-time exponent map u_i → φ_τ(u_i) as the unknown and derives it from a stated variational objective, before the model learns anything. Most prior RoPE extrapolation work acts on the realized spectrum or the base/range; our starting question was whether the exponents should be spaced uniformly at all. We do not claim to be first to modify RoPE frequencies: the levels differ in what is parameterized and at what stage, not in what is numerically achievable.

**2. Direct controlled comparison.** The exact-range control shows this is not a notational difference. With the highest sampled frequency, the lowest sampled frequency, the log span, the initialization, the token order, the optimizer, the budget and all 32 evaluation anchors held identical, and only the 30 interior frequencies changed, the endpoint-normalized Cosh interior allocation improves fixed-range OOD NLL by 0.478/0.205/0.113 at 512/1K/2K, winning 32/32, 27/32 and 22/32 anchors. A three-seed factorial reproduces the direction under the same constraint across bases 500K/1M, training lengths 256/1024 and head dimensions 32/64/128, with the pre-specified 1.25× Cosh and deformation-matched exponential arms favoring the non-geometric allocation in 10/12 and 9/12 structural configurations. Since every quantity a scalar base can set is held fixed, the effect is not attributable to base or range selection.

We did not identify a public author implementation as of 23 July, so we used a paper-faithful reimplementation of the rule specified in §6.1: θ_train = L_train, θ_infer = L_target, ω_i(θ) = θ^(-2i/d). Under target retargeting the ordering reverses (+0.061/+0.182/+0.279) and that rule obtains lower NLL, which is what a target-aware range method is for. The requested comparison is positive where it isolates the claimed variable, and the reversal bounds the claim to fixed-range allocation advantage rather than universal superiority.

**3. Stronger evaluation.** The metareview names five gaps; the response addresses each.

*Larger models, with matched controls.* On OLMo-2 (1.485B actual parameters), with every LoRA backward pass capped at physical 4K, a matched Native/EVQ pair scores 0/100 versus 69/100 on strict autoregressive first-number exact at 8K on the official niah_single_1 task, with a second independently trained EVQ seed at 67/100 on the same rows. On a further set in which every generation-boundary gap exceeds the largest such gap available anywhere in 4K training, Native is 0/100 while the two EVQ adapters score 49/100 and 48/100.

*Stronger benchmarks.* On OLMo-2 (1.485B), under identical physical-4K supervision over all 13 RULER families, Native/EVQ official macro is 82.16%/37.51% at 4K, 0.08%/21.29% at 8K, and 0%/6.13% at 16K: Native is stronger in-window, while EVQ supplies the 2×/4× length transfer. On LLaMA-3-8B, under identical physical-8K supervision over the same 13 families, 16K official macro goes from 0.295% to 14.03%. A separate matched 8B adaptation gives the probability-side picture: Native-LoRA/EVQ-LoRA NLL is 1.919/2.309 at 8K, 4.691/3.181 at 16K and 6.899/4.851 at 32K, with the 16K and 32K direction holding in all 24 evaluation packs and all three domains. At true 16K, median target-block hit@16 rises from 18.75% to 64.06%, and deleting the remote gold block from every head worsens EVQ NLL by 1.506 while leaving the Native control essentially unchanged — the adapted model is using the distant content rather than recovering it from local context. Top-1 generation is not solved at that scale, so we report this as probability and routing evidence.

*Real downstream tasks.* Two of those 13 families are built from real-document QA datasets — SQuAD v2 and HotpotQA distractor, with human-written questions over real passages. At 16K, Native-LoRA's normalized-exact score is zero on all 13 tasks while EVQ-LoRA has normalized-exact successes including the HotpotQA task, so the effect reaches real-document multi-hop QA and not needle retrieval alone.

*Base frequencies.* The exact-range factorial above crosses bases 500K/1M and head dimensions 32/64/128 under the same pinned-range construction, so its cross-configuration direction is not confined to the submitted base or head dimension.

*Diverse architectures.* The submission already reported a 432M scarce-channel MLA study over three seeds, where with only 16 rotary channels PPL@16K goes from 138.8±5.5 to 95.6±4.1 and to 71.1±4.1 with the same fixed s = 4 transform (Table 18), plus 129M/382M bidirectional 3D-RoPE video-DiT experiments (Table 14), an 8B LLaMA-3 LoRA evaluation (Appendix D, Table 23) and a 750M continuation whose 8K strict autoregressive exact goes from 0% to 77.5% with PPL@16K from 45.1 to 24.4 (Table 12).

As supporting evidence, a 1.485B from-scratch branch trained from the public step-0 initialization for 2.097B counted tokens shows the same in-window-cost / long-range-gain ordering, with the EVQ−Geo NLL delta moving from +0.0724/+0.0381 at 2K/4K to −0.0437/−0.1351 at 8K/16K and paired document bootstrap intervals excluding zero at every length. It shares initialization, recipe, data-order prefix and evaluation rows with the released geometric checkpoint, which came from the upstream distributed trainer while our branch used a single-GPU loop on the same recipe.

**4. Attribution.** Each confound in the surrogate → Cosh → operating-rule chain now has its own control. An independent τ sweep answers operating-point sensitivity; fixed zero-parameter analytic schedules under an unchanged operator separate allocation shape from learned capacity and tuning effort; the exact-range factorial separates shape from base/range; the base and head-dimension factorial answers robustness.

On the τ rule specifically, the derivation supplies the scaling form τ ∝ d_eff / √L_train but not the proportionality constant, which is a functional of the data dependency structure, the model's feature usage and the span convention. Two independent studies — a 99-run reanalysis over nine configurations and the 12-configuration exact-range factorial — show the predicted behaviour: across all 21 configurations the empirically selected τ never leaves a 0.75×–1.5× window around the formula value, while the best point inside that window moves with the configuration. The default is a zero-search operating point that selects the right basin, not a claimed global optimum, and the revision will present it as that.

**What we ask.** The three conditions in the metareview are met with direct evidence: a formula-level technical distinction, a controlled comparison that isolates it, and matched mature-model results on stronger benchmarks and real-document QA. The remaining boundaries — target-aware retargeting favours the range method, the mature-model results are task-family-adapted and mostly single-seed, and we claim no state of the art — bound the scope of the claim. We do not think they bear on whether the training-time exponent allocation is a distinct, independently identifiable design variable with effects that persist across base, head dimension, architecture and scale.
