**1. Technical distinction.** We should have cited Oka et al. (FMRoPE) and the revision will. We do not claim novelty for the observation that some RoPE channels are ineffective; §2 of the submission already credits prior work on channel inequality, including Barbero et al. (2025) and Resonance RoPE.

The distinction is read off the frequency-table formula. In ω_i = b^(-u_i) with u_i = 2i/d, a method can parameterize the realized vector, the base, or the exponent. FMRoPE selects the scalar base/range b → b(T) for a declared context, preserving the normalized geometric order. EVQ-Cosh treats the fixed-base training-time exponent map u_i → φ_τ(u_i) as the unknown and derives it from a stated variational objective, before the model learns anything. Most prior RoPE extrapolation work acts on the realized spectrum or the base/range; our starting question was whether the exponents should be spaced uniformly at all. We do not claim to be first to modify RoPE frequencies: the levels differ in what is parameterized and at what stage, not in what is numerically achievable.

**2. Direct controlled comparison.** The exact-range control shows this is not a notational difference. With the highest sampled frequency, the lowest sampled frequency, the log span, the initialization, the token order, the optimizer, the budget and all 32 evaluation anchors held identical, and only the 30 interior frequencies changed, the endpoint-normalized Cosh interior allocation improves fixed-range OOD NLL by 0.478/0.205/0.113 at 512/1K/2K, winning 32/32, 27/32 and 22/32 anchors. A three-seed factorial reproduces the direction under the same constraint across bases 500K/1M, training lengths 256/1024 and head dimensions 32/64/128, with the pre-specified 1.25× Cosh and deformation-matched exponential arms favoring the non-geometric allocation in 10/12 and 9/12 structural configurations. Since every quantity a scalar base can set is held fixed, the effect is not attributable to base or range selection.

We implement the published §6.1 rule directly: θ_train = L_train, θ_infer = L_target, ω_i(θ) = θ^(-2i/d). Under target retargeting the ordering reverses (+0.061/+0.182/+0.279) and that rule obtains lower NLL, which is what a target-aware range method is for. The requested comparison is positive where it isolates the claimed variable, and the reversal bounds the claim to fixed-range allocation advantage rather than universal superiority.

On the YaRN claim the metareview also names, submitted Table 3 applies the same fixed s = 8 transform to both substrates across three training seeds: Geo+YaRN versus EVQ+YaRN obtains 61±3% versus 100±0% teacher-forced NLL-gap retrieval at 8K, and PPL 82.9 versus 70.9. The supported claim is substrate dependence — the training-time frequency table changes what the same inference-time transform can recover.

**3. Stronger evaluation.** The metareview names five gaps; the response addresses each.

*Larger models, with matched controls.* The readout was built in three stages, each tightening the attribution. The submitted version showed the effect exists at 8B: LLaMA-3 LoRA PPL goes from 176.3 to 21.5 at 16K and 1942.5 to 104.3 at 32K, a change from unusable to usable at 2× and 4×. We then tested whether a mature model can use that headroom in free-running generation. On OLMo-2 (1.485B actual parameters), matched Native and EVQ arms received an identical downstream chain — a 300-step LoRA parent, a 100-step query-gap alignment and a 32-step answer-plus-EOS stage — with the same trainer, seed, data, optimizer, budget and objective, and every backward pass capped at 4,096 physical tokens. Judged by literal equality of the complete answer string followed by a terminal EOS token, from raw token IDs, Native/EVQ is 95/100 versus 100/100 at 4K, 18/100 versus 98/100 at 8K, and 0/100 versus 60/100 at 16K; EVQ reaches 48/50 and 31/66 on the beyond-training-gap subsets. In every stage the two arms share the base model, LoRA rank, data, seed, optimizer, budget and evaluation rows; the single difference is the fixed frequency table.

*Stronger benchmarks.* On OLMo-2 (1.485B), under identical physical-4K supervision over all 13 RULER families, Native/EVQ official macro is 82.16%/37.51% at 4K, 0.08%/21.29% at 8K, and 0%/6.13% at 16K; we return to the in-window gap below. On LLaMA-3-8B, under identical physical-8K supervision over the same 13 families, 16K official macro goes from 0.295% to 14.03%. A separate matched 8B adaptation gives the probability-side picture: Native-LoRA/EVQ-LoRA NLL is 1.919/2.309 at 8K, 4.691/3.181 at 16K and 6.899/4.851 at 32K, with the 16K and 32K direction holding in all 24 evaluation packs and all three domains. At true 16K, median target-block hit@16 rises from 18.75% to 64.06%, and deleting the remote gold block from every head worsens EVQ NLL by 1.506 while leaving the Native control essentially unchanged — the adapted model is using the distant content rather than recovering it from local context.

*Real downstream tasks.* We also asked whether the in-window cost above belongs to the frequency table or to the adaptation. RoPE acts only on Q and K, so from the matched parents we froze every inherited V/O LoRA tensor — verified bitwise — and continued only Q/K, again at physical 4K. On 200 held-out 2WikiMultiHopQA prompts, exact match is 22.0% versus 21.5% at 4K, 0% versus 17.5% at 8K, and 0% versus 4.0% at 16K. The unadapted parents score 0% exact at every length, so the Q/K continuation confers the task equally on both substrates and only the EVQ substrate carries it past the training length. Under the same restriction the 13-family RULER continuation gives 72.19%/42.44% at 4K, 2.02%/31.63% at 8K and 0.38%/5.03% at 16K: the EVQ arm improves at 4K and 8K and the in-window gap narrows from 44.7 to 29.8 points, so adaptation scope affects that trade-off materially without accounting for all of it. Separately, two of the 13 RULER families are built from real-document QA datasets — SQuAD v2 and HotpotQA distractor, with human-written questions over real passages. At 16K, Native-LoRA's normalized-exact score is zero on all 13 tasks while EVQ-LoRA has normalized-exact successes including the HotpotQA task, so the effect reaches real-document multi-hop QA and not needle retrieval alone.

*Base frequencies.* A three-seed suite at base 1M with d_head = 128, held out from the calibration of the submitted rule, gives mean tail NLL deltas of −0.802/−0.664/−0.433/−0.287/−0.212 at 1K/2K/4K/8K/16K, all three seeds agreeing and paired 95% intervals excluding zero. The exact-range factorial additionally crosses bases 500K/1M and head dimensions 32/64/128, so the effect is not confined to the submitted base or head dimension.

*Diverse architectures.* The submission already reported a 432M scarce-channel MLA study over three seeds, where with only 16 rotary channels PPL@16K goes from 138.8±5.5 to 95.6±4.1 with all three seeds agreeing (Table 18), plus 129M/382M bidirectional 3D-RoPE video-DiT experiments (Table 14) and a 750M continuation whose 8K strict autoregressive exact goes from 0% to 77.5% (Table 12). A 1.485B from-scratch branch — same initialization, recipe, data-order prefix and evaluation rows as the released geometric checkpoint — adds a pre-specified same-recipe trajectory, with the EVQ−Geo NLL delta moving from +0.0381 at 4K to −0.0437 at 8K and −0.1351 at 16K and paired document bootstrap intervals excluding zero at every length.

**4. Attribution.** Each confound in the surrogate → Cosh → operating-rule chain now has its own control: fixed zero-parameter analytic schedules under an unchanged operator separate allocation shape from learned capacity and tuning effort, and the exact-range factorial separates shape from base and range. On the τ rule, the derivation supplies the scaling form τ ∝ d_eff / √L_train but not the proportionality constant, which is a functional of the data dependency structure, the model's feature usage and the span convention. Across 21 configurations in two independent studies — a 99-run reanalysis and the exact-range factorial — the empirically selected τ never leaves a 0.75×–1.5× window around the formula value, while the best point inside that window moves with the configuration, which is the predicted behaviour. The revision will present c = 1 as a zero-search operating point.

**What we ask.** The remaining boundaries — target-aware retargeting favours the range method, and the mature-model results are task-family-adapted and mostly single-seed — bound the scope of the claim. We do not think they bear on whether the training-time exponent allocation is a distinct, independently identifiable design variable with effects that persist across base, head dimension, architecture and scale.
