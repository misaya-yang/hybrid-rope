**1. Technical distinction.** We should have cited Oka et al. (FMRoPE) and the revision will. We do not claim novelty for the observation that some RoPE channels are ineffective; §2 of the submission already credits it to prior work, citing Barbero et al. (2025) and Resonance RoPE.

The distinction is read off the frequency-table formula. In ω_i = b^(-u_i) with u_i = 2i/d, a method can parameterize the realized vector, the base, or the exponent. FMRoPE selects the scalar base b → b(T) for a declared context, preserving the normalized geometric order. EVQ-Cosh treats the fixed-base exponent map u_i → φ_τ(u_i) as the unknown and derives it from a stated variational objective, before the model learns anything. Most prior RoPE extrapolation work acts on the realized spectrum or the base; our starting question was whether the exponents should be spaced uniformly at all. The levels differ in what is parameterized and at what stage, not in what is numerically achievable.

**2. Direct controlled comparison.** With both sampled extrema, the log span, the initialization, token order, optimizer, budget and all 32 evaluation anchors held identical, and only the 30 interior frequencies changed, the endpoint-normalized Cosh interior allocation improves fixed-range OOD NLL by 0.478/0.205/0.113 at 512/1K/2K, winning 32/32, 27/32 and 22/32 anchors. A three-seed factorial reproduces the direction under the same constraint across bases 500K/1M, training lengths 256/1024 and head dimensions 32/64/128, the pre-specified 1.25× Cosh and deformation-matched exponential arms favoring non-geometric allocation in 10/12 and 9/12 configurations. Since every quantity a scalar base can set is held fixed, the effect is not attributable to base or range.

We implement the published §6.1 rule directly: θ_train = L_train, θ_infer = L_target, ω_i(θ) = θ^(-2i/d). Under target retargeting the ordering reverses (+0.061/+0.182/+0.279) and that rule obtains lower NLL, which is what a target-aware range method is for. The requested comparison is positive where it isolates the claimed variable.

On the YaRN claim the metareview names, submitted Table 3 applies the same fixed s = 8 transform to both substrates over three seeds: Geo+YaRN versus EVQ+YaRN obtains 61±3% versus 100±0% teacher-forced NLL-gap retrieval at 8K and PPL 82.9 versus 70.9. The supported claim is substrate dependence: the training-time table changes what the same inference-time transform can recover.

**3. Stronger evaluation.** The metareview names five gaps; the response addresses each.

*Larger models, with matched controls.* The submitted version showed the effect exists at 8B: LLaMA-3 LoRA PPL goes from 176.3 to 21.5 at 16K and 1942.5 to 104.3 at 32K, unusable to usable at 2× and 4×. We then tested whether a mature model can use that headroom in free-running generation. On OLMo-2 (1.485B), matched Native and EVQ arms received an identical three-stage downstream chain, every backward pass capped at 4,096 physical tokens. Judged by literal equality of the complete answer string plus a terminal EOS token, from raw token IDs, Native/EVQ is 95/100 versus 100/100 at 4K, 18/100 versus 98/100 at 8K, and 0/100 versus 60/100 at 16K. In every stage the two arms share the base model, LoRA rank, data, seed, optimizer, budget and evaluation rows; the single difference is the fixed frequency table. This is a retrofit setting: both arms start from the same released instruct checkpoint and take the same adaptation, with only the EVQ arm's frequency table replaced, so the effect does not require pretraining from scratch.

*Stronger benchmarks.* On OLMo-2, under identical physical-4K supervision over all 13 RULER families, Native/EVQ official macro is 82.16%/37.51% at 4K, 0.08%/21.29% at 8K, and 0%/6.13% at 16K; we return to the in-window gap below. On LLaMA-3-8B, under identical physical-8K supervision, 16K official macro goes from 0.295% to 14.03%. A separate matched 8B adaptation adds the probability side: Native/EVQ NLL is 1.919/2.309 at 8K, 4.691/3.181 at 16K and 6.899/4.851 at 32K, the 16K and 32K direction holding in all 24 evaluation packs and three domains. At true 16K, deleting the remote gold block worsens EVQ NLL by 1.506 while leaving Native unchanged, and median target-block hit@16 rises from 18.75% to 64.06% — the model is using the distant content, not reconstructing it locally.

*Real downstream tasks.* Our initial recipe adapted Q, K, V and O. Since RoPE acts directly on Q and K, we tested whether restricting the continuation to those tensors better localizes the adaptation: from the matched parents we froze every inherited V/O tensor — verified bitwise — and continued only Q/K at physical 4K. On 200 held-out 2WikiMultiHopQA prompts, exact match is 22.0% versus 21.5% at 4K, 0% versus 17.5% at 8K, and 0% versus 4.0% at 16K. The unadapted parents score 0% at every length, so the continuation recovers nearly identical 4K accuracy on the two substrates while only EVQ carries that capability beyond the training length. Under the same restriction the 13-family RULER continuation gives 72.19%/42.44% at 4K and 2.02%/31.63% at 8K: the EVQ arm improves at both and the in-window gap narrows from 44.7 to 29.8 points, so adaptation scope affects that trade-off materially without accounting for all of it. Two of the 13 RULER families are also built from real-document QA data (SQuAD v2, HotpotQA distractor); at 16K Native-LoRA is zero on all 13 tasks while EVQ-LoRA has normalized-exact successes including HotpotQA.

*Base frequencies.* A three-seed suite at base 1M with d_head = 128, held out from the calibration of the submitted rule, gives mean tail NLL deltas of −0.802/−0.664/−0.433/−0.287/−0.212 at 1K/2K/4K/8K/16K, all three seeds agreeing and paired 95% intervals excluding zero. The exact-range factorial additionally crosses bases 500K/1M and head dimensions 32/64/128, so the effect is not confined to the submitted base or head dimension.

*Diverse architectures.* The submission already reported a 432M scarce-channel MLA study over three seeds, where with only 16 rotary channels PPL@16K goes from 138.8±5.5 to 95.6±4.1, all three seeds agreeing (Table 18), plus 129M/382M bidirectional 3D-RoPE video-DiT experiments (Table 14) and a 750M continuation whose 8K strict autoregressive exact goes from 0% to 77.5% (Table 12). A pre-specified 1.485B from-scratch branch — same initialization, recipe, data-order prefix and evaluation rows as the released geometric checkpoint — moves the EVQ−Geo NLL delta from +0.0381 at 4K to −0.0437 at 8K and −0.1351 at 16K, paired document bootstrap intervals excluding zero at every length.

**4. Attribution.** The matched analytic-schedule ablation the metareview names is run. With the positional operator, protocol and evaluation fixed and every arm a zero-parameter analytic schedule — so parameter count and tuning budget are identical by construction — EVQ-Cosh minus geometric mean tail NLL is −0.256/−0.305/−0.223/−0.238 at 1K/2K/4K/8K across three seeds, all agreeing, with span-matched uniform and deformation-matched power and exponential arms included. That separates allocation shape from capacity and tuning effort; the exact-range factorial separates it from base and range. On the τ rule, the derivation supplies the scaling form τ ∝ d_eff / √L_train but not the constant, which is a functional of the data, feature usage and span convention. Across 21 configurations in two studies the empirically selected τ never leaves a 0.75×–1.5× window around the formula value, while the best point inside that window moves with the configuration — consistent with the predicted behaviour. The revision will present c = 1 as a zero-search operating point.

**What we ask.** The three conditions are met with direct evidence, in the order the metareview sets them.

Two boundaries genuinely constrain the claim and we state them plainly. Under target retargeting the FMRoPE rule obtains lower NLL, which limits us to a fixed-range allocation advantage rather than universal superiority. And EVQ pays an in-window cost in several settings in exchange for its long-range gain, consistent with reallocating a finite channel budget toward longer distances. On sampling: each matched adaptation arm uses one training seed, with a second independently trained EVQ seed reproducing the OLMo strict-retrieval result; the fixed-schedule ladder, the exact-range factorial and the base/head-dimension controls each use three seeds, and the 8B direction holds in all 24 evaluation packs and all three domains.

Neither boundary bears on the question the metareview poses: whether the training-time exponent allocation is a distinct, independently identifiable design variable whose effect persists across base, head dimension, architecture and scale. On the evidence above we believe it is.
