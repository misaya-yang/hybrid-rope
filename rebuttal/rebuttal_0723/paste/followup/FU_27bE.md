Thank you again. One part of your question has its answer in the other threads, and since it is the part about production-scale persistence we bring it here rather than leave it split.

Your question asks whether the benefit persists at the bases, head dimensions and model scales associated with production long-context systems. Our response answered the base and head-dimension half — the held-out base-1M/d_head=128 suite and the exact-range factorial across 500K/1M and 32/64/128 — and the scale half with the 1.485B pre-specified run. The downstream and 8B evidence responded to other reviews, so it is not in this thread. It bears directly on your question.

**8B, matched arms.** On LLaMA-3-8B under identical physical-8K supervision over all 13 RULER families, 16K official macro goes from **0.295% to 14.03%**, where the Native-LoRA arm is at essentially zero. The submitted version already reported extrapolated PPL going from **176.3 to 21.5** at 16K and 1942.5 to 104.3 at 32K. A separate matched 8B adaptation adds the probability side: Native/EVQ NLL is 1.919/2.309 at 8K, **4.691/3.181** at 16K and 6.899/4.851 at 32K, with the 16K and 32K direction holding in all 24 evaluation packs and all three domains.

That last result speaks to a mechanism question your framing invites: whether the model is using distant content or reconstructing it locally. At true 16K, deleting the remote gold block from every head worsens EVQ NLL by **1.506** while leaving the matched Native control essentially unchanged, and median target-block hit@16 rises from **18.75% to 64.06%**.

**1.485B, free-running generation.** On OLMo-2 1.485B, matched Native and EVQ arms took an identical downstream chain with every backward pass capped at 4,096 physical tokens. Judged by literal equality of the complete answer string plus a terminal EOS token, read from raw token IDs, Native/EVQ is 95/100 versus 100/100 at 4K, **18/100 versus 98/100** at 8K, and **0/100 versus 60/100** at 16K. The two arms share the base model, LoRA rank, data, seed, optimizer, budget and evaluation rows; the single controlled intervention is the fixed frequency table.

**In-window, reported rather than avoided.** Over 13 RULER families the same comparison is 82.16%/37.51% at 4K, improving to 72.19%/42.44% when adaptation is restricted to the tensors RoPE acts on. On 200 held-out 2WikiMultiHopQA prompts under that restriction the arms are level in-window — exact match **22.0%/21.5%** at 4K — and separate beyond it, 0%/17.5% at 8K and 0%/4.0% at 16K, from unadapted parents at 0% everywhere. Training from scratch, where no table swap occurs, the same allocation costs **+0.0381 NLL** at 4K against −0.0437 at 8K and −0.1351 at 16K.

On sampling, since it bears on your point about supplementary tiers: the fixed-schedule ladder, the exact-range factorial and the base/head-dimension controls each use three agreeing seeds. The matched adaptation arms use one training seed each, with a second independently trained EVQ seed reproducing the OLMo strict-retrieval result (69/100 and 67/100 at 8K against Native 0/100). We label them at those tiers rather than merging them.
