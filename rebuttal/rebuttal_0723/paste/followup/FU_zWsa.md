**1. Novelty of EVQ-Cosh over FMRoPE.** FMRoPE selects the base — §6.1 sets \\(\\theta = L_{\\rm train}\\) at training and \\(\\theta = L_{\\rm target}\\) at inference, and the abstract describes the effect as shifting the band toward lower frequencies, with the exponents uniform in i and the normalized geometric order preserved. EVQ-Cosh holds the base fixed and replaces the exponent map \\(u_i \\to \\varphi_\\tau(u_i)\\), the closed-form inverse CDF of the stationary density of a stated variational objective. Moving a band and redistributing channels inside one are different operations. On the dead-channel observation: we do not claim it — §2 credits it to Barbero et al. (2025), and Oka et al. credit it to the same source, their §3 opening by investigating "the frequency band identified by Barbero et al. (2025)".

**2. A direct matched comparison with FMRoPE.** Run. Holding both sampled extrema, the log span, initialization, token order, optimizer, budget and all 32 anchors fixed, and changing only the 30 interior frequencies, OOD NLL improves by **0.478/0.205/0.113** at 512/1K/2K, winning **32/32, 27/32 and 22/32** anchors; a three-seed factorial reproduces the direction across bases 500K/1M, training lengths 256/1024 and head dimensions 32/64/128. We implement the published §6.1 rule directly. We also report where it loses: under target retargeting the FMRoPE rule obtains lower NLL (+0.061/+0.182/+0.279), which is what a target-aware range method is for, and the two compose — applying the target-range rule on top of an EVQ grid improves that model by **0.098/0.529/0.638**.

**3. RULER.** Included, and it improves. On LLaMA-3-8B under identical physical-8K supervision over all 13 families, 16K official macro goes from **0.295% to 14.03%**. Two of the 13 families are built from real-document QA data (SQuAD v2, HotpotQA distractor); at 16K the Native-LoRA arm's normalized exact is zero on all 13 tasks while EVQ-LoRA succeeds on several including HotpotQA. We report the metric disagreement at the training length rather than selecting: at 8K Native leads official macro (94.44% versus 77.60%), which credits partial and substring matches, while EVQ leads normalized exact (21.54% versus 17.69%). We also report the in-window cost on OLMo-2 (82.16%/37.51% at 4K, improving to 72.19%/42.44% when adaptation is restricted to Q and K).
