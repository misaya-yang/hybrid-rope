Each of our responses addressed one review, so three points that cut across the panel appear in only one thread. Since the metareview turns on those points, we consolidate them here. Nothing below is new evidence; it is material already posted in an adjacent thread.

**1. The distinction from FMRoPE, and the control built to test it.**

Every method in this literature modifies the frequency table \\(\\omega_i = b^{-u_i}\\); what differs is which quantity is the design variable. FMRoPE selects the base: its §6.1 sets \\(\\theta = L_{\\rm train}\\) at training and \\(\\theta = L_{\\rm target}\\) at inference, and its abstract describes the effect as shifting the band toward lower frequencies. Under that rule the exponents remain uniform in i and the normalized geometric order is preserved exactly. EVQ-Cosh holds the base fixed and replaces the exponent map \\(u_i \\to \\varphi_\\tau(u_i)\\), the closed-form inverse CDF of the stationary density of a stated variational objective. Moving a band and redistributing channels inside one are different operations.

If the effect reduced to where the band sits, pinning the band would remove it. We pinned both sampled extrema and the log span — the quantities a base or range choice sets — together with initialization, token order, optimizer, budget and all 32 anchors, and varied only the 30 interior frequencies. OOD NLL improves by **0.478/0.205/0.113** at 512/1K/2K, winning **32/32, 27/32 and 22/32** anchors; a three-seed factorial reproduces the direction across bases 500K/1M and head dimensions 32/64/128.

We report the boundary in the same breath: when both grids are retargeted to the declared length the ordering reverses (**+0.061/+0.182/+0.279**), which is what a target-aware range method is for. The two levels compose rather than compete — applying the target-range rule on top of an EVQ grid improves that model by **0.098/0.529/0.638** NLL. We should have cited Oka et al.; the revision will, with a table contrasting the levels. We do not claim the dead-channel observation: §2 credits it to Barbero et al. (2025), the source Oka et al. also cite.

**2. The four levels between the theorem and the operating rule.**

Two reviews asked for this separation and the metareview names the same gap, so we state it once here. (i) Cosh is the stationary density of C_app — exact, conditional on that surrogate. (ii) The quadratic surrogate, the discrete grid and the pure-tether branch are modelling choices, checked empirically rather than proved: allocations derived from the surrogate reduce the **exact-kernel** collision score by **24–92% across 12 configurations**, and a collision-only oracle search on the exact kernel reaches comparable reductions. Its coefficients are fitted from that exact kernel on the deployed grid, with no NLL, PPL or task score entering the fit. (iii) Trained-model results test the predicted direction; they do not validate the surrogate. (iv) The analysis yields the scaling form \\(\\tau \\propto d_{\\rm eff}/\\sqrt{L_{\\rm train}}\\) but not the constant, which is set to one as a zero-search convention.

**3. The matched analytic-schedule ablation, including what it does not show.**

The metareview names this as particularly useful evidence, so we give it in full. Operator, protocol and evaluation are fixed and every arm is a zero-parameter analytic schedule, so parameter count and tuning budget are identical by construction rather than by tuning. EVQ-Cosh minus Geo mean tail NLL is **−0.256/−0.305/−0.223/−0.238** at 1K/2K/4K/8K across three agreeing seeds, with span-matched uniform and deformation-matched arms included.

Within that ladder, a deformation-matched exponential is statistically indistinguishable from Cosh (+0.0007 NLL, exact sign-flip p = 0.836). What survives is the allocation axis rather than Cosh specifically: pre-specified 1.25× Cosh and the matched exponential both improve weighted OOD NLL over Geo, by **0.0121 and 0.0106**, favouring non-geometric allocation in **10/12 and 9/12** configurations. We therefore claim that allocation is a separately identifiable design axis and that Cosh is the closed-form operating point derived from the surrogate — not that Cosh is a universal optimum, and §6 will say so.

On the other two sensitivities the metareview names: across 21 configurations in two studies the selected τ never leaves a **0.75×–1.5×** window around the rule value; and on a held-out base of 1M with d_head = 128, applied untuned, tail-NLL deltas are **−0.802/−0.433/−0.212** at 1K/4K/16K with all three seeds agreeing, against a **+0.069** in-domain cost at 512.
