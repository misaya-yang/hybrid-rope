Your question asks whether the benefit persists across the bases, head dimensions and model scales associated with production long-context systems. Our response answered the base and head-dimension part, and the scale part with the 1.485B pre-specified run. The 8B and downstream evidence was written in reply to other reviews and so is not in this thread, but it bears directly on what you asked, and we would rather bring it here than leave the answer split.

**8B, matched arms.** On LLaMA-3-8B under identical physical-8K supervision over all 13 RULER families, 16K official macro goes from **0.295% to 14.03%**. The submitted version already reported extrapolated PPL going from **176.3 to 21.5** at 16K and 1942.5 to 104.3 at 32K. A separate matched 8B adaptation gives NLL of 1.919/2.309 at 8K, **4.691/3.181** at 16K and 6.899/4.851 at 32K, with the 16K and 32K direction holding in all 24 evaluation packs and all three domains.

That last suite also addresses whether the model uses distant content or reconstructs it locally. At true 16K, deleting the remote gold block from every head worsens EVQ NLL by **1.506** while leaving the matched Native control essentially unchanged, and median target-block hit@16 rises from **18.75% to 64.06%**.

**1.485B, free-running generation.** Matched Native and EVQ arms took an identical downstream chain with every backward pass capped at 4,096 physical tokens. By literal equality of the complete answer string plus a terminal EOS token, read from raw token IDs, Native/EVQ is 95/100 versus 100/100 at 4K, **18/100 versus 98/100** at 8K, and **0/100 versus 60/100** at 16K. The two arms share the base model, LoRA rank, data, seed, optimizer, budget and evaluation rows; the single controlled intervention is the fixed frequency table.

**In-window, on the same models.** Over 13 RULER families the same comparison is **82.16%/37.51%** at 4K, improving to 72.19%/42.44% when adaptation is restricted to the tensors RoPE acts on. On 200 held-out 2WikiMultiHopQA prompts under that restriction the arms are level in-window — exact match **22.0%/21.5%** at 4K — and separate beyond it, 0%/17.5% at 8K and 0%/4.0% at 16K, from unadapted parents at 0% everywhere.

**On evidence tiers, since your review raises them.** Three agreeing seeds: the fixed-schedule ladder, the exact-range factorial, the base and head-dimension controls, the 432M MLA study. One training seed per matched arm: the 1.485B and 8B adaptations, with a second independently trained EVQ seed reproducing the OLMo strict-retrieval result (**69/100 and 67/100** at 8K against Native 0/100). We label them at those tiers rather than merging them.
