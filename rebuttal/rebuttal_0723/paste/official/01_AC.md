The metareview names four kinds of evidence as particularly useful. Four results in the submitted version speak to them, and our responses did not point to any of them. They are now in the reviewer threads; the numbers are here.

**Sensitivity to the allocation parameter — Table 6, p. 18.** The predicted prefactor \\(c_{\\rm pred}=\\sqrt{45\\,Q_1(L,b)}\\) is compared against \\(c_{\\rm coll}\\), the exact-kernel collision-score minimizer, over nine configurations crossing \\(d_{\\rm head}\\in\\{32,64,128\\}\\) and \\(L\\in\\{256,512,1024\\}\\). Ratio **0.981 ± 0.003**, CV **0.28%**, leave-one-out maximum error **0.008**. No trained model enters this check.

**Base frequency — Table 17, p. 32.** A trained base sweep from **100 to 50,000**, over which the dead-channel count rises from 0/16 to about 9/16. EVQ is stable across base ≥ 500. Video-DiT, denoising MSE, one to two seeds.

**YaRN complementarity — Table 19, p. 33.** Matched-scale YaRN improves PPL by **−3.1%** on the geometric substrate against **−40.7%** on EVQ at 8K, τ = 4.0. The same table records that NTK-aware scaling is *not* complementary at large τ: at 32× it is 198.1 for Geo against 331.4 for EVQ.

**Real downstream tasks — Table 21, p. 35.** QuALITY, **2,086 real-document questions**, distractor-padded. Four-option accuracy sits near the 25% random baseline for every arm — capacity, not positional encoding, binds at 454M. Gold-answer NLL separates and widens with the extrapolation ratio: **−1.7%** at 4K, **−30.1%** at 2×, **−21.4%** at 4×. Single seed.
