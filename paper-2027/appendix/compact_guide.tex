\section*{Supplementary Material}
The supplement follows the main argument. Appendix~\ref{sec:compact-geometry}
proves the positional statements; Appendix~\ref{sec:compact-constructions}
derives the allocations; Appendix~\ref{sec:identification-details} specifies
the controlled interventions. Complete frozen, native-window and learned
results are in Appendices~\ref{sec:compact-frozen},~\ref{sec:compact-native}
and~\ref{sec:compact-learning}, respectively. The source archive includes
numerical inputs, construction/scoring code and CPU verification scripts.

Fixed-support interventions isolate placement; matching total displacement
tests residual shape, and preserving the spectrum tests coordinate assignment.
Frozen comparisons retain the weights, while paired learning studies match
their training or adaptation conditions.
