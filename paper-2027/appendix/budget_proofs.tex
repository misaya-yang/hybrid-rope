\section{Finite-Budget Bounds: Conditions and Proofs}
\label{sec:budget-proofs}

\paragraph{T1: a finite slow-pair bound.}
Let $G_0$ be the Gram matrix of $[1,t]$ in $L^2(\mu)$,
$\sigma_0=\sqrt{\lambda_{\min}(G_0)}>0$, and
$h_\mu^2=\mathbb E_\mu[t^4/4+t^6/36]$.
If $0<x\le\varepsilon$ and $\varepsilon^2h_\mu<\sigma_0$, then
\begin{equation}
e(x)\le\frac{x^4h_\mu^2}{(\sigma_0-\varepsilon^2h_\mu)^2}.
\end{equation}
Indeed, $|\cos(xt)-1|\le x^2t^2/2$ and
$|\sin(xt)/x-t|\le x^2t^3/6$ give
$\|v_x-v_0\|_{\mathrm{HS}}\le x^2h_\mu$.
Singular-value perturbation gives
$\sigma_{\min}(v_x)\ge\sigma_0-x^2h_\mu$.
Since $(I-P_0)v_0=0$, orthonormalization bounds the residual by
$x^2h_\mu/(\sigma_0-x^2h_\mu)$. Squaring and bounding the denominator
proves the result. Exact weighted-QR residuals can be substantially sharper.

\paragraph{T2 and T3.}
The rank approximation in Proposition~\ref{prop:finite-budget} has residual
squared norm $\eta$. The best rank-$r_0$ approximation cannot be worse, and the
squared singular values of $A_\Omega$ are the eigenvalues of its Gram matrix.
Moreover $(I-P_U)A_\Omega$ has operator norm at most $\sqrt\eta$.
For any bounded $c$, project $b-A_\Omega c$ onto $U^\perp$ and apply the
reverse triangle inequality. This yields the positive-part lower bound.
For a declared threshold $\zeta>0$, at most $r_0+\eta/\zeta$ eigenvalues can
exceed $\zeta$; this diagnostic depends on the chosen tolerance and is not
model accuracy.

\paragraph{T4: a nonlocal softmax bound.}
Assume $\mu$ is a probability measure with positive support. Center $b$ and
$f$ under $\mu$, with both bounded by $M$ in absolute value. Along
$g_s=(1-s)b+sf$, the tilted probability satisfies $p_{g_s}\ge e^{-2M}\mu$.
For $\delta=f-b$,
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{Var}_{p_{g_s}}(\delta)
=\inf_c\mathbb E_{p_{g_s}}(\delta-c)^2
\ge e^{-2M}\inf_c\mathbb E_\mu(\delta-c)^2
=e^{-2M}\|\delta\|_\mu^2.
\end{equation}
The Hessian of log-partition is this variance. Integrating its quadratic form
along the segment with weight $1-s$ gives
$D_{\rm KL}(p_b\Vert p_f)\ge e^{-2M}\|b-f\|_\mu^2/2$.
Because $U$ includes constants, centering leaves the projected residual
unchanged. If the uncentered model response has coefficient norm at most $B$,
T3 consequently gives
\begin{equation}
D_{\rm KL}(p_b\Vert p_f)\ge\frac{e^{-2M}}2
(\|(I-P_U)b\|_\mu-B\sqrt\eta)_+^2.
\end{equation}
This is a bound for a specified controlled read. Independent values and a query
that cannot access them are needed for a value-reading interpretation; it is
not a lower bound on a Transformer language-model loss.
