The supplementary material follows the paper's argument, expanding each connection between geometry, learning, and practical allocation. Table~\ref{tab:argument-guide} provides entry points to the complete results and proofs.

Question & Supporting material \\
\midrule
What does the allocation supply? & Full-pair geometry, finite-window calculations, and static ordering examples (Apps.~\ref{sec:geometry-proofs}--\ref{sec:static-counterexamples}). \\
What is learned from it? & Paired training and range retargeting (App.~\ref{sec:exact-range-control}); crossed weights/tables (App.~\ref{sec:crossing-151m}, Fig.~\ref{fig:weight-table-crossing}); assignment and kernel equivalence (App.~\ref{sec:obstruction-proof}). \\
How can allocations be constructed? & Cosh derivation and finite quantization (App.~\ref{sec:proofs}); multi-shape factorial (App.~\ref{sec:exact-range-factorial}); learned-frequency comparator (App.~\ref{sec:pe-dominant}). \\
What happens during learning? & Training protocols and $750$M continuation (App.~\ref{sec:experiment-details}); full MLA curves (App.~\ref{sec:mla-results}); $1.485$B, selective-Q/K, and $8$B adaptation (App.~\ref{sec:mature-details}); video DiT (App.~\ref{sec:video-dit}). \\
How does a mature model respond? & Fixed-support controls (App.~\ref{sec:frozen-fixed-support}); static profiles, placement, BM, and natural QA (App.~\ref{sec:adjustment-details}); measured profile diagnostics (App.~\ref{sec:profile-diagnostic-audit}). \\
