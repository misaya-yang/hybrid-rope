\section{Supporting Results}

\subsection{Multi-head Latent Attention}
\label{sec:mla-results}

We test the finite-budget hypothesis in a 432M-parameter MLA transformer with
24 layers, width 1024, 16 heads, and per-head width 64. Its decoupled rotary
subspace has $d_{\mathrm{rope}}{=}32$ ($K{=}16$ frequencies); the remaining
$d_{\mathrm{nope}}{=}32$ dimensions are non-rotary, and the shared KV latent
rank is 256. The model trains from scratch on 500M FineWeb-Edu tokens at
$L_{\mathrm{train}}{=}8192$ and base $500\mathrm{K}$ over seeds 42, 43, and
88. \evq{} uses the pre-specified $\tau{=}1.414$ setting. This experiment tests
frequency allocation at fixed architecture.

\evq{} reduces 16K PPL by $31.1\%$ ($138.8\to95.6$), while 8K PPL changes by
$+1.1\%$. All three training seeds favour \evq{} at 16K. The \evq{}+MLA-blend
row has the lowest mean PPL at 16K and beyond. These rows establish the tested
ordering for this architecture and protocol.
