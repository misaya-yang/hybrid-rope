\section{Supporting Results}

\subsection{Multi-head Latent Attention}
\label{sec:mla-results}

We report allocation at one fixed rotary budget in a 432M-parameter transformer following
the MLA architectural pattern \citep{deepseekv2}, with 24 layers, width 1024,
16 heads, and per-head width 64.
Its decoupled rotary
subspace has $d_{\mathrm{rope}}{=}32$ ($K{=}16$ frequencies); the remaining
$d_{\mathrm{nope}}{=}32$ dimensions are non-rotary, and the shared KV latent
rank is 256. The model trains from scratch on 500M FineWeb-Edu tokens
\citep{penedo2024fineweb} at
$L_{\mathrm{train}}{=}8192$ and base $500\mathrm{K}$ over seeds 42, 43, and
88. \evq{} uses the pre-specified $\tau=1.414$ setting.
At $16$K, Geo PPL is
$141.1/132.5/142.8$ for seeds $42/43/88$, whereas \evq{} is
$93.7/92.7/100.3$; all three paired directions agree.

\paragraph{Evaluation corpus and aggregation.}
The archived evaluator reads the shared cached FineWeb-Edu stream \texttt{val\_fineweb-edu\_5000000.pt}, nominally five million GPT-2 tokens. For each method and seed it resets NumPy RandomState to $9999$, iterates through lengths $8192$, $16384$, $20480$, $24576$, $28672$, and $32768$, and draws eight distinct start offsets per length. Every chunk scores all $L-1$ next-token targets, with full-sequence cross-entropy and no tail restriction. Its per-model PPL is the exponential of the mean chunk NLL; the table then takes the arithmetic mean and sample standard deviation of the three per-seed PPLs. Thus the requested targets per model are $8(L-1)$ at each length; starts are sampled without replacement, but overlapping windows are possible. The same offsets are reused across methods.

All methods use the same fixed FineWeb-Edu evaluation cache and paired chunk offsets. The archived result JSON retains every seed and length, allowing the reported means, seed variation and scaling comparisons to be recomputed.

For the supporting overlay, let $\lambda_k=2\pi/\omega_k$ and
$t_k=\operatorname{clip}((\lambda_k-L_{\mathrm{train}})/(31L_{\mathrm{train}}),0,1)$.
The MLA wavelength-blend operator sets
$\omega'_k=\omega_k/[1+t_k(s-1)]$: it preserves wavelengths below the training
length, applies the full factor $s$ above $32L_{\mathrm{train}}$, and interpolates
between them.

\subsection{Learning and natural-QA summaries}
\label{sec:learning-summary}
The $750$M continuation starts from a common $2$K Geo checkpoint and uses
$500$M tokens at $4$K, including $3\%$ passkey and $10\%$ downstream mix.
Its $8$K task endpoint is greedy answer-token exact, not complete-response exact.
The OLMo adaptation pair trains on physical inputs at most $4$K with explicit
target-range positions; evaluation uses real contiguous prompts. The Q/K-only
RULER adaptation has Cosh--Native macro differences $-29.75/+29.61/+4.65$
points at $4/8/16$K. These are learned protocols, not static-installation evidence.
