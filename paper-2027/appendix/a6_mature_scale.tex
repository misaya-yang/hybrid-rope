\subsection{OLMo-2 $1.485$B}

\paragraph{Matched routing conversion.}
We use OLMo-2-0425-1B-Instruct \citep{olmo2furious} ($1.485$B actual
parameters) with rank-$64$, alpha-$128$ Q/K/V/O LoRA \citep{hu2022lora}.
Native and \evq{} share the checkpoint, training rows and order, token budget,
optimizer, and evaluation rows; the fixed frequency substrate is the intended
intervention. Every backward pass contains at most $4$K physical tokens, but
explicit position IDs expose target-range relative phases. This is therefore a
physical-memory cap, not phase-free $4$K training. On $8$K
\texttt{niah\_single\_1}, strict first-generated-number exact is $0/100$
for Native and $69/100$ for the matched \evq{} seed; an independently trained
\evq{} seed scores $67/100$ on the same rows. The two \evq{} results are
training-seed replications on a shared evaluation set. Natural-text NLL and the distinct
autoregressive endpoint are reported separately in Table~\ref{tab:mature}.

\paragraph{Selective Q/K phase adaptation.}
To test a less intrusive mature-checkpoint adaptation, we start independently
from the matched Native and \evq{} Stage-A parents, freeze every inherited V/O
LoRA tensor (verified bitwise), and continue only the existing Q/K tensors for
$300$ steps. Both arms use the same $1{:}1{:}2$ mixture of continuous-$4$K,
target-$8$K, and target-$16$K phase exposure, complete answer-plus-EOS
supervision, and physical sequences no longer than $4$K; long phases again use
explicit position IDs. On $200$ held-out 2WikiMultiHopQA rows per length,
Native/\evq{} exact match is $22.0/21.5\%$ at $4$K, $0/17.5\%$ at $8$K, and
$0/4.0\%$ at $16$K. Prompts retain the official question and context and are
deterministically filled with answer-filtered 2Wiki distractors to reach the
target length. Thus this is held-out task-family length transfer, not an
unmodified LongBench leaderboard result. The RULER adapters are independent of
the QA adapters: Table~\ref{tab:ruler} reports both the earlier $276$-step
full-Q/K/V/O continuation and the $300$-step selective-Q/K continuation, each
trained over all $13$ families.

\paragraph{Family-level in-window structure.}
For the selective-Q/K RULER adapters at $4$K, the official-score changes
(\evq{} minus Native) for
\texttt{niah\_single\_1/2/3} are $-5/0/-60$ percentage points, averaging
$-21.67$.  The changes for
\texttt{niah\_multikey\_1/2/3}, \texttt{niah\_multivalue}, and
\texttt{niah\_multiquery} are $-65/-95/-50/-26.25/-25$, averaging $-52.25$.
This split is a post-hoc mechanism diagnostic over one matched seed, not a
separate benchmark claim; it motivates the multi-reference prediction in
\S\ref{sec:discussion}.

\paragraph{From initialisation.}
Starting from the public step-$0$ initialisation, one $2.097$B-token \evq{}
trajectory reaches PPL $167.45/156.87/159.64$ against the released Geo
step-$1{,}000$ checkpoint's $161.19/163.88/182.73$ at $4$/$8$/$16$K.
The paired \evq{}-minus-Geo NLL is $+0.0381/-0.0437/-0.1351$, with
$122/128$ and $126/128$ held-out documents favouring \evq{} at $8$K and
$16$K. This is one same-initialisation, same-scientific-recipe trajectory:
\evq{} used a reviewed single-GPU HF loop, whereas the released Geo checkpoint
used the original distributed trainer. This row evaluates natural-text LM;
capability is evaluated by the separate autoregressive protocols above.

\subsection{LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct}

\paragraph{Probability and causal source use.}
In the matched seed-$42$, $300$-step Q/K/V/O LoRA protocol, \evq{}-minus-Native
NLL is $+0.390/-1.510/-2.048$ at $8$/$16$/$32$K. On ten frozen true-$16$K
passkey cases, median target-block hit@16 over $32$ retrieval heads rises from
$18.75\%$ to $64.06\%$. Removing the remote gold block from every head changes
NLL by $-0.0095$ for Native and $+1.5055$ for \evq{}. These are matched
teacher-forced probability and causal-routing measurements; autoregressive
task accuracy is evaluated separately.

\paragraph{RULER-family continuation.}
A separate $516$-step continuation uses identical physical-$8$K supervision
over all $13$ RULER families, with one seed per arm and disjoint generated
training/evaluation rows. At $16$K, Native/\evq{} official macro is
$0.295/14.03\%$ and normalized exact is $0/1.54\%$. At the $8$K training
length the metrics disagree, so Table~\ref{tab:ruler} reports both official
macro ($94.44/77.60\%$) and normalized exact ($17.69/21.54\%$).

\paragraph{Endpoint scope.}
NLL, attention routing, strict autoregressive exact match, multi-hop QA, and
RULER remain separate endpoints. The 2Wiki and RULER continuations use the
same task families at train and test with disjoint rows, so they establish
task-adapted length transfer, not unseen-task transfer. The natural-text and
routing rows make no task-transfer claim.
