\section{Native-Window Allocation: Construction and Complete Results}
\label{sec:compact-native}
\subsection{Reference objective and numerical construction}
\label{sec:ncp-construction-new}
NCP improves task performance within the native context by reallocating the
interior frequencies while fixing both sampled endpoints. Let
$\phi_k=(L-1)\omega_k$ and $\omega'_k=\omega_ke^{-u_k}$.
The reference problem is a binary target--distractor comparison with unit
amplitudes, aligned target phase and uniformly random distractor phase:
\begin{align}
r(\theta)&=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}
 \log\!\left(1+\exp(\cos\psi-\cos\theta)\right)d\psi,\\
\mathcal R(\phi)&=2\int_0^1(1-t)r(\phi t)dt.
\label{eq:ncp-reference-risk}
\end{align}
The triangular distance weight is the distribution of absolute separation
between two uniform positions on a continuous unit interval. The aligned
reference comparison supplies a public design objective. The Transformer task
scores below evaluate the resulting fixed construction.

We minimize Eq.~\eqref{eq:ncp-main} with fixed $\lambda=9/4$
(quadratic coefficient $\lambda/2=9/8$), subject to
\begin{equation}
u_0=u_{K-1}=0,\quad 0\le u_k\le 2/9,\qquad
\log\frac{\omega'_k}{\omega'_{k+1}}
 \ge\frac12\log\frac{\omega_k}{\omega_{k+1}}.
\label{eq:ncp-constraints}
\end{equation}
The endpoint constraints keep support fixed; the gap condition preserves
ordering and at least half each original adjacent log gap.
Writing $r(\theta)=a_0+\sum_{j\ge1}a_j\cos(j\theta)$ gives
\begin{equation}
\mathcal R(\phi)=a_0+\sum_{j\ge1}a_j
 \operatorname{sinc}^2(j\phi/2),\qquad
\operatorname{sinc}(x)=\frac{\sin x}{x},\quad\operatorname{sinc}(0)=1.
\label{eq:ncp-fourier}
\end{equation}
This follows by integrating each cosine against $2(1-t)$.
With $q(\phi)=d\mathcal R/d\log\phi$, an interior scalar solution satisfies
$\lambda u=q(\phi e^{-u})$. We compute $r$ on a $1024$-point angular grid,
retain $32$ Fourier modes and solve the scalar stationary equations by
bisection, using zero shift when the native gradient is nonpositive. The two
endpoint shifts are reset to zero. The implemented $32$-mode scalar objective is strictly convex. For
$f(x)=\operatorname{sinc}^2(x/2)$, its log-curvature is
$h(x)=2\cos x-6\sin x/x+8(1-\cos x)/x^2$ and $|h(x)|\le4$:
for $0\le x\le4$, the integral representation gives
$|h(x)|\le x/3+x^2/6$; for $x\ge4$, it gives the bound
$\sqrt{4+36/x^2}+16/x^2\le3.5$. The retained coefficients satisfy
$\sum_{j=1}^{32}|a_j|<0.554403$, so scalar curvature is at least
$9/4-4\sum_j|a_j|>0.03238$. Thus the stationary root, or the zero-shift
boundary when its derivative is nonnegative, is the unique box minimizer.
On the deployed OLMo grid the log-gap constraints are inactive, so these
scalar minimizers also solve the constrained finite-series problem.

The deployed grid has $64$ pairs, base $500000$ and native length $4096$.
It changes $46$ FP32 entries, reaches maximum relative slowdown $16.7164\%$
and retains a minimum log-gap ratio of $0.7681$. The maximum scalar stationarity
residual is below $10^{-11}$. The mean reference risk decreases from
$0.614082$ to $0.609641$; the penalized objective is $0.611853$.
These are reference-model quantities, separate from model task scores.
The table and unit gain are fixed across layers and inputs; construction reads
no checkpoint weights, activations or evaluation outputs. The source archive
includes the original constructor and an exact FP32 table reproduction check.

\subsection{Native-context task results and companion controls}
\label{sec:ncp-results-new}
The OLMo native $4$K development panel contains $60$ source-order inputs per
RULER task, $780$ total, without content padding. It is the same frozen panel
used for the companion allocation studies. NCP and original RoPE share the
checkpoint, unit gain, inputs, BF16 precision, greedy decoder, batch $1$ and
$4096$-token prefill chunks. Their scores are $70.7821/69.3739\%$;
the paired task-equal difference is $+1.4081$ points, interval
$[0.2009,2.5833]$. NCP has higher per-input scores on $62$ inputs,
original RoPE on $31$, with $687$ ties. Every task remains in the aggregate.

\subsection{Language modeling and benefit from earlier context}
\label{sec:ncp-context-new}
We evaluate $128$ nonoverlapping windows from $103$ documents in the
PG-19 and Proof-Pile test corpora. Each $4097$-token window
defines $4096$ next-token inputs. We score only its final $256$ target tokens,
once with full history and once with $512$ preceding tokens plus the same
teacher-forced targets. Shared tokens retain identical relative positions;
the checkpoint, targets and score positions are paired. Loss is averaged
within document and then equally over documents. Paired document intervals
use $20000$ draws, seed $20260915$.

by $0.004845$. Its additional benefit from earlier context is $0.007941$
$[0.005529,0.010346]$ nat/token. This measures context use at the same prediction
targets, separately from generated-task accuracy.
