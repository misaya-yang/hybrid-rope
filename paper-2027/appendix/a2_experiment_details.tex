\subsection{Larger-scale supporting evidence}

The \(750\mathrm{M}\) `continue@4K' experiment starts both arms from the same
2K geometric checkpoint and applies a 500M-token continuation with either the
geometric or EVQ table (seed 42). At 16K, PPL is \(45.1\) versus \(24.4\); on
40 passkey trials at 8K, strict autoregressive exact match is \(0\%\) versus
\(77.5\%\). This is a single-seed post-hoc continuation result, not a
from-scratch allocation control.

\subsection{Modality-dependent \texorpdfstring{$\tau$}{tau} correction for video DiT}
\label{sec:tau-correction}

The text-style reference used for this sweep gives
$\tau{=}K_t/\sqrt{T}=2.83$ for $K_t{=}16$ and $T{=}32$, whereas the best tested
Video DiT point is $\tau{=}1.5$ ($0.53\times$). We use this empirical
correction only to select the supporting video configuration; the sweep does
not derive a modality-general coefficient.

\subsection{Cross-modal evidence: video DiT with bidirectional attention}
\label{sec:video-dit}

We evaluate a 129.6M video diffusion transformer with bidirectional attention
and 3D RoPE on Oscillating Moving MNIST
\citep{srivastava2015unsupervised}. Training uses 32 frames; evaluation uses
128 frames ($4\times$) with temporal \rs{}. For each seed, the geometric and
EVQ-Cosh ($\tau{=}1.5$) branches are trained in the same head-to-head run.

Table~\ref{tab:dit-h2h} reports two independent seeds. The within-seed
contrasts favor EVQ on train, all-frame extrapolation, and far-frame
extrapolation MSE in both runs; the two-seed mean deltas are $-26\%$, $-16\%$,

video effect size.

\paragraph{Low-phase-channel diagnostic.}
At base${}=10{,}000$, six of the 16 temporal frequencies accumulate less than
$0.1$ rad over the 32 training frames. In the pre-specified diagnostic,
$\tau{=}1.2$ leaves 11/16 channels above this threshold (effective rank 9),
whereas $\tau{=}1.5$ leaves 12/16 (rank 10). Repeating the head-to-head
comparison at base${=}1{,}000$ places 13/16 geometric channels above the
threshold. In this single-seed run, $\tau{=}1.2$ and $\tau{=}1.5$ differ by
less than $0.3\%$ on the reported metrics and both improve far-frame MSE by
about $48\%$ relative to Geo. This is consistent with the diagnostic; it is
not an equivalence or causal estimate.

\subsection{What the schedule concretely does}
\label{sec:schedule-detail}

At $\tau{=}4$, $b{=}5\times10^5$, the warp moves budget toward short
inverse-frequency scales: the continuous quantile $u{=}0.5$ moves from
$1/\omega{=}707$ to $9.69$, and $u{=}0.9$ from $134{,}609$ to $1{,}720$.
The deployed midpoint grid therefore changes sampled extrema and span at finite
$K$; the exact-range experiments anchor those quantities explicitly. At
$\tau{=}2$, $\rho_\tau(0.1){=}1.71$ and $\rho_\tau(1){=}0.55$, while the band
$1/\omega{=}2048$ has relative density $0.76$. Thus thinning concentrates on
channels with $1/\omega\gg\Ltr$ while preserving more of the training-scale
band. This motivates a directional prediction: channels with $1/\omega\ll\Ltr$
complete many periods during training, so positions beyond $\Ltr$ land in
already-trained phase regions, while channels with $1/\omega\gg\Ltr$ never
complete one and extrapolate into unseen phase. Moving budget toward the former
should favour extrapolation, while any in-window cost must be measured after
training. The surrogate does not assign the much larger mismatch observed when
a pretrained model is retrofitted with a new table.
