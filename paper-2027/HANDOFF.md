allocation effects at fixed frequency support, separates positional-basis structure
from learned frequency–coordinate compatibility, and demonstrates useful
constructions under their respective training or deployment protocols.

The current argument has three evidence groups: identify allocation; distinguish
learned compatibility; demonstrate utility. Exact TailSpline is a method candidate
within the third group. Its conditional mathematical optimum does not establish
task superiority, and it cannot inherit Cosh, BM, or mix075 results.
