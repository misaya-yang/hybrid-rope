# Extended research records

The submission PDF is the compact manuscript in the parent directory. Its six
appendices contain the proofs, protocols and complete primary results needed
to assess the main claims.

This folder preserves the previous appendix sources and associated tables/vector
figures as historical records. They are not compiled into the submission PDF.
Their section names, labels and cross-references belong to the earlier manuscript.
The active numerical inputs and CPU verifiers remain in the parent `figs/` folder.

Retained topics include additional architecture and video experiments, earlier
frozen profiles, amplitude comparisons, approximation studies, interval-objective
analyses and adaptation details. These records are preserved for inspection and
reuse; they are not extra prerequisites for reading the compact manuscript.

The corresponding full pre-reorganization source archive is retained in the
repository research history. No experimental raw output was deleted or regenerated.
