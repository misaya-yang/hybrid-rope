\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
A context window is useful when a model can use the information within it.
For a model trained to length $L$, an extension factor $s$ specifies a target
length $sL$; quality at the lengths served by that window determines its value.
RoPE's base selects a frequency range, while the placement of frequencies
inside it determines the positional basis. We study how this internal
allocation can improve model quality within the intended context range.

MrRoPE unifies frequency rescaling through mixed-radix conversion and proposes
progressive extension \citep{tian2026mrrope}; YaRN and LeRoPE further demonstrate
the value of scaling and learning frequencies \citep{peng2024yarn,karypis2026lerope}.
We investigate the positional structure and quality of these allocations.
Paired experiments hold the
frequency endpoints fixed and show that interior placement improves both learned
extrapolation and frozen-model performance. Additional controls distinguish
its effects from total frequency displacement and learned coordinate assignment.

Full sine--cosine geometry reveals how distinct frequencies can supply
overlapping positional directions. For frozen extension, a model already uses
its native frequencies; we introduce TailSpline to allocate extension through
a smooth connection to the low-frequency tail. A single static table improves
clean RULER over MrRoPE-Pro at both $2L$ and $4L$, by $3.39$ and $11.72$
percentage points, with a small observed native-window trade-off.
Cosh provides a complementary frequency transport for extrapolation, used in
paired training to establish the wider value of changing $z$.

Our contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Identify the value of allocation.} Controlled training and frozen
interventions separate interior placement from range, total displacement,
and learned coordinate assignment.
\item \textbf{Characterize its positional structure.} Full-pair geometry
quantifies finite-window overlap; a discrete kernel criterion distinguishes
spectral changes from fixed changes of basis.
\item \textbf{Improve quality within the extended window.} TailSpline gives a
closed-form frozen allocation with strong paired task gains at intermediate
and target lengths. Cosh supplies a supporting extrapolation instance.
\end{itemize}
