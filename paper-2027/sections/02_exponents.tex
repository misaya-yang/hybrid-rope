\section{Allocation as a Design Variable}
\label{sec:exponents}\label{sec:identification}
For $K=d_{\rm rot}/2$ pairs, standard RoPE uses $\omega_k=b^{-k/K}$. Any strictly ordered positive table admits
\begin{equation}
x_k=-\log\omega_k=a+Rz_k,\quad z_k=\frac{x_k-x_0}{x_{K-1}-x_0},\quad z_0=0,\ z_{K-1}=1.
\label{eq:table-decomposition}
\end{equation}
The endpoints $(a,R)$ fix the sampled log-frequency interval, called the support; $K-2$ interior coordinates define allocation. Every geometric table has $z_k=k/(K-1)$ irrespective of base. The full space $\mathcal Z_K=\{0=z_0<\cdots<z_{K-1}=1\}$ includes analytic subfamilies and positive-gap learned parameterizations.

The endpoints determine the covered range; $z$ determines how the rotary pairs
are placed inside it. Fixing endpoints isolates this placement effect, rather
than requiring all deployment methods to retain the native range.
For deployment relative to a native table, write
\begin{equation}
\omega'_k=\omega_k^N s^{-m_k},\qquad d_k=\log(\omega_k^N/\omega'_k)=m_k\log s.
\label{eq:movement-allocation}
\end{equation}
Changing frequencies preserves RoPE's relative-position composition and norm preservation \citep{su2024roformer}. Multiplying cosine/sine amplitudes by $g$ separately scales the rotary Q/K contribution by $g^2$. Fixed-support experiments isolate $z$; complete methods may jointly choose allocation, range and amplitude.

Geo denotes each protocol's geometric reference and Native a released model's inherited table. Standard and midpoint geometric grids share $z$ but differ by an overall frequency offset. Exact grid and endpoint conventions are in Appendix~\ref{sec:table-identities}.

\paragraph{Separating the effects of allocation.}
Fixed endpoints isolate internal placement. Holding $D=\sum_kd_k$ fixed
then compares allocations with equal total log displacement; permuting a fixed
frequency multiset tests coordinate assignment. At fixed native table and
target endpoints, $D$ is itself a statistic of $z$. Equal-displacement
controls therefore resolve finer configuration effects within this design
space. The complete control definitions are in
Appendix~\ref{sec:identification-details}.
