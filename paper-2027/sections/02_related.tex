\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Write $x_k=-\log\omega_k=a+Rz_k$ as in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:table-decomposition}. Prior work changes several, nonexclusive
parts of this parameterisation: realised phases, support $(a,R)$, interior
allocation $z$, or a larger positional operator. The coordinates are distinct
but can interact.

\paragraph{Range and support.}
RoPE encodes relative displacement through rotation \citep{su2024roformer}; we
take that operator as given and ask where its finite frequencies are placed.
Position interpolation rescales positions, while YaRN and LongRoPE apply
frequency-dependent or channel-wise range transport
\citep{chen2024position,peng2024yarn,ding2024longrope,shang2025longrope2}.
The geometric-family base bounds attainable context length and governs a
distinct extrapolation regime \citep{xu2024base,liu2024scaling}. FMRoPE moves
$(a,R)$ through that scalar base while retaining a uniform exponent grid
\citep{oka2026fmrope}. MrRoPE gives a distinct training-free
mixed-radix conversion of pretrained frequencies \citep{tian2026mrrope}.
These comparisons change support or realised phases; our controlled question
pins both endpoints and the log-span before moving $z$.

\paragraph{Frequency use and operator scope.}
Trained RoPE uses its bands unevenly: query/key norms, data-induced dependency
scales, and long-range retrieval expose different frequency structure
\citep{barbero2025round,oka2026frequencyentropy,wu2026datashapes,chiang2025rotary}.
HoPE, CoPE, and RoPE-ID respectively replace, clip, or restrict selected
rotations \citep{chen2025hope,li2026copeclipped,wertheimer2026frayed}. Our
geometry instead measures the phase-invariant effective dimension of all $K$
two-dimensional pair subspaces, and the intervention reallocates those pairs
without removing a plane. GRAPE generalises the planar group action and
Selective RoPE learns input-dependent angles
\citep{zhang2026grape,movahedi2026selectiverope}; we retain the canonical planes
and one session-static frequency table.

\paragraph{Learning and constructing frequency tables.}
LeRoPE learns one log-scale per frequency band, shared across layers and heads,
and reports consistent non-geometric profiles on a 52M--2.5B model ladder
\citep{karypis2026lerope}. In its 217M ablation, retraining with a frequency
table frozen from an independent LeRoPE run retains $63.6\%$ of the full
validation-perplexity gain over RoPE, compared with $10.4\%$ for partial RoPE.
The frozen-table ablation locates substantial value in the table itself; our
fixed-support intervention isolates the degree of freedom carrying that value.
AdaRoPE learns per-head and per-dimension
frequencies together with length-dependent attention scaling
\citep{wang2026adarope}. Resonance RoPE analytically snaps wavelengths to close
phase gaps during interpolation \citep{wang2024resonance}; \evq{} instead
obtains a shared fixed table from a stated convex surrogate and tests its
interior allocation at fixed support. DAPE, FIRE, FoPE, and content-dependent
re-positioning learn a larger positional function than a fixed RoPE table
\citep{zheng2024dape,li2024fire,hua2025fope,li2026repo}.
