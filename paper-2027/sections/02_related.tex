\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Nonuniform scaling and frozen extension.}
YaRN blends interpolation and extrapolation across frequency bands
\citep{peng2024yarn}. MrRoPE organizes scaling through mixed-radix conversion
and designs the intermediate allocation, with MrRoPE-Pro progressively
redistributing its increments \citep{tian2026mrrope}. These are the closest
frozen-extension references. TailSpline retains MrPro's outer bands and derives
a different transition from a discrete tail-connection objective; matched
support and total-displacement controls identify allocation and residual shape
effects. LongRoPE and LongRoPE2 combine nonuniform scaling with search and
learning \citep{ding2024longrope,shang2025longrope2}; our main deployment
comparisons install fixed rules without weight updates.

\paragraph{Learnable frequencies.}
LeRoPE learns per-frequency log scales jointly with model weights and analyzes
their attention gradients \citep{karypis2026lerope}. Its fixed-frequency
transfer experiment also separates the learned table from joint learning
dynamics. Our work complements this direction with controlled interior-placement
interventions and public-parameter constructions for frozen extension and
native-window quality. All three constructions use public RoPE parameters: Cosh studies learning
and adaptation, while TailSpline and NCP target frozen deployment.

\paragraph{Positional structure and learned frequency use.}
Round and Round, HoPE and Massive Values investigate frequency roles and
content use \citep{barbero2025round,chen2025hope,jin2025massive}; FoPE changes
Fourier components \citep{hua2025fope}. STRING characterizes relative-translation
encodings, while GRAPE connects group generators, frequencies and norms
\citep{schenck2025string,zhang2026grape}. Our complete-pair analysis and
integer-position criterion distinguish positional overlap, coordinate assignment
and a changed spectrum. Selective RoPE and RePo instead learn input-dependent
rotations or positions \citep{movahedi2026selectiverope,li2026repo}.

HELMET distinguishes application behavior beyond synthetic retrieval
\citep{yen2024helmet}, motivating separate retrieval, natural-QA and native-window
results.
