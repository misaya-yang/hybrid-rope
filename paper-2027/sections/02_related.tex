\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Frequency design and context extension.}
RoPE \citep{su2024roformer} admits many frequency choices. Base-scaling analyses and FMRoPE connect frequency range, training length and extrapolation \citep{liu2024scaling,xu2024base,oka2026fmrope}. Position interpolation rescales all frequencies; NTK-aware and Dynamic NTK adjust the geometric base \citep{chen2024position,bloc972023ntk,emozilla2023dynamic}. YaRN~\citep{peng2024yarn} combines bandwise scaling and gain. MrRoPE explicitly designs mixed-radix increments and compares uniform with progressive conversion \citep{tian2026mrrope}. S3PE explores sigmoid-shaped interpolation with continued pretraining \citep{zhang2025s3pe}. Nonuniform and smooth frequency design are therefore established. Our contribution combines paired fixed-support training, equal-displacement controls, full-pair geometry and analytic allocation objectives. TailSpline retains MrRoPE's outer-band policy and derives the transition profile from a low-frequency boundary objective on adjacent log-frequency gaps.

\paragraph{Learned frequencies and positional structure.}
LeRoPE learns shared per-frequency scales and includes Fixed LeRoPE, training with transferred frozen tables \citep{karypis2026lerope}; AdaRoPE studies head-specific frequencies and amplitude \citep{wang2026adarope}. These establish learned-frequency value, whereas our paired endpoints isolate interior allocation and our explicit constructors require no learned table. Studies of symbolic/positional attention and data-dependent bands examine how models use frequencies \citep{barbero2025round,urrutia2026decoupling,oka2026frequencyentropy,wu2026datashapes}. DoPE uses entropy of model representations to guide denoising \citep{xiong2025dope}. Our full-pair analysis concerns the supplied positional functions under a declared separation measure, using classical principal-angle and frame geometry \citep{bjorck1973angles,benedetto2003frames}; compatibility experiments connect this basis to learned use.

\paragraph{Broader design spaces.}
LongRoPE and LongRoPE2 combine nonuniform scaling with search or continued training \citep{ding2024longrope,shang2025longrope2}. FoPE, GRAPE, Selective RoPE and RePo alter the Fourier components, group action or position dependence \citep{hua2025fope,zhang2026grape,movahedi2026selectiverope,li2026repo}. These are complementary to reallocating frequencies within the existing rotary operator. Our constructions use this existing operator for learning-time and frozen-deployment allocation.
