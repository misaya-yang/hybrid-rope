\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Frequency design and context extension.}
RoPE \citep{su2024roformer} admits many frequency choices. Base-scaling analyses and FMRoPE connect frequency range, training length and extrapolation \citep{liu2024scaling,xu2024base,oka2026fmrope}. Position interpolation rescales all frequencies; NTK-aware and Dynamic NTK adjust the geometric base \citep{chen2024position,bloc972023ntk,emozilla2023dynamic}. YaRN combines frequency-dependent interpolation with amplitude scaling \citep{peng2024yarn}. MrRoPE explicitly designs mixed-radix increments and compares uniform with progressive conversion \citep{tian2026mrrope}. Nonuniform frequency design is therefore established. Our contribution is controlled evidence for gains beyond range changes, followed by explicit allocation objectives. TailSpline retains the standard outer-band policy but optimizes the transition's low-frequency junction; it does not reintroduce the mixed-radix coordinate as a new idea.

\paragraph{Learned frequencies and positional structure.}
LeRoPE learns shared per-frequency scales and also trains with transferred fixed tables \citep{karypis2026lerope}; AdaRoPE studies head-specific frequencies and amplitude \citep{wang2026adarope}. These establish learned-frequency value, whereas our paired endpoints isolate interior allocation and our explicit constructors require no learned table. Studies of symbolic/positional attention and data-dependent bands examine how models use frequencies \citep{barbero2025round,urrutia2026decoupling,oka2026frequencyentropy,wu2026datashapes}. DoPE uses entropy of model representations to guide denoising \citep{xiong2025dope}. Our full-pair analysis concerns the supplied positional functions under a declared separation measure, using classical principal-angle and frame geometry \citep{bjorck1973angles,benedetto2003frames}; compatibility experiments connect this basis to learned use.

\paragraph{Broader design spaces.}
LongRoPE and LongRoPE2 combine nonuniform scaling with search or continued training \citep{ding2024longrope,shang2025longrope2}. FoPE, GRAPE, Selective RoPE and RePo alter the Fourier components, group action or position dependence \citep{hua2025fope,zhang2026grape,movahedi2026selectiverope,li2026repo}. These are complementary to reallocating frequencies within the existing rotary operator. We compare complete methods under their actual learning or frozen-deployment protocols, rather than treating fixed-support identification as a restriction on subsequent design.
