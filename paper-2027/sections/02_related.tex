\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Frequency scaling and frozen deployment.}
RoPE \citep{su2024roformer}, base analyses and FMRoPE connect frequency range with extrapolation \citep{liu2024scaling,xu2024base,oka2026fmrope}. Position interpolation, NTK scaling, YaRN and MrRoPE provide established rescaling policies \citep{chen2024position,bloc972023ntk,peng2024yarn,tian2026mrrope}. MrRoPE's mixed-radix products and cumulative log displacement are equivalent coordinates for the same static frequency transform. TailSpline retains its outer bands and changes the transition objective. Our controlled interventions identify the value of the internal allocation, and our discrete objective yields an explicit tail-connection rule. LongRoPE and LongRoPE2 combine search and learning, the latter using mixed-context training \citep{ding2024longrope,shang2025longrope2}. Their deployment results have different training budgets from a frozen table swap.

\paragraph{Frequency function and learning.}
Round and Round studies positional and semantic frequency use; its $p$-RoPE gains include Gemma training from scratch \citep{barbero2025round}. HoPE studies high-frequency positional encoding \citep{chen2025hope}; Massive Values studies large Q/K components \citep{jin2025massive}. FoPE changes Fourier components, while LeRoPE learns frequencies and derives their attention/value gradients \citep{hua2025fope,karypis2026lerope}. We build on frequency design by isolating the value of interior placement and connecting positional structure to explicit constructions for learning, frozen extension and native-window tasks.

\paragraph{Group structure and changes of basis.}
STRING characterizes differentiable matrix encodings satisfying an identity and a group-like relative-translation law, and relates them to RoPE through an orthogonal basis \citep{schenck2025string}. GRAPE connects group generators, learned frequencies and frequency--norm coupling \citep{zhang2026grape}. Our integer-position criterion uses rotation spectra to separate a frequency change from a fixed change of basis (Appendix~\ref{sec:discrete-kernel-proof}).

\paragraph{Task structure and positional dependence.}
PINE changes inter-document masks and ordering to target document-level invariance \citep{wang2024pine}; PRoPE encodes relative camera geometry \citep{li2025cameras}. Selective RoPE and RePo learn input-dependent rotations or positions \citep{movahedi2026selectiverope,li2026repo}. These modify different objects from a static frequency allocation. HELMET separates application categories whose behavior synthetic retrieval alone does not predict \citep{yen2024helmet}, motivating distinct retrieval, natural-QA and native-window evidence rather than a single pooled score.

Our contribution connects controlled allocation interventions, complete-pair
positional analysis and explicit constructions. Fixed-range and equal-displacement
comparisons establish the design freedom; TailSpline and NCP demonstrate its
value for long-context and native-window tasks, respectively.
