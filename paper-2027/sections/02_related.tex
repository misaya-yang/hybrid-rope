\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Positional encoding can determine whether a transformer generalises beyond its
training length \citep{kazemnejad2023impact}. RoPE extensions rescale positions,
change a scalar base, transport frequency bands, or remap remote phases
\citep{chen2024position,peng2024yarn,ding2024longrope,shang2025longrope2,
oka2026fmrope,tian2026mrrope,tang2026jetlong}. Frequency-dependent transport can
realise a non-uniform $z$, but couples it to support or phase changes. Base
analyses bound the geometric family's attainable context and identify a critical
dimension \citep{xu2024base,liu2024scaling}; learned use of a supplied grid adds
a data-dependent view \citep{wu2026datashapes}. We isolate $z$ directly: what
changes when both endpoints and the log-span are pinned and only its interiors
move?

Other work measures unequal use of RoPE bands, removes or clips selected
rotations, learns input-dependent angles or frequency tables, or replaces the
fixed table with a larger positional function
\citep{barbero2025round,oka2026frequencyentropy,chiang2025rotary,
chen2025hope,li2026copeclipped,wertheimer2026frayed,zhang2026grape,
movahedi2026selectiverope,wang2024resonance,zheng2024dape,li2024fire,
hua2025fope,li2026repo}. LeRoPE and AdaRoPE make allocation explicit through
learned non-geometric frequencies \citep{karypis2026lerope,wang2026adarope};
LeRoPE's frozen-table ablation also makes the in-window value of allocation
visible. We retain all $K$ canonical rotary planes, identify $z$ at fixed
support, and derive a closed-form table without frequency search or learned
positional parameters.
