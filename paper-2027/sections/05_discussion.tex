\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

RoPE assigns a finite number of rotary channels to a continuous spectral range,
and Theorem~\ref{thm:budget} says what that costs: usable positional dimension
is the nominal $2K$ deflated by the mean redundancy $\bar c$. The exact-range
experiment shows the deflation is not fixed by the operator, its base, or its
sampled endpoints --- moving only the interior channels changes the trained
model. Frequency allocation is a design decision, not an implementation detail,
and \evq{} is one closed-form, zero-parameter way to exercise it.

Allocation and range transport act at different times on different objects:
allocation shapes the table a model is trained or adapted on, transport acts on
that table at inference. The $\rs{}$ and MLA results show the substrate still
matters after a range operator is applied and when few rotary pairs exist, and
the phase-matched OLMo experiment separates allocation from exposure --- both
arms receive identical target-$8$K/$16$K positions, data, updates, and trainable
modules, yet Native reaches $2.02\%$ RULER at $8$K against $31.63\%$. The near
tie at $4$K on 2Wiki exact ($22.0/21.5\%$) shows the in-window trade is
task-dependent rather than a uniform loss of capability.

The budget accounting of \S\ref{sec:budget-shift} makes that trade falsifiable:
thinning the intermediate $L<\lambda\le M$ band should cost more on simultaneous
multi-reference resolution than on single-reference retrieval, and a post-hoc
split of the named NIAH families agrees in direction ($-21.7$ against $-52.3$
points at $4$K). What the theory supplies is a budget identity, its slow-end collapse, and an
exact obstruction to repairing a table swap after training; what it does not
supply is a preferred curve or a certified operating point. That division is the
durable part: operator design,
spectral allocation, and deployment-time transport are distinct, composable
parts of a positional-encoding system. A RoPE table spends a finite spectral
budget, and where its interior channels land should be chosen deliberately,
before that budget is transported.
