\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}
A frequency range leaves a consequential choice open: where to place the
frequencies within it. Controlled interventions and equal-displacement
comparisons establish the value of this choice. Full-pair geometry characterizes
positional overlap, while coordinate interventions distinguish it from learned
frequency use.

TailSpline improves long-context task quality across model families and on
natural question answering. NCP improves native-window tasks at fixed support;
Cosh supplies paired learning and extrapolation evidence. Together, these
constructions establish $z$ as a practical design dimension for extension and
in-window quality\label{page:bodyend}.
