\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}
A frequency range leaves a consequential choice open: where to place the
frequencies within it. Controlled interventions and equal-displacement
comparisons establish the value of this choice. Full-pair geometry characterizes
positional overlap, while coordinate interventions distinguish it from learned
frequency use.

TailSpline turns this freedom into a closed-form extension, improving
long-context task quality across model families and on natural question
answering. NCP demonstrates a complementary use of allocation within the native
window, with fixed support and no weight updates. Cosh supplies paired learning
and extrapolation evidence. Together, these constructions make $z$ a practical
design dimension for both extension and in-window quality.
