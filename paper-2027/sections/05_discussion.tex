\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}
Frequency allocation is a practical design dimension within and beyond RoPE's
native window. Controlled interventions establish its value beyond frequency
range and total displacement. Complete-pair geometry and coordinate interventions
distinguish positional structure from learned use. TailSpline improves
long-context quality across model families and on natural QA; NCP improves
native-window tasks at fixed support, and Cosh supplies supporting learning
and extrapolation evidence.\label{page:bodyend}
