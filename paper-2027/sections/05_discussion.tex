\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}
Choosing RoPE's frequency range leaves an important design choice open:
how its interior frequencies support the model's use of context. Our controls
isolate this choice, and the constructions turn it into improvements in frozen
extension, native-window language modeling, and learned extrapolation. The
resulting view of allocation connects positional structure with learned
frequency use, making quality throughout the working context range a concrete
design objective.
