\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}
Internal exponent allocation $z$ is a design freedom for improving RoPE-based models. Fixed-support and equal-displacement controls identify the value of internal placement; full-pair geometry characterizes the positional structure it controls. Cosh and TailSpline turn this freedom into explicit density and boundary designs, with language-modeling, retrieval and natural-QA gains.

The resulting design space extends from learned allocations to static, zero-training adjustments. Internal placement can be optimized jointly with support, boundaries and amplitude to serve different model and task requirements. The joint in-window/OOD gains in controlled configurations motivate systematic in-window enhancement of mature models as a further application.
