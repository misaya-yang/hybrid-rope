\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}
Internal exponent allocation $z$ is independently useful and constructively exploitable. Fixed-support experiments isolate its extrapolation and frozen-task effects; finite-window geometry characterizes the supplied positional structure. Cosh learning, TailSpline zero-training deployment and BM natural QA demonstrate practical gains under their respective protocols.

The observed scope is specific: Llama has small native/intermediate PPL costs and a QA-family regression; OLMo has substantial generation-cap rates; small Qwen panels favor MrPro over BM. These outcomes delimit the tested constructions, while the positive results establish allocation value. Complete methods can combine $z$ with support, boundaries, terminal scaling and amplitude. Systematic mature in-window enhancement and broader transfer of boundary choices remain future directions.
