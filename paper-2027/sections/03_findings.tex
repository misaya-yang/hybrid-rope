\subsection{The effect remains in frozen models}
\label{sec:exp-frozen-support}

On OLMo-$2$-$1$B and Qwen-$2.5$-$1.5$B, we fix the checkpoint, sampled endpoints, $64$ pairs, attention amplitude, input rows, and decoder. The range extends from the native fast frequency to one-quarter of the native slow frequency. On OLMo's nine held-out RULER tasks \citep{hsieh2024ruler}, uniform normalized exponents score $0.56\%$, a coarse ramp $61.04\%$, and a residual-guided profile $60.47\%$. Qwen's four-task development comparison gives $57.75/64.00/66.50\%$ (Appendix~\ref{sec:frozen-fixed-support}). The simple ramp already captures much of the difference: the finding is the consequence of interior placement at fixed support.

\paragraph{Beyond one shape.}\label{sec:factorial}
The matched-endpoint M4 factorial tests two bases, two training lengths, three head dimensions and three paired seeds. Reference Cosh, a preassigned $1.25\times$ strength and matched exponential improve weighted OOD NLL in $7/12$, $10/12$ and $9/12$ configurations. These short, $128$-step runs support allocation breadth (Appendix~\ref{sec:exact-range-factorial}); the longer paired training above establishes the replicated extrapolation result.

A separate three-seed native Std-RoPE shape comparison also improves all five reported $512$--$8$K extrapolation lengths, so the effect is not restricted to a midpoint geometric reference (Appendix~\ref{sec:native-grid-control}).
