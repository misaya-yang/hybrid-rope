\section{Identifying the Value of \texorpdfstring{$z$}{z} Beyond Support}
\label{sec:controlled-findings}

We first test whether internal allocation $z$ is useful after frequency support is fixed. Paired training measures its contribution to learned extrapolation; frozen interventions test task gains without weight updates. Both isolate internal placement from the sampled endpoints.

\subsection{The effect remains in frozen models}
\label{sec:exp-frozen-support}

On OLMo-$2$-$1$B and Qwen-$2.5$-$1.5$B, we fix the checkpoint, sampled endpoints, $64$ pairs, attention amplitude, input rows, and decoder. The range extends from the native fast frequency to one-quarter of the native slow frequency. On OLMo's nine held-out RULER tasks \citep{hsieh2024ruler}, uniform normalized exponents score $0.56\%$, a coarse ramp $61.04\%$, and a residual-guided profile $60.47\%$. Qwen's four-task development comparison gives $57.75/64.00/66.50\%$ (Appendix~\ref{sec:frozen-fixed-support}). The simple ramp already captures much of the difference: the finding is the consequence of interior placement at fixed support.

\paragraph{Internal shape beyond total displacement.}\label{sec:equal-displacement}
Fixed endpoints do not fix the sum of native-relative shifts $\sum_k d_k$. A separate frozen OLMo comparison holds that sum, endpoints, band and gain fixed: the symmetric profile BM and uniform transition MrRoPE-Uni score $51.32$ and $32.12\%$ on a six-task, $48$-input $16$K development panel. Total displacement therefore does not explain this score difference (Appendix~\ref{sec:four-method-control}). This control is separate from the training and natural-QA results; an additional development control fixes the increment centroid as well (Appendix~\ref{sec:fixed-total-profile}).

\paragraph{Beyond one shape.}\label{sec:factorial}
The short-run M4 factorial varies base, training length and head dimension across three seeds; Cosh and a matched exponential improve OOD loss in multiple configurations (Appendix~\ref{sec:exact-range-factorial}). A separate three-seed native Std-RoPE comparison improves all five $512$--$8$K lengths (Appendix~\ref{sec:native-grid-control}), extending the evidence beyond one geometric reference.
