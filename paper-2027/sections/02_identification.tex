\section{Support--Allocation Causal Decomposition}
\label{sec:identification}

\subsection{Fixed support isolates structured allocation}
\label{sec:exp-identify}

\paragraph{A clean interior-allocation intervention.}
Within each of three $151.9$M training seeds, both arms share architecture,
initialisation, token order, optimiser, LR schedule, global batch, a
$499{,}974{,}144$-token budget, and $32$ evaluation anchors. The baseline is
FMRoPE at fixed training support, implementing \citet[\S6.1]{oka2026fmrope}
with $\theta{=}L_{\mathrm{train}}{=}256$ and geometric exponents. Anchored
\evq{} ($\tau{=}4$) normalises its quantiles to the same extrema and log-span,
so only the $K{-}2=30$ interior frequencies move while retaining the deployed
Cosh table's $z$.
Every training seed favours anchored \evq{} at every OOD length. Anchored
\evq{} minus FMRoPE NLL is $+0.026$ at the $256$-token training length and
$-0.281/-0.176/-0.146$ at $512$/$1$K/$2$K
(Fig.~\ref{fig:spectral-budget-overview}b). No geometric base can reproduce this
intervention once the sampled support is pinned: \emph{fixed support $+$
different $z$ $\Rightarrow$ different trained-model behaviour.} Thus $z$ is a
separately identifiable coordinate, not a disguised base change.

\paragraph{Target-aware support identifies the second coordinate.}
The second condition permits the declared $L_{\mathrm{target}}$ to retarget each
arm's sampled support while preserving its normalised interior shape, giving the
paper-faithful FMRoPE control its intended target-aware input. The ordering then
reverses in all three seeds: anchored \evq{} minus FMRoPE NLL is
$+0.060/+0.227/+0.460$ at $512$/$1$K/$2$K. The two conditions answer different
causal questions. Fixed support isolates the effect of structured interior
allocation; target-aware retargeting shows how support selection changes which
allocation is effective.
Their reversal establishes support and allocation as distinct but interacting
coordinates of the same finite table. The full protocol, per-seed contrasts,
and both support conditions are in App.~\ref{sec:identification-details}.

\paragraph{Configuration and shape breadth.}
The fixed-support result is not confined to one base, head width, or Cosh
strength. A pre-specified $50.9$M factorial crosses two bases, two training
lengths, three head dimensions, and three paired seeds while pinning sampled
endpoints and log-span in every arm. Three Cosh strengths and a
deformation-matched exponential provide four structured non-geometric curves.
The strongest Cosh arm improves the weighted OOD objective in $10/12$
configurations; the matched exponential does so in $9/12$ and closely tracks the
formula-Cosh arm. This broadens the finding from one curve to a family of
structured allocations: Cosh supplies the analytic construction, while the
$151.9$M intervention above remains the effect-size owner. The complete matrix
is in App.~Table~\ref{tab:m4}; model-scale persistence is tested separately in
\S\ref{sec:exp-mature}.
