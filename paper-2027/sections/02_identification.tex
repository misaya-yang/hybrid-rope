\section{Identifying the Allocation Axis}
\label{sec:identification}

\subsection{Fixed-support training intervention}
\label{sec:exp-identify}

\paragraph{Allocation is identifiable at fixed support.}
Within each of three $151.9$M training seeds, both arms share architecture,
initialisation, token order, optimiser, LR schedule, global batch, a
$499{,}974{,}144$-token budget, and $32$ evaluation anchors. The baseline is
FMRoPE at fixed training support, implementing \citet[\S6.1]{oka2026fmrope}
with $\theta{=}L_{\mathrm{train}}{=}256$ and geometric exponents. Anchored
\evq{} ($\tau{=}4$) normalises its quantiles to the same extrema and log-span,
so only the $K{-}2=30$ interior frequencies move while retaining the deployed
Cosh table's $z$.
Every training seed favours anchored \evq{} at every OOD length. The anchored
\evq{} minus FMRoPE NLL difference is $+0.026$ at the $256$-token training
length and $-0.281/-0.176/-0.146$ at $512$/$1$K/$2$K
(Fig.~\ref{fig:spectral-budget-overview}c). No geometric base can reproduce this
intervention once the sampled support is pinned: \emph{fixed support $+$
different $z$ $\Rightarrow$ different trained-model behaviour.} Thus $z$ is a
separately identifiable coordinate, not a disguised base change.
Retargeting the support reverses the tested ordering, showing that support and
allocation are distinct but interacting design coordinates; the full protocol
and per-seed contrasts are in App.~\ref{sec:identification-details}.

\paragraph{Configuration and shape breadth.}
The fixed-support direction is not confined to one base, head width, or one
Cosh schedule. A pre-specified $50.9$M factorial crosses two bases, two training
lengths, three head dimensions, and three paired seeds while pinning the sampled
endpoints and log-span in every arm. Three Cosh strengths and a
deformation-matched exponential provide four distinct non-geometric interior
curves. The strongest Cosh arm improves the weighted OOD objective in $10/12$
structural configurations; the matched exponential does so in $9/12$ and
closely tracks the formula-Cosh arm. The factorial therefore broadens the
finding from one table to a family of interior reallocations: Cosh supplies the
analytic construction, while $z$ is the consequential design coordinate. The
$151.9$M intervention above remains the effect-size owner; the complete matrix
is in App.~Table~\ref{tab:m4}, and model-scale persistence is tested separately
in \S\ref{sec:exp-mature}.
