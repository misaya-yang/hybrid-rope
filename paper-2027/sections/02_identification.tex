\subsection{A paired intervention at fixed support}
\label{sec:exp-identify}

We train paired $151.9$M models with $K=32$ rotary pairs at length $256$. Within each of three seeds, architecture, initialization, token order, optimizer, schedule, and the $499{,}974{,}144$-token budget are identical. The geometric arm uses the FMRoPE training-base rule $b=L_{\mathrm{train}}$ \citep{oka2026fmrope}; the other anchors Cosh quantiles with $\tau=4$ to the same endpoints (construction in \S\ref{sec:construction}). Only the $30$ interior exponents differ.

With the training table retained at evaluation, mean Cosh-minus-geometric tail NLL is $+0.026$ at the training length and $-0.281/-0.176/-0.146$ at $512/1024/2048$ tokens. All three training seeds improve at every extended length (Fig.~\ref{fig:spectral-budget-overview}b; full contrasts in Appendix~\ref{sec:exact-range-control}). Thus interior allocation improves extrapolation at a small training-window cost, without changing either frequency endpoint.
