\subsection{A paired intervention at fixed support}
\label{sec:exp-identify}

Reallocating interior frequencies improves learned extrapolation at the same frequency endpoints. We train paired $151.9$M models with $K=32$ rotary pairs at length $256$.

Within each of three seeds, architecture, initialization, token order, optimizer, schedule, and the $499{,}974{,}144$-token budget are identical. The matched geometric control (Geo) uses the training-base configuration $b=L_{\mathrm{train}}$ from FMRoPE \citep{oka2026fmrope}; the EVQ-Cosh intervention anchors its quantiles with $\tau=4$ to the same highest and lowest frequencies and log-span (\S\ref{sec:construction}). Only the $30$ interior $z$ coordinates differ.

With the training support retained at evaluation, mean Cosh-minus-Geo tail NLL is $+0.026$ at the training length and $-0.281/-0.176/-0.146$ at $512/1024/2048$ tokens. All three paired seeds improve at every extended length (Fig.~\ref{fig:method-overview}b).

Retargeting both supports to $b=L_{\rm eval}$ while retaining each trained checkpoint and normalized allocation reverses the ordering: Cosh-minus-Geo NLL becomes $+0.060/+0.227/+0.460$, with all three seeds favoring Geo (Fig.~\ref{fig:spectral-budget-overview}a). Allocation is a useful design variable, and its preferred setting depends on the support policy.
