\subsection{A paired intervention at fixed support}
\label{sec:exp-identify}

Our small-model Cosh training studies span $50.9$M--$750$M. Within this series,
a paired $151.9$M experiment fixes both frequency endpoints to isolate
interior placement, using $K=32$ rotary pairs and training length $256$.

Within each of three seeds, architecture, initialization, token order, optimizer, schedule, and the $499{,}974{,}144$-token budget are identical. The matched geometric control (Geo) uses $b=L_{\mathrm{train}}$; the EVQ-Cosh intervention anchors its quantiles with $\tau=4$ to the same highest and lowest frequencies and log-span (\S\ref{sec:construction}). Only the $30$ interior $z$ coordinates differ.

With the training support retained at evaluation, mean Cosh-minus-Geo tail NLL is $+0.026$ at the training length and $-0.281/-0.176/-0.146$ at $512/1024/2048$ tokens. All three paired seeds improve at every extended length (Fig.~\ref{fig:method-overview}b).

Retargeting both supports to $b=L_{\rm eval}$ while retaining each trained checkpoint and normalized allocation reverses the ordering: Cosh-minus-Geo NLL becomes $+0.060/+0.227/+0.460$, with all three seeds favoring Geo (Table~\ref{tab:exact-range-3seed}). Allocation is a useful design variable, and its preferred setting depends on the support policy.
