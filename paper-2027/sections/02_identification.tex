\subsection{A paired intervention at fixed support}
\label{sec:exp-identify}

We train paired $151.9$M models with $K=32$ rotary pairs at length $256$. Within each of three seeds, architecture, initialization, token order, optimizer, schedule, and the $499{,}974{,}144$-token budget are identical. The geometric arm uses the FMRoPE training-base rule $b=L_{\mathrm{train}}$ \citep{oka2026fmrope}; the other anchors Cosh quantiles with $\tau=4$ to the same endpoints (construction in \S\ref{sec:construction}). Only the $30$ interior exponents differ. The factorial in \S\ref{sec:factorial} extends fixed-endpoint comparisons to bases $500$K and $1$M across twelve configurations.

With the training table retained at evaluation, mean Cosh-minus-geometric tail NLL is $+0.026$ at the training length and $-0.281/-0.176/-0.146$ at $512/1024/2048$ tokens. All three training seeds improve at every extended length (Fig.~\ref{fig:spectral-budget-overview}b; full contrasts in Appendix~\ref{sec:exact-range-control}). Thus internal placement can shift the learned length response while frequency endpoints remain unchanged. To see what this intervention changes in the model's input to attention, we next examine the basis supplied by a rotary pair.
