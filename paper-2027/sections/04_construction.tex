\section{Explicit Frequency Allocations}
\label{sec:allocation-methods}
Allocation changes how a fixed number of rotary pairs covers the frequency
range. Our main frozen construction, TailSpline, designs this change around
the model's native table. Cosh supplies a complementary transport for paired
training; Section~\ref{sec:ncp-main} develops a native-window allocation.

\subsection{Cosh as a supporting extrapolation transport}
\label{sec:construction}
Cosh changes $z$ through a monotone map of normalized exponents. Motivated by
finite-window overlap among slow pairs, it balances log-frequency density
concentration against the joint occupancy of the slow tail. The density objective and its unique minimizer
are derived in Appendix~\ref{sec:proofs}. Its inverse CDF is
\begin{equation}
Q_\tau(u)=1-\frac{\operatorname{arcsinh}((1-u)\sinh\tau)}{\tau},\qquad
u_k=\frac{k+1/2}{K}.
\label{eq:warp}
\end{equation}
The uniform-density limit is recovered as $\tau\to0$. Affine anchoring of
$Q_\tau(u_k)$ to the chosen endpoints gives a convex allocation that moves
interior frequencies toward the faster end; Appendix~\ref{sec:table-identities}
gives the installed grid and Eq.~\eqref{eq:anchored-cosh-order}.
This is the transport used in the fixed-range training intervention of
\S\ref{sec:exp-identify}. The strength $\tau$ determines the amount moved:
the fixed-support reference is $d_{\rm head}/\sqrt{L_{\rm train}}$, and the
MLA extrapolation recipe uses $1.414$. The factorial compares neighboring
strengths and a matched exponential transport
(Appendix~\ref{sec:exact-range-factorial}). Every installation retains the
standard rotary operator.
