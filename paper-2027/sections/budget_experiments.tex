\paragraph{Training.}
We reuse the 12-layer, width-768 decoder with MLP width 3072 and vocabulary
50304. The default is 7629 updates of 65536 input tokens each, or
499,974,144 tokens per arm, at training length 2048. Initialization, token
prefix, row order, optimizer, learning-rate schedule, and update count are
paired within each seed. The optimizer is fused AdamW with
$\beta=(0.9,0.95)$, weight decay 0.01, peak/minimum rates
$6\times10^{-4}/6\times10^{-5}$, 762 warmup updates, and clipping 1.0.
The final common budget is frozen from actual throughput and available resources
before observing method comparisons. A shortened, unstable training run is
reported as a pilot rather than a converged budget frontier.

\paragraph{Same-target evaluation.}
Before comparison, freeze 512 independent long documents from the held-out
FineWeb-Edu shard. Each has at least 8193 tokens, permitting 8192 inputs and
one shifted final label. Contexts of 512, 2048, 4096, and 8192 tokens predict
the same final 256 targets. The primary metric is document-mean NLL at 4096;
PPL is its exponential. Report 2048 same-target and whole-window NLL for
retention, and 8192/512 as trends. Every evaluation uses the training table
and unit gain, without test-time scaling or routing.

\paragraph{Remote-context control.}
For the 4096-token input, replace the first 3584 tokens with a fixed independent
document's prefix while retaining the last 512 input tokens, labels, length,
and position IDs. The same document derangement applies to all arms. Define
$C_A=\ell_A^{\rm replaced}-\ell_A^{\rm intact}$ and
$I_{\rm context}=C_{E16}-C_{G16}$. Greater sensitivity supports useful remote
context only alongside lower intact NLL; sensitivity to a wrong prefix alone
is not evidence of better use.

\paragraph{Contrasts and decision.}
At the primary length, report per-seed
\begin{align}
D_{16}&=\ell_{G16}-\ell_{E16},&D_{32}&=\ell_{G32}-\ell_{E32},&J&=D_{16}-D_{32},\\
H&=\ell_{E16}-\ell_{G32},&P&=\ell_{P16}-\ell_{E16},&U&=\ell_{U16}-\ell_{E16}.
\end{align}
The practical and noninferiority margins are 0.02 nat/token. Half-budget
preservation requires the interval for $H$ below the margin, native-window
retention, and comparison with both subset baselines. A positive $J$ separately
supports mitigation of budget loss. If G16 does not lose quality against G32,
there is no observed bottleneck to recover. A recovery ratio is reported only
when that denominator is stable and at least 0.02.

Three training seeds are the replication units. Report all seed contrasts,
their mean and standard deviation, and any Student-$t$ interval with the
normality assumption and two degrees of freedom explicit. Document bootstraps
condition on the trained checkpoints; documents are not training replications.
The main plot will show both G/E budget points, individual seeds, and P16/U16,
with native-window quality in a separate panel. Pending runs are not plotted
as predicted scores.

\section{Existing Evidence and Its Scope}
\label{sec:budget-external}

Prior fixed-support 151.9M training supports an allocation effect but used
length 256 and does not supply a baseline for the new 2K experiment
(App.~\ref{sec:exp-identify}). The older 50.9M factorial changes head count,
head dimension, and shape strength; it does not isolate the present budget
intervention.

The existing 432M MLA-style study has $d_{\rm rope}=32$, hence $K=16$.
Across three seeds, raw 16K PPL is 138.8 for geometric RoPE and 95.6 for EVQ;
8K is 35.4 versus 35.8. Under its shared run-specific wavelength blend, 16K
is 117.9 versus 71.1. This provides a relevant single-budget result and a
reason to test native retention, but no allocation-by-budget interaction.
Existing 750M training and retrieval results provide separate scale evidence.
Their original protocols and evidence boundaries remain in the supporting
appendices; neither is relabeled as a replication of the new experiment.
