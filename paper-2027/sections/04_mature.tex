\subsection{TailSpline for training-free deployment}
\label{sec:mature-adjustments}\label{sec:exp-zero-training-system}\label{sec:coordinate}\label{sec:bm-main}
A frozen model already uses its native frequencies. We retain its high-frequency band and divide its low-frequency tail by $s$, while designing the intervening allocation. Following the $32/1$-turn boundaries used by MrRoPE \citep{tian2026mrrope}, let $l$ be the last pair making more than $32$ turns over the native window and $h$ the first making less than one. Put $n=h-l$ and $q=\operatorname{clip}(k-l,0,n)$. The transition accumulates increments $\epsilon_q=m_q-m_{q-1}$, with $m_0=0$, $m_n=1$.

The increments have a direct spectral meaning. For a geometric native grid with gap $c=\log b/K$,
\begin{equation}
x'_{l+q}-x'_{l+q-1}=c+\epsilon_q\log s.
\label{eq:increment-log-gap}
\end{equation}
Their unit sum allocates exactly $\log s$ of extra span across the band. Beyond it, $m=1$ is constant, so the extra gap returns to zero.

MrRoPE-Pro uses increasing increments, $m_q=q(q+1)/[n(n+1)]$. We instead smooth the transition into the fully interpolated tail, whose subsequent increments are zero:
\begin{equation}
\min_{\epsilon:\,\sum_{q=1}^n\epsilon_q=1}
\sum_{q=1}^{n-1}(\epsilon_{q+1}-\epsilon_q)^2+\epsilon_n^2.
\label{eq:tailspline-objective}
\end{equation}
This one-sided objective penalizes internal changes and the terminal jump. It leaves the high-frequency entry unpenalized, an explicit boundary prior rather than a fitted model statistic.
\begin{theorem}[Finite-grid TailSpline]\label{thm:tailspline}
The unique minimizer of Eq.~\eqref{eq:tailspline-objective} has positive increments and cumulative profile
\begin{equation}
\epsilon_q=\frac{3(n+q)(n-q+1)}{n(n+1)(2n+1)},\qquad
m_q^{\rm TS}=\frac{q(3n^2+3n+1-q^2)}{n(n+1)(2n+1)}.
\label{eq:tailspline}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
The quadratic objective is strictly convex; solving its tridiagonal stationarity system gives Eq.~\eqref{eq:tailspline} (Appendix~\ref{sec:tailspline-details}). Adding the symmetric entry penalty $\epsilon_1^2$ yields BM, whose increments are proportional to $q(n-q+1)$ (Appendix~\ref{sec:bm-construction}). TailSpline reduces the terminal gap jump relative to MrPro by the exact factor $3/(2n+1)$: $0.086$ for Llama's $n=17$ and $0.081$ for OLMo's $n=18$. Figure~\ref{fig:tailspline-main}a includes the two outer zero-increment regions so both entry and tail jumps are visible. This is a construction guarantee, whose task consequences are tested below.

Install $\omega'_k=\omega_k^Ns^{-m_k}$ before prefill and retain it across all layers and input lengths for the declared $s$. We use $g=1+0.1\ln s$, matched to the baseline. Neither construction reads weights, activations or calibration outputs; the boundary rule uses only the native grid and reference length. The objectives prove construction properties; the following experiments test task value.
