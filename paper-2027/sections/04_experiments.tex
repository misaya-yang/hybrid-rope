\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup and endpoints}
\label{sec:exp-setup}

NLL/PPL, teacher-forced NLL-gap retrieval, causal source use, strict
autoregressive exact match, task F1/exact match, and RULER macro are kept as
separate endpoints throughout. \evq{} installs a different frequency table
before training; every trained system is evaluated with its own table.
Controlled from-scratch runs use fixed GPT-style decoder stacks
\citep{radford2019gpt2}; corpora, hyperparameters and seed scope are in
App.~\ref{sec:supporting-experiments} and with each result.

\subsection{Identifying the allocation axis}
\label{sec:exp-identify}

\paragraph{Protocol.} In the seed-$42$ pair, two $151.9$M models
share architecture, trainable initialisation, token order, optimiser, LR
schedule, batch geometry, a $499{,}974{,}144$-token budget, and $32$ frozen
evaluation anchors. The baseline is our paper-faithful implementation of the
uniform-in-log rule in \citet[\S6.1]{oka2026fmrope}, using training base
$256$; the intervention is endpoint-normalised cosh spacing at $\tau{=}4$, a
support-normalised form of the deployed midpoint Cosh quantiles. The two forms
share exactly the same normalised allocation $z$ in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:table-decomposition}. Their sampled extrema and log-frequency
span are identical, so only the $K{-}2=30$ interior frequencies move. We
measure paired final-$128$-token teacher-forced NLL.

  log-span pinned, moving only the $30$ interior frequencies incurs $+0.033$

\paragraph{Result.} Cosh-minus-uniform NLL is $+0.033$ at the $256$-token
training length and $-0.478/-0.205/-0.113$ at $512$/$1$K/$2$K in the seed-$42$
arm (Fig.~\ref{fig:identification}a). Since every sampled scalar support
quantity is pinned, no geometric base can reproduce the intervention:
\emph{fixed support $+$ different $z$ $\Rightarrow$ different trained-model
behaviour.}

\paragraph{Cross-configuration replication.} A pre-specified $50.9$M
factorial spans two bases, two training lengths, three head dimensions, and
three paired seeds, with additional boundary-$\tau$ arms. It repeats the
exact-range control and scores weighted
teacher-forced OOD NLL over $2\times/4\times/8\times$, using the $12$
structural configurations as the statistical units (Table~\ref{tab:m4}). The
pre-specified $1.25\times$ cosh and matched-exponential schedules favour the
non-uniform allocation in $10/12$ and $9/12$ configurations. The factorial
establishes cross-configuration direction; the $151.9$M pair provides the
direct effect size.

\paragraph{The same range transform does not erase the training table.}
In a separate $454$M three-seed composition test, applying the same fixed-scale
\rs{} transform changes $8$K teacher-forced NLL-gap passkey retrieval from $41\%$ to $61\%$ on Geo
but from $53\%$ to $100\%$ on \evq{}; $8$K PPL becomes $82.9$ versus $70.9$
(App.~Table~\ref{tab:evq-ramp}). Thus the leverage of a shared range operation
depends on the table seen during training. This is a YaRN-family composition
test, not a claim of superiority over a separately tuned range method. The
target-retargeted exact-range condition and its deployment boundary are
reported in App.~\ref{sec:identification-details}.

\subsection{The allocation signature persists at mature scale}
\label{sec:exp-mature}

\paragraph{$1.485$B from initialisation.}
A same-initialisation, same-scientific-recipe step-$0\!\rightarrow\!1{,}000$
trajectory over $2.097$B tokens gives \evq{}-minus-Geo NLL of
$+0.072/+0.038/-0.044/-0.135$ at $2$/$4$/$8$/$16$K, with $122/128$ and
$126/128$ held-out documents favouring \evq{} at $8$/$16$K. The released Geo
checkpoint and \evq{} arm use different trainer implementations, so this is
not a bitwise-paired comparison. Its role is scale persistence for natural-text
modelling, not causal identification or task capability.

\paragraph{$8$B with $300$ adaptation steps.}
On LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct \citep{grattafiori2024llama3}, matched rank-$64$
Q/K/V/O LoRA changes Native/\evq{} PPL from $6.82/10.07$ at the physical $8$K
cap to $108.96/24.07$ at $16$K and $991.48/127.91$ at $32$K. The long-range
direction holds on all $24$ frozen natural-text packs and all three source
domains. At true $16$K, median target-block hit@16 rises from $18.75\%$ to
$64.06\%$, and deleting the remote gold block changes NLL by
$-0.0095/+1.5055$. This is matched probability and causal source-use evidence,
not by itself top-1 task accuracy.

\paragraph{$1.485$B capability conversion with Q/K-only adaptation.}
Starting from matched OLMo-2 Stage-A parents, both arms freeze inherited V/O
LoRA and continue only Q/K for $300$ steps with the same data, order, optimiser,
trainable count, and $4$K/$8$K/$16$K phase exposure; physical sequences remain
at most $4$K. On held-out 2WikiMultiHopQA, Native/\evq{} exact match is
$22.0/21.5\%$ at $4$K, $0/17.5\%$ at $8$K, and $0/4.0\%$ at $16$K
\citep{bai2024longbench}. An independent continuation over all $13$ RULER
families gives official macro $72.19/42.44\%$, $2.02/31.63\%$, and
$0.38/5.03\%$ at the same lengths \citep{hsieh2024ruler}. Thus near-equal
in-window QA can coexist with a large $2\times$ transfer difference, while
RULER exposes a genuine in-window/long-range crossover.

\paragraph{$8$B task-adapted transfer.}
A separate $516$-step physical-$8$K LLaMA continuation yields Native/\evq{}
official RULER macro $94.44/77.60\%$ at $8$K and $0.295/14.03\%$ at $16$K;
normalised exact is $17.69/21.54\%$ and $0/1.54\%$. Training and evaluation rows
are disjoint but share generator families, so these rows establish task-adapted
length transfer rather than unseen-task transfer.

\subsection{Attributing the schedule and operating rule}
\label{sec:exp-tau}

\paragraph{The rule selects a basin, not a point optimum.} Across $21$
configurations in two studies, the best tested $\tau$ stays within a
$0.75\times$--$1.5\times$ window around the operating rule. An independent
sweep selects $\tau{=}5$ against a rule value of $5.657$, a $0.0119$ NLL
difference. Within the factorial, the rule is the best of the three tested Cosh
multipliers in $4/12$ configurations, while $1.25\times$ and $0.75\times$ are
best in $6/12$ and $2/12$. The scaling structure is useful; its finite
coefficient is empirical.

\paragraph{Cosh is a derived instance, not the only effective shape.} The
\emph{matched exponential} uses the endpoint-normalised quantile curve
$s_a(u){=}(e^{au}{-}1)/(e^a{-}1)$, with $a$ fixed so that its log-frequency
nodes have the same RMS displacement from the uniform grid as rule-cosh. The
two arms therefore move the same amount of budget with different curvature.
It improves over Geo in $9/12$
configurations and is not separated from rule-cosh: cosh-minus-exponential is
$+0.00074$ NLL, bootstrap $[-0.0055,0.0075]$, exact sign-flip $p{=}0.836$.
The empirical result identifies interior allocation. Cosh contributes a
closed-form, zero-learned-parameter solution of the stated convex surrogate, not a
uniqueness claim for trained objectives. Shape attribution therefore rests on
the fixed analytic schedules above, where parameter count and
optimisation effort are identical by construction.
