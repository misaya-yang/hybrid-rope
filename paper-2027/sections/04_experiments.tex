\section{Consequences across Model-Building Regimes}
\label{sec:experiments}

The fixed-support experiment identifies the coordinate. We next present its
consequences in the order that requires the least intervention: first a frozen
checkpoint, then matched adaptation, and finally co-adaptation from training.

\noindent\textbf{Zero training:} change $z$ after training while fixing
checkpoint, support, amplitude, rows, and decoder, asking whether allocation is
actionable without optimisation (OLMo $16$K RULER: same-support log-linear
$0.56\%$, official Transformers YaRN $7.94\%$, derived $60.47\%$).

\noindent\textbf{Matched adaptation:} retain each pretrained substrate while
matching adaptation capacity, data, and schedule, asking whether the coordinate
persists through low-rank adaptation (remote-source deletion adds $1.5055$ NLL
for the tested $8$B \evq{} adapter).

\noindent\textbf{From training:} install $z$ before optimisation while matching
the training contract, asking whether weights co-adapt to a different basis
($3/3$ seeds favour the reallocation at every tested OOD length).

\subsection{Zero-training frozen-checkpoint intervention}
\label{sec:exp-frozen-support}

\paragraph{Pure allocation at fixed support.}
Using $s{=}4$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:movement-allocation} constructs alternative tables
with the same fast and slow endpoints; all arms also share the checkpoint,
attention amplitude, evaluation rows, decoder, precision, and hardware. The
OLMo table, amplitude, split rule, checkpoint, and metric were frozen before
the nine confirmation tasks were evaluated. Table~\ref{tab:frozen-fixed-support}
separates standard references from the matched-support block. On OLMo, derived
minus same-support log-linear is $+59.92$ points ($[54.88,64.80]$), and
derived minus official Transformers YaRN is $+52.54$ points
($[46.99,57.95]$). The common amplitude $1+0.1\ln4$ is YaRN's default
factor-four attention scaling. On Qwen at $64$K, derived reaches $66.50\%$ versus
$57.75\%$ for the log-linear grid and $60.25\%$ for YaRN. The model-relative
derivation places the useful split; a label-free movement-profile ramp at that
split reproduces the OLMo recovery at $61.04\%$
(App.~\ref{sec:frozen-fixed-support}).

\paragraph{Zero-training deployment.}
Building on the pure-$z$ effect, the zero-training method installs the
reallocated long table with a fixed amplitude; a Native/long session route is
also evaluated as a deployment option. The system adds no learned parameters or
training tokens.

\paragraph{Natural-text likelihood.}
On FineWeb-Edu, the long profile at $4$K gives NLL $2.8774$ versus $2.7538$ for
Native, a difference of $+0.1236$ NLL. Against official Transformers YaRN at
factor four,
PG-19 tail NLL improves from $3.4404/3.7955$ to $3.1060/3.0977$ at $8/16$K.

\paragraph{Task capability.}
On $13$-family RULER at $8/16$K, macro is $0/0\%$ for Native,
$23.82/5.88\%$ for official Transformers YaRN at factor four, and
$67.72/54.40\%$ for the zero-training policy, which is higher on $12$ of $13$
families at each length. On full-$200$ Qasper, the session policy raises
token F1 from $18.03\%$ to $24.57\%$; on full-$200$ 2Wiki, applying the long
profile to every row raises token F1 from $25.69\%$ to $27.74\%$ (the routed
policy scores $26.66\%$). Across the six-task natural-context matrix, the
routed method scores $28.58/24.97\%$ at $2/4\times$, versus
$21.27/25.59\%$ for YaRN. Full protocols are in
App.~\ref{sec:frozen-fixed-support}.

\subsection{Matched low-rank adaptation}
\label{sec:exp-capability}

\paragraph{$1.485$B task-family length transfer.}
Starting from protocol-matched Native and \evq{} Stage-A parents, both OLMo-2
arms freeze inherited V/O LoRA and continue only Q/K for $300$ steps with
identical data, order, optimiser, trainable count, and phase exposure. On $200$
held-out 2WikiMultiHopQA rows per length, Native/\evq{} token-F1 is
$25.99/24.84\%$ at $4$K, $0.07/21.48\%$ at $8$K, and $0/8.57\%$ at $16$K
\citep{ho2020twowiki,bai2024longbench}. An independent $13$-family RULER
continuation gives Native/\evq{} macro $72.19/42.44\%$ at $4$K,
$2.02/31.63\%$ at $8$K, and $0.38/5.03\%$ at $16$K
\citep{hsieh2024ruler}. Training and evaluation use disjoint rows from the same
task families, establishing task-family-adapted length transfer
(App.~\ref{sec:mature-details}).

\paragraph{$8$B causal remote-source use.}
On LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct \citep{grattafiori2024llama3}, matched rank-$64$ Q/K/V/O
LoRA gives Native/\evq{} PPL $6.82/10.07$, $108.96/24.07$, and
$991.48/127.91$ at $8/16/32$K, with the long-range direction holding across all
$24$ frozen natural-text packs and three domains. At true $16$K, median
target-block hit@16 rises from $18.75\%$ to $64.06\%$; blocking decode attention
to the remote gold block changes NLL by $-0.0095/+1.5055$. This establishes
causal remote-source use in the tested adapted model
(App.~\ref{sec:mature-details}).

\subsection{From-training and co-adapted evidence}
\label{sec:exp-mature}

The paired three-seed result in \S\ref{sec:exp-identify} is the primary
fixed-support identification. The experiments below test whether the same
allocation axis persists across architecture, scale, and modality.

\paragraph{Scarce-channel architecture.}
Interior allocation remains consequential when the rotary budget is only
$K{=}16$: a $432$M MLA-style transformer \citep{deepseekv2} trained from scratch
over three seeds gives Geo/\evq{} PPL $35.4/35.8$ at the $8$K training length
and $138.8/95.6$ at $16$K, with every seed favouring \evq{}
(App.~Table~\ref{tab:mla}).

\paragraph{Full-parameter persistence and early-training scale.}
Starting both $750$M arms from a shared $2$K Geo checkpoint and continuing all
parameters for $500$M tokens at $4$K gives Geo/\evq{} PPL $22.0/22.3$ at $4$K
but $45.1/24.4$ at $16$K; strict autoregressive exact retrieval at $8$K changes
from $0\%$ to $77.5\%$ (App.~Table~\ref{tab:750m}). At $1.485$B, a pre-specified
early-training comparison starts from the same public initialisation, follows
the same scientific recipe, and uses a matched $2.097$B-token budget. \evq{}
crosses by $8$K, with $122/128$ and $126/128$ held-out documents favouring it at
$8/16$K (App.~Fig.~\ref{fig:olmo-scale-crossover}). Together these protocols
repeat the in-window/long-length crossover through $1.485$B.

\paragraph{Modal breadth.}
On Oscillating Moving MNIST, two head-to-head seeds of a $129.6$M video DiT
with bidirectional $3$D RoPE give mean Geo-to-\evq{} denoising-MSE deltas of
$-26\%$ on train, $-16\%$ over all extrapolated frames, and $-33\%$ on far
extrapolated frames (App.~Table~\ref{tab:dit-h2h}), providing supporting
modality breadth beyond autoregressive text.
