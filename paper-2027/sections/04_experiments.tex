\section{Model Quality Within and Beyond the Native Window}
\label{sec:experiments}\label{sec:exp-mature}
We evaluate one static TailSpline table across context lengths, test internal
shape with an equal-displacement control, and examine in-window allocation with
NCP. Supporting Cosh experiments study allocation during learning.
The protocol glossary in Table~\ref{tab:protocol-glossary} distinguishes
these interventions.

\subsection{Task quality with a frozen TailSpline table}
\label{sec:tailspline-evaluation}
Llama-$3$-$8$B-Instruct uses $s=4$, native length $8192$, band $[18,35]$
and gain $1+0.1\ln4$. OLMo-$2$-$1$B-Instruct uses native length $4096$
and band $[14,32]$ with the same scale and gain rule. T/P denote TailSpline
and MrRoPE-Pro. Each pair shares weights, outer frequencies, endpoints, gain,
inputs and decoder; only its internal allocation changes. Clean RULER
\citep{hsieh2024ruler} uses source-order inputs, no content padding or depth
selection, batch $1$ and the official scorer. Table~\ref{tab:clean-length-main}

\paragraph{Quality across the window.}
The Llama $32$K panel contains $2{,}600$ paired prompts, with actual lengths
$28{,}270$--$32{,}606$ tokens. Its $+11.72$-point gain spans all four task
families; $12$ of $13$ task means improve. The $16$K panel adds $650$ pairs
and a $+3.39$-point gain. RULER QA improves by $8$ and $9$ points at
$16$K and $32$K, respectively. The same table serves native-length inputs:
on $650$ clean $8$K prompts, it scores $85.16\%$, with a slight reduction
from original RoPE's $90.03\%$. Full native and extended task breakdowns
are in Appendix~\ref{sec:clean-confirmation}.

\paragraph{Cross-model confirmation.}
OLMo's clean $16$K panel contains $2{,}600$ pairs and improves by $41.42$
points, with positive differences on all $13$ tasks. Retrieval, tracking,
aggregation and QA improve by $60.19$, $9.70$, $16.13$ and $7.50$ points,

\paragraph{Natural question answering.}

source-context clusters. Each model retokenizes the same source pool;
OLMo's actual inputs are $4{,}110$--$16{,}319$ tokens, all beyond its native

\paragraph{Internal shape at equal displacement.}
\label{sec:clean-shape-main}
The clean T--C comparison matches frequency endpoints, outer bands, gain and

\subsection{Improving task performance within RoPE's native context}
\label{sec:ncp-main}
Optimizing $z$ improves task performance within RoPE's native context. We construct
\emph{Native Contrastive Proximal} allocation (NCP) from the public native grid
and length. A binary reference comparison models an aligned target competing
with a uniformly phased distractor. Averaging its logistic loss over relative
separations gives a reference risk $\mathcal R(\phi)$ at phase span
$\phi=(L-1)\omega$. NCP selects log-frequency shifts $u_k$ through
\begin{equation}
\min_u\ \sum_k\left[\mathcal R(\phi_ke^{-u_k})+\frac{9}{8}u_k^2\right],
\qquad \omega'_k=\omega_ke^{-u_k}.
\label{eq:ncp-main}
\end{equation}
The quadratic term keeps the allocation near its native grid. We fix both
endpoints, bound shifts, and preserve frequency ordering and minimum log gaps;
Appendix~\ref{sec:ncp-construction-new} gives the reference model, constraints
and numerical construction. Gain remains $1$, and the same table is installed
across layers and inputs without weight updates or calibration.

On OLMo's native $4$K clean Full-13 panel ($780$ paired prompts), NCP raises
the score from $69.37$ to $70.78\%$, a $+1.41$-point gain with paired interval
$[0.20,2.58]$. This gain establishes a direct in-window benefit from optimizing
frequency allocation at fixed support. Appendix~\ref{sec:ncp-results-new}
reports the complete task breakdown and companion direction controls.

\subsection{Supporting extrapolation evidence with Cosh}
\label{sec:exp-capability}
A $432$M MLA-style model uses $16$ rotary pairs, $500$M training tokens at $8$K
and three seeds \citep{deepseekv2,deepseekv3}, with fixed Cosh strength
$\tau=1.414$. All arms use the same FineWeb-Edu evaluation chunks. This recipe
uses unanchored midpoint tables, distinct from the fixed-endpoint intervention.
Cosh improves all five extended lengths in every seed; $16$K PPL falls from
$138.8$ to $95.6$. The benefit appears during training and persists under a
common wavelength blend (Fig.~\ref{fig:allocation-learning};
Appendix~\ref{sec:mla-results}).
\begin{figure}[ht]
\centering\includegraphics[width=.70\linewidth]{fig_allocation_learning.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{Cosh improves extrapolation across training seeds.} Three-seed
mean and range for the $432$M MLA model; dashed curves use the common wavelength
blend ($s=4$). Evaluation uses common text chunks.}

\end{figure}
A $750$M single-seed continuation fixes endpoints and reduces $16$K PPL from
$45.1$ to $24.4$; $8$K answer-token exact match rises from $0/40$ to $31/40$.
Full recipes and matched OLMo adaptation are in Appendix~\ref{sec:learning-summary}.
