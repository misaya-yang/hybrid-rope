\section{Consequences across Model-Building Regimes}
\label{sec:experiments}

The fixed-support experiment identifies the variable. The studies below test
whether it remains consequential under different architectures, training
stages, scales, and pretrained checkpoints. Each comparison retains its own
metric and control.

%% =========================================================================
\subsection{Architecture, range composition, and full-parameter scale}
\label{sec:exp-mature}

\paragraph{Scarce-channel MLA.}
A $432$M MLA transformer trains from scratch over three seeds with only
$d_{\mathrm{rope}}{=}32$, hence $K{=}16$ rotary frequencies. Geo/\evq{} PPL
is $35.4/35.8$ at the $8$K training length. At $16$K, however, it is
$138.8/95.6$: a $31.1\%$ reduction, with every seed favouring \evq{}
(App.~Table~\ref{tab:mla}). The result shows that \evq{} is effective in this
$K{=}16$ setting; it is not a controlled scaling law in $K$.

\paragraph{Range composition.}
The training-time table changes the outcome of the same index-space scaling
rule. In a $454$M three-seed MHA study, a fixed \rs{} rule raises $8$K teacher-forced
retrieval from $41\%$ to $61\%$ on Geo and from $53\%$ to $100\%$ on \evq{}
(App.~Table~\ref{tab:evq-ramp}). The code path, cut indices, and scaling formula
are shared; because the underlying frequencies differ, this is a composition
result rather than a movement-matched mechanism control.

\paragraph{Continuation and full-parameter scale evidence.}
The crossover also appears after full-parameter continuation. Starting both
$750$M arms from a shared $2$K Geo checkpoint and continuing all parameters for
$500$M tokens at $4$K gives Geo/\evq{} PPL $22.0/22.3$ at $4$K but
$45.1/24.4$ at $16$K; strict autoregressive exact retrieval at $8$K changes
from $0\%$ to $77.5\%$ (App.~Table~\ref{tab:750m}). At $1.485$B, a
same-initialisation, same-scientific-recipe from-initialisation comparison with
a $2.097$B-token budget crosses in favour of \evq{} at $8$K and $16$K;
$122/128$ and $126/128$ held-out documents favour it at those lengths. Exact
PPL values and protocol details appear in App.~\ref{sec:mature-details}. Both
larger protocols exhibit the same effective-context crossover: Geo retains the
lower in-window PPL, while \evq{} overtakes it at longer lengths. Together they
carry the full-parameter scale evidence through $1.485$B.

\paragraph{Frozen checkpoints.}
\label{sec:exp-frozen-support}
At fixed support, attention amplitude, checkpoint, data, and decoder, changing
only interior $z$ raises OLMo-2 $16$K RULER macro from $0.56\%$ to $60.47\%$;
the paired difference is $59.92$ points with interval $[54.88,64.80]$. A coarse
projected ramp scores $61.04\%$ and is indistinguishable from the detailed
profile. On Qwen2.5 at $64$K ($2\times$ Native), it is
$57.75\%/66.50\%$.
These released-checkpoint interventions show that the
allocation coordinate remains consequential after pretraining without claiming
profile-detail uniqueness (App.~\ref{sec:frozen-fixed-support}).

\subsection{From effective context to remote-content use}
\label{sec:exp-capability}

\paragraph{$1.485$B task-family-adapted length transfer.}
Starting from protocol-matched Native and \evq{} OLMo-2 Stage-A parents, both
arms freeze inherited V/O LoRA and continue only Q/K for $300$ steps with identical data, order,
optimiser, trainable count, and $4$K/$8$K/$16$K phase exposure; physical
sequences remain at most $4$K. On held-out 2WikiMultiHopQA, Native/\evq{}
token-F1 is $25.99/24.84\%$ at $4$K, $0.07/21.48\%$ at $8$K, and
$0/8.57\%$ at $16$K
\citep{bai2024longbench}. Thus Q/K adaptation keeps the in-window QA score
close to Native while only the \evq{} arm carries substantial answer overlap
to $8$K in this task family. This is length transfer within the adapted family,
not unseen-task generalization. An
independent continuation over all $13$ RULER families shows the same $2\times$
direction, with official macro $2.02/31.63\%$ at $8$K
\citep{hsieh2024ruler}; all lengths and exact-match values appear in
App.~\ref{sec:mature-details}.

\paragraph{$8$B causal remote-source use.}
On LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct \citep{grattafiori2024llama3}, rank-$64$ Q/K/V/O LoRA
uses the same adaptation pipeline for Native and \evq{}. PPL is $6.82/10.07$,
$108.96/24.07$, and $991.48/127.91$ at $8$/$16$/$32$K, with the long-range
direction holding on all $24$ frozen natural-text packs and all three source
domains. More importantly, at true $16$K, median target-block hit@16 rises
from $18.75\%$ to $64.06\%$. Blocking answer-side decode attention to the remote
gold block in every head, while preserving all prompt tokens and rotary KV
indices, changes NLL by $-0.0095/+1.5055$. This position-preserving intervention
shows causal dependence on the remote source in the tested adapted model rather
than a local readout artifact. The task-family continuation is reported
in App.~\ref{sec:mature-details}.

A two-seed $129.6$M video DiT also favours \evq{} on training, all-frame
extrapolation, and far-frame MSE in both runs
(App.~\ref{sec:video-dit}), extending the evidence to bidirectional 3D RoPE.
