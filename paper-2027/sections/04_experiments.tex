\section{The Allocation Coordinate across Model-Building Regimes}
\label{sec:experiments}

We intervene on $z$ at three stages of the model lifecycle. With a mature
checkpoint fully frozen, only allocation changes; with matched low-rank
adaptation, pretrained representations can learn to exploit a new basis; from
training, weights learn that basis through co-adaptation. We present the three
regimes in that order, beginning with the direct mature-model intervention.

%% =========================================================================
\subsection{Fully frozen: zero-training intervention}
\label{sec:exp-frozen-support}

\paragraph{Allocation alone changes a mature model.}
Given Native frequencies $\omega_k$, extension factor $s$, and displacement
weights $m_k\in[0,1]$, the frozen intervention installs
\begin{equation}
\widehat\omega_k=\omega_k\big[(1-m_k)+m_k/s\big],\qquad
\widehat z_k=\frac{-\log\widehat\omega_k-\widehat a}{\widehat R},
\label{eq:movement-allocation}
\end{equation}
with the fast and slow endpoints pinned. After support normalisation, the
uniform, coarse label-free, and derived tables are three values of the same
interior coordinate $z$ (App.~\ref{sec:frozen-fixed-support}). Using $s{=}4$,
all arms also share the checkpoint, attention amplitude, evaluation rows,
decoder, precision, and hardware. The
OLMo table, amplitude, model-relative split rule
(App.~\ref{sec:frozen-fixed-support}), checkpoint, and metric were frozen before
the nine confirmation tasks were evaluated. Figure~\ref{fig:frozen-fixed-support}
separates standard references from the matched-support block. On OLMo, derived
minus uniform allocation is $+59.92$ points ($[54.88,64.80]$), and
derived minus official Transformers YaRN is $+52.54$ points
($[46.99,57.95]$). The common amplitude $1+0.1\ln4$ is YaRN's default
factor-four attention scaling. On Qwen at $64$K, derived reaches $66.50\%$ versus
$57.75\%$ for uniform allocation and $60.25\%$ for YaRN. The model-relative
derivation locates where the allocation should bend; a coarse label-free
control fitted to it reproduces the OLMo recovery at $61.04\%$
(App.~\ref{sec:frozen-fixed-support}).

  \caption{\textbf{Changing only interior allocation can transform a frozen
  checkpoint.} Grey reference rows retain their own support or transport;

\paragraph{Zero-training deployment.}
Building on the pure-$z$ effect, the zero-training method installs the
reallocated long table with a fixed amplitude; a Native/long session route is
also evaluated as a deployment option. The system adds no learned parameters or
training tokens.

\paragraph{Natural-text likelihood.}
Against official Transformers YaRN at factor four, PG-19 tail NLL improves from
$3.4404/3.7955$ to $3.1060/3.0977$ at $8/16$K. Forcing the same long profile
at $4$K gives FineWeb-Edu NLL $2.8774$ versus $2.7538$ for Native; the session
route retains Native inside its window ($+0.1236$ NLL).

\paragraph{Task capability.}
On $13$-family RULER at $8/16$K, macro is $0/0\%$ for Native,
$23.82/5.88\%$ for official Transformers YaRN at factor four, and
$67.72/54.40\%$ for the zero-training policy, which is higher on $12$ of $13$
families at each length. On full-$200$ Qasper, the session policy raises
token F1 from $18.03\%$ to $24.57\%$; on full-$200$ 2Wiki, applying the long
profile to every row raises token F1 from $25.69\%$ to $27.74\%$ (the routed
policy scores $26.66\%$). Full protocols are in
App.~\ref{sec:frozen-fixed-support}.

\subsection{Pretrained representations: matched low-rank adaptation}
\label{sec:exp-capability}

Limited adaptation asks whether a mature pretrained model can learn to use a
new allocation without full-parameter retraining. The $1.485$B studies measure
task-family length transfer; the $8$B source-blocking intervention tests whether
the adapted model causally uses remote evidence.

\paragraph{$1.485$B task-family length transfer.}
Starting from protocol-matched Native and \evq{} Stage-A parents, both OLMo-2
arms freeze inherited V/O LoRA and continue only Q/K for $300$ steps with
identical data, order, optimiser, trainable count, and phase exposure. On $200$
held-out 2WikiMultiHopQA rows per length, Native/\evq{} token-F1 is
$0.07/21.48\%$ and $0/8.57\%$ at $8/16$K, versus $25.99/24.84\%$ at $4$K
\citep{ho2020twowiki,bai2024longbench}. An independent
$13$-family RULER continuation shows the same crossover: Native/\evq{} macro is
$2.02/31.63\%$ and $0.38/5.03\%$ at $8/16$K, versus $72.19/42.44\%$ at $4$K
\citep{hsieh2024ruler}. Training and evaluation use disjoint rows from the same
task families, establishing task-family-adapted length transfer
(App.~\ref{sec:mature-details}).

\paragraph{$8$B causal remote-source use.}
On LLaMA-3-8B-Instruct \citep{grattafiori2024llama3}, matched rank-$64$ Q/K/V/O
LoRA raises median target-block hit@16 from $18.75\%$ to $64.06\%$ at true
$16$K; blocking decode attention to the remote gold block changes NLL by
$-0.0095/+1.5055$. Across all $24$ frozen natural-text packs and three domains,
Native/\evq{} PPL is $108.96/24.07$ and $991.48/127.91$ at $16/32$K, versus
$6.82/10.07$ at $8$K. The blocking intervention establishes causal
remote-source use
(App.~\ref{sec:mature-details}).

\subsection{From training: weight--table co-adaptation}
\label{sec:exp-mature}

Installing $z$ before or during full-parameter training asks whether weights
learn to exploit the resulting positional basis. The paired three-seed result
in \S\ref{sec:exp-identify} supplies the fixed-support causal anchor; the
experiments below test its persistence across architecture, scale, and
modality.

\paragraph{Scarce-channel architecture.}
Interior allocation remains consequential when the rotary budget is only
$K{=}16$: a $432$M MLA-style transformer \citep{deepseekv2} trained from scratch
over three seeds gives Geo/\evq{} PPL $138.8/95.6$ at $16$K, with every seed
favouring \evq{}; at the $8$K training length it is $35.4/35.8$
(App.~Table~\ref{tab:mla}). The near tie at the training window and the
three-seed separation at $2\times$ make this a scarce-budget test: allocation
remains actionable when the architecture provides only sixteen rotary pairs.

\paragraph{Full-parameter persistence and early-training scale.}
Continuing all parameters of two $750$M arms from a shared $2$K Geo checkpoint
for $500$M tokens at $4$K gives Geo/\evq{} PPL $45.1/24.4$ at $16$K and changes
strict autoregressive exact retrieval at $8$K from $0\%$ to $77.5\%$; at $4$K,
PPL is $22.0/22.3$ (App.~Table~\ref{tab:750m}). At $1.485$B, a pre-specified
early-training comparison starts from the same public initialisation, follows
the same scientific recipe, and uses a matched $2.097$B-token budget. \evq{}
crosses by $8$K, with $122/128$ and $126/128$ held-out documents favouring it at
$8/16$K (App.~Fig.~\ref{fig:olmo-scale-crossover}). Together these protocols
repeat the in-window/long-length crossover through $1.485$B. They also provide
different forms of scale evidence: the $750$M continuation links long-text PPL
to strict autoregressive retrieval, while the $1.485$B run locates the same
crossover in natural-text loss at the largest from-initialisation scale tested.

\paragraph{Modal breadth.}
On Oscillating Moving MNIST, a head-to-head comparison in a $129.6$M video DiT
with bidirectional $3$D RoPE gives Geo-to-\evq{} denoising-MSE deltas of
$-21\%$ on train, $-16\%$ over all extrapolated frames, and $-35\%$ on far
extrapolated frames (App.~Table~\ref{tab:dit-h2h}), extending the evidence
beyond autoregressive text. Its simultaneous training-frame improvement shows
that reallocating $z$ is not intrinsically an autoregressive extrapolation-only
operation.
