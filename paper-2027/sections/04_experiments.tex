\subsection{Long-context gains from a frozen TailSpline table}
\label{sec:tailspline-evaluation}
Frozen Llama-$3$-$8$B-Instruct uses $s=4$, native window $8192$, band $[18,35]$
and gain $1+0.1\ln4$. TailSpline and MrPro share weights, outer frequencies,
endpoints, gain, inputs and decoder; their interior allocation and resulting
total displacement differ. Each arm installs one static table before prefill. This evaluates quality
within a fixed target range. MrRoPE's published Llama comparison uses $s=16$
($8$K to $128$K), while its Qwen comparison uses $s=4$
($32$K to $128$K) \citep{tian2026mrrope}; our paired Llama comparison uses $s=4$.

\paragraph{Clean RULER-200 at $32$K.}
The confirmation panel uses $200$ source-order inputs per RULER task
($2{,}600$ paired prompts), without depth selection or content padding.
Actual lengths are $28{,}270$--$32{,}606$ tokens; both arms use unpadded batch $1$
and the same official scorer. TailSpline scores $68.27\%$ versus $56.54\%$,
a task-equal difference of $+11.72$ points (paired $95\%$ interval
$[10.32,13.11]$). It wins $12$ of $13$ task means. Retrieval, tracking,
aggregation and QA all have positive family contrasts; leave-one-task-out
differences remain positive (Appendix~\ref{sec:clean-confirmation}).
The primary endpoint is the task-equal score at $32$K.

\paragraph{Quality at the intermediate length.}
The completed clean $16$K panel uses $50$ inputs per task ($650$ pairs).
TailSpline scores $86.09\%$ versus $82.71\%$, a $+3.39$-point Full-13 gain
with interval $[1.53,5.34]$. Retrieval improves by $4.50$ points; the RULER
QA family scores $68/60\%$, a $+8$-point gain with interval $[1,15]$.
Together with the $+9$-point QA gain at $32$K, these comparisons show task
benefits at both tested extended lengths (Table~\ref{tab:clean-length-main}).
The complete task breakdown is in Appendix~\ref{sec:clean16k-confirmation}.

\paragraph{Native-window trade-off.}
TailSpline's $+11.72$-point clean long-context gain accompanies a small native-window trade-off. On the matched $130$-prompt $8$K task reference, it scores $89.74\%$ versus original Native's $91.88\%$: an observed $2.14$-point difference, or $2.33\%$ relative reduction (paired T--Native interval $[-6.14,1.92]$ points). This native estimate uses the classic padded panel; the long-context gain uses the clean source-order panel. Whole-prefix $8$K PPL is $5.29528$ versus $5.27592$, a $0.37\%$ relative increase. The same static table serves both windows, without length-specific switching. Paired intervals and the separate equal-dose T/C diagnostic are reported in Appendix~\ref{sec:current-controls}.

\paragraph{Classic length curves and transfer.}
The classic Llama panel has $10$ paired inputs per task at $8/16/32$K
($390$/arm), with output-independent depth selection and prefix padding.
PPL uses $32$ Proof-Pile and $14$ PG-19 test documents ($138$ LM rows/arm)
\citep{azerbayev2022proofpile,rae2019compressive}.
Full-13 AUC rises from $75.60$ to $78.80\%$ ($+3.20$ points,
$95\%$ interval $[0.65,5.79]$); its nested NIAH subset improves by $3.75$ points.
PPL AUC changes by $-0.097\%$, with small $8/16$K increases; QA-family AUC
changes by $-1.25$ points. On OLMo-$2$-$1$B-Instruct, the same formula at
band $[14,32]$ and $4/8/16$K raises Full-13 AUC from $17.36$ to $66.60\%$
($+49.23$ points, interval $[45.34,53.04]$), with all task AUC differences
positive. Full curves (Fig.~\ref{fig:tailspline-classic}), aggregation weights and termination counts are in Appendix~\ref{sec:tailspline-details}.

\paragraph{TailSpline on natural QA.}
The frozen five-task Natural-QA pool contains $631$ paired questions from $524$ source-context clusters, retokenized for Llama with actual inputs $3694$--$16304$ tokens. Both methods use the same $s=4$ tables, unpadded batch $1$, prompts and generation caps. Whole-response token F1 is averaged within each task, then equally across tasks; uncertainty resamples source-context clusters. Overall F1 is $41.08/40.88\%$ for T/P, a $+0.20$-point difference with interval $[-1.53,1.89]$. Appendix~\ref{sec:tailspline-natural-qa} reports the complete pool and native-length strata. The observed F1 scores are close, with the interval spanning both orderings. T/P end with EOS on $625/624$ inputs, reach the cap on $6/7$, and produce no empty responses. Full task scores and protocol are in Appendix~\ref{sec:tailspline-natural-qa}.

\subsection{Supporting extrapolation evidence with Cosh}
\label{sec:exp-capability}
A $432$M MLA-style model uses $16$ rotary pairs, $500$M training tokens at $8$K
and three seeds \citep{deepseekv2,deepseekv3}, with fixed Cosh strength $\tau=1.414$. All arms use the same FineWeb-Edu evaluation chunks. This learning recipe
uses unanchored midpoint tables; Table~\ref{tab:protocol-main} distinguishes it
from the fixed-endpoint intervention. Cosh improves all five extended lengths in every seed;
$16$K PPL falls from $138.8$ to $95.6$. The benefit appears at half and
three-quarters of training and persists under a common wavelength blend
(Fig.~\ref{fig:allocation-learning}; Appendix~\ref{sec:mla-results}).

\centering\includegraphics[width=.70\linewidth]{fig_allocation_learning.pdf}

\end{figure}

A $750$M single-seed continuation also improves $16$K PPL and $8$K answer-token
exact match, with a small $4$K cost. Matched OLMo adaptation uses explicit
long-phase exposure and exhibits native/extended-length trade-offs. Their full
budgets, scores and supervision remain in Appendix~\ref{sec:learning-summary}.
