\section{Model Effects of an Analytic Allocation}
\label{sec:experiments}
\label{sec:exp-mature}

We evaluate the analytic construction in matched learning settings before turning to frozen deployment. Each comparison specifies its own training regime, finite grid, and evaluation endpoint.

\subsection{Language modeling and retrieval}
\label{sec:exp-capability}

\paragraph{Scarce rotary channels.}
A $432$M MLA-style model allocates only $16$ pairs to explicit rotation \citep{deepseekv2,deepseekv3}. Both arms train on $500$M tokens at length $8192$. Across three seeds, \evq{} reduces mean $16$K perplexity from $138.8$ to $95.6$, with $8$K perplexity at $35.4$ and $35.8$ (Table~\ref{tab:training-models}). This tests allocation when few coordinates carry explicit rotation.

\paragraph{Continued full-parameter training.}
The $750$M experiment continues training from a $2$K checkpoint for $500$M tokens at length $4096$. The Cosh arm preserves endpoints. At $16$K, perplexity falls from $45.1$ to $24.4$ ($4$K: $22.0$ vs $22.3$). Across $40$ associated $8$K passkey cases, greedy exact match rises from $0\%$ to $77.5\%$. The teacher-forced probe is saturated for both arms, separating probability-based retrieval from complete generation (Appendix~\ref{sec:experiment-details}).

\paragraph{Matched Llama-$3$-$8$B adaptation.}
We compare Native and \evq{} under the same $300$-step rank-$64$ LoRA adaptation at length $8$K \citep{hu2022lora,grattafiori2024llama3} across $24$ frozen temporal text packs. Figure~\ref{fig:8b-length-curve} shows the full curve: Native/\evq{} PPL is $6.82/10.07$ at $8$K, $108.96/24.07$ at $16$K, and $991.48/127.91$ at $32$K. We characterize this as an adaptation length-transfer trade-off rather than lossless extension, demonstrating that installed allocation changes extrapolation under matched adaptation budgets.

\paragraph{Source use and task-family continuation.}
On ten true-$16$K passkey cases, removing decode attention to the remote gold block increases answer-token NLL by $1.5055$ in \evq{}, compared with $-0.0095$ in Native, confirming reliance on distant source access. A separate continuation trains both arms on RULER generator families at length $8$K and evaluates on new rows: at $16$K, official macro is $0.30\%$ for Native-LoRA and $14.03\%$ for \evq{}-LoRA (exact match $0\%$ vs $1.54\%$), while at $8$K it is $94.44/77.60\%$ (Appendix~\ref{sec:llama8b}).

\paragraph{Composition and further settings.}
In the $454$M three-seed experiment, applying the same index-based smooth-ramp scaler to both learned tables gives $16$K PPL of $157.7$ for Geo and $107.5$ for \evq{} (Appendix~\ref{sec:range-composition}). OLMo scale checks and video-DiT comparisons appear in Apps.~\ref{sec:olmo2-1b} and~\ref{sec:video-dit}.
