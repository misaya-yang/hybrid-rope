The appendices specify the installed frequency tables, exponent grids, attention amplitudes, training and adaptation recipes, datasets, and evaluation procedures. The fixed-range experiment reports three paired training seeds; the larger-model and frozen-model tables identify their comparison units and sample counts. The accompanying source archive contains the active TeX, bibliography, plotted figures, numerical summaries, a SHA256 manifest, and standalone verification of the explicit Gram-matrix examples. Experimental checkpoints and public datasets are documented separately, together with the preprocessing required for selected reruns.

The core fixed-support identification uses six runs of $499{,}974{,}144$ tokens each; the $432$M comparison uses six $500$M-token runs, and the $750$M continuation uses two $500$M-token runs. These are protocol-specific training budgets, with the corresponding recipes and additional studies detailed in the appendices.
