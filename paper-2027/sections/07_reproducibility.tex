The appendices specify the installed frequency tables, exponent grids, attention amplitudes, training and adaptation recipes, datasets, and evaluation procedures. The fixed-range experiment reports three paired training seeds; the larger-model and frozen-model tables identify their comparison units and sample counts. The source archive provides the active TeX, vector figures, numerical summaries and CPU checks of the Gram-matrix and allocation identities. It also includes the frozen two- and three-arm data-preparation, table-construction, generation, scoring and paired-report entrypoints with their local dependencies. The runtime guide specifies commands, public data sources and the pinned RULER revision.

The core fixed-support identification uses six runs of $499{,}974{,}144$ tokens each; the $432$M comparison uses six $500$M-token runs, and the $750$M continuation uses two $500$M-token runs. These are protocol-specific training budgets, with the corresponding recipes and additional studies detailed in the appendices.
