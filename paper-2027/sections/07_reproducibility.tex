The appendices specify the installed frequency tables, exponent grids,
attention amplitudes, training and adaptation recipes, datasets, and evaluation
procedures. The fixed-range experiment reports three paired training seeds;
the larger-model and frozen-model tables identify their comparison units and
sample counts. Figure sources and numerical summaries are bound to recorded
experiment artifacts; the appendices provide complete derivations of the
closed-form construction and explicit exponent-adjustment formulas.
Training, evaluation, and analysis records are kept with their corresponding
protocols so that the reported tables and figures can be reconstructed.
