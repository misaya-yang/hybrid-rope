The new six-arm experiment is specified by a frozen protocol, full frequency
tables, and same-target input construction. Implementation checks cover fixed
pair identities, inactive coordinates, parameter initialization, temperature,
causal label alignment, and remote-prefix replacement. Training records must
include data and frequency hashes, paired row order, optimizer state, token
budget, and resumable progress. The primary analysis reports training-seed and
conditional document uncertainty separately. At this revision, CPU checks and
the implementation are available; new model results remain pending. Previous
experiments retain their own protocols and artifact-availability limitations
in the supporting appendices. In particular, the single-budget MLA aggregate
does not imply availability of its original training checkpoints.
