\section{Scope and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The new claim to test is whether allocation reduces the long-context cost of a
smaller rotary budget under exact architecture and training controls. The
finite-distance results establish conditional restrictions on position
responses. They neither rank all trained models nor guarantee that EVQ beats
frequency-subset selection. A negative result against P16 or U16 must remain
in the main comparison and change the interpretation of the construction.

A successful experiment would establish representation efficiency for the
specified decoder and evaluation range. It would not establish universal
scaling with rotary width, superiority in zero-training extension, or an
automatic halving of KV memory. Decoupled rotary keys in MLA may connect width
to cache cost, but the present full-width MHA experiment does not measure that
systems benefit. The separate KLD follow-up studies recurrent memory and does
not form a second method or supporting result for this paper.
