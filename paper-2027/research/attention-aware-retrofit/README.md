## Theme and success conditions

The programme asks how far one global request-static RoPE table and gain can
extend a mature checkpoint while Native NLL and downstream damage each remain
about `0.12` or lower.

1. **Zero training:** frozen weights; maximize useful reach toward 8x. The
   current OLMo log-p2 s4/c=.074 arm is the 4x incumbent. Its PG-19 retention is
   `0.875302` and five-task retention is `0.915103`; the NLL gate is marginal
   against a literal `0.88` retention target. The tested s8 form is negative,
   not a global 8x impossibility result.
2. **Low-cost adaptation:** train only on small physical 2x/4x data, keep the
   same Native gates, and evaluate untouched 8x/16x/32x. The completed Q/K LoRA
   improves PG-19 but not generated capability, so likelihood-only adaptation
   is not the solution.

The engineering contract excludes routing, dual tables, cache switching, and
head-specific clocks. Exact Native equivalence, a unique checkpoint-derived
`m_k`, operator-conjugacy tightness, and a universal LM selector are not current
success criteria.
