## Current paper identity

For causal analysis, the finite frequency table is decomposed as

\[
x_k=-\log\omega_k=a+Rz_k.
\]

The paper identifies normalized interior allocation `z` at fixed sampled
support `(a,R)`, shows how it changes the full sin/cos positional basis, and
uses EVQ-Cosh as one zero-learned-parameter closed-form construction on that
coordinate. Geometry, frozen intervention, matched adaptation, and
from-training evidence are separate epistemic layers. EVQ-Cosh is not a
universal LM optimum, and static effective rank is not a behavioural ranker.

## Protocol boundaries

- A pure-`z` claim holds sampled support/base, operator, checkpoint or training
  contract, attention gain, routing, data, rows, and decoder fixed. If another
  component changes, report the combined intervention under its own owner.
- `Geo`, `Native`, `FMRoPE`, anchored EVQ-Cosh, EVQ-Cosh, the repository
  YaRN-style operator, and the MLA wavelength-blend operator retain the locked
  meanings in `AGENTS.md`.
- NLL/PPL, answer-token NLL, teacher-forced retrieval gap, strict generation,
  token F1, exact match, RULER, and causal source-use are distinct endpoints.
- Training seeds, fixed-checkpoint row bootstraps, task rows, configurations,
  and single matched trajectories are different uncertainty units.
- Fixed-support effects are length- and checkpoint-conditional. Target
  retargeting reverses the tested ordering and establishes support-policy
  conditionality; it does not identify a general support-by-allocation law,
  make the coordinates additive, or erase the fixed-support estimand.
