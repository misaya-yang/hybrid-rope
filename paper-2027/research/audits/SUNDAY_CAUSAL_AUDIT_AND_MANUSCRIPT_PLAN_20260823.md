## Executive verdict

The Sunday evidence contains one clean new causal corollary and one useful but
bundled practical policy.

1. **Clean causal corollary:** in frozen mature checkpoints, sampled support,
   attention amplitude, checkpoint, rows, decoder, precision, and hardware are
   fixed while only normalized interior allocation `z` changes. The resulting
   OLMo/Qwen differences support inference-time sensitivity to fixed-support
   `z` under the stated RULER protocols.
2. **Clean mechanism diagnostic:** two paired 151.9M checkpoint pairs reverse
   their runtime-table preference. This supports weights/table compatibility
   and co-adaptation for those checkpoint pairs; it is not a population
   estimate or a replacement for the three-seed exact-range result.
3. **Bundled practical result:** the zero-training Native/s4 policy combines a
   frozen long table, fixed attention amplitude, and observed-request session
   routing. Its system-level outcomes are valid as policy outcomes, but its
   full gain cannot be attributed to `z` alone.
4. **Negative identification result:** the detailed uniqueness profile is not
   necessary under current evidence. A much simpler nearest movement-profile
   ramp matches it on OLMo and Qwen. The contribution is the identified
   allocation axis and model-relative split, not a new interpolation family.

Decision executed: the mature fixed-support `z` corollary and one short
practical-policy consequence were promoted after the owner/code repairs. The
bundle is not presented as a new YaRN, a universally target-free operator, or a
pure-allocation result.

## Architecture Decisions

### D1 — one physical table, several causal coordinates

The implementation consumes one ordered frequency tensor. For causal
accounting, write `x=a+Rz`; this does not imply separate implementation modules.

### D2 — mature corollary, not second method thesis

The new body story, if promoted, is:

> Fixed-support allocation remains consequential in frozen mature
> checkpoints.

The zero-training session policy is a practical corollary, not a second central
method or a new interpolation family.

### D3 — exact Native retention is a construction property

The session policy selects the Native or one frozen s4 profile before prefill
and keeps the route fixed for the KV-cache lifetime. Native parity establishes
short-path retention; it does not explain long-task gains.

### D4 — label-free ramp is a falsification control

The ramp is selected by CPU movement-profile MSE against a frozen derived
profile. It uses no task labels, but the target profile's `p=2` setting was
selected earlier on OLMo core-4. OLMo unseen-9 is the confirmation set.
