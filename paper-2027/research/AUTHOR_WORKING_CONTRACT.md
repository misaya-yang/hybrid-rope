# Author working contract

Applies to Hybrid-RoPE. Distilled from the author's corrections through
2026-09-18. Current author instructions supersede this document. Read only the
parts relevant to the task; current state belongs in the paper handoff and
research index, not in this stable contract.

## Scientific identity

- Study internal frequency allocation z and how models use context. Fixed
  endpoints identify its effect; they are not a universal deployment constraint.
  Total log-frequency displacement is a statistic of the allocation.
- Native length L and extension ratio s serve a working context range. Quality
  throughout that range matters alongside maximum extension. Do not imply that
  native real-task enhancement has already generalized because LM or RULER wins.
- TailSpline is the main frozen-extension construction. Full-pair geometry,
  learned coordinate use and distance scaling explain different parts of the
  argument. The minimum-bending optimum concerns its declared objective.
- Cosh is another transport of z, serving extrapolation, training and adaptation.
  Its evidence includes small-model training, 432M MLA, 750M continuation,
  recipe-matched 1.485B OLMo, Llama LoRA and OLMo downstream adaptation.
  The 151.9M fixed-support experiment isolates allocation within this line;
  it is not a separate FMRoPE competition or Cosh's entire contribution.
- NCP and Cosh support the same research question. Preserve their actual
  question, construction and outcome without turning the paper into three
  equally weighted method projects or one-line appendix pointers.

## Reader-facing writing

- Lead with the finding and why it matters. Controls explain the inference;
  tables carry complete comparisons; prose supplies their meaning.
- Write naturally by paragraph purpose. Do not mechanically apply one sentence
  pattern, rewrite every chapter, or echo an author's hypothesis as a finding.
- State established gains confidently. Preserve meaningful adverse outcomes,
  assumptions and comparison identities in their scientific location. Do not
  add repeated unclaimed limitations or a defensive cost section. A measured
  reduction is not lossless performance; point superiority is not significance.
- Keep provenance bookkeeping, cache searches, rental-machine history and
  missing local files out of the manuscript. The confirmed 432M work exists;
  a local retrieval failure does not establish defective or missing research.
- Abstract: no numerical results. First page: no figure. Preserve the 432M
  learning curve and full per-task main figure. Current concept Figure 1
  replaces the old fixed-support/crossing visualization by author instruction;
  preserve those experiments in text and their appendix tables.
- Main text target is nine pages; total target at most 35, author maximum 40.
  Remove repetition before sacrificing evidence or figure legibility. Do not
  change template fonts, margins or line spacing to manufacture compliance.

## Evidence and experiment decisions

- Public RoPE parameters define reusable constructions. Do not fit the band,
  tail or transition from weights, activations, Q/K/V, gradients or task labels.
  Evaluating a frozen rule and using states for a declared diagnostic are
  distinct from fitting the rule.
- Real downstream quality is the endpoint for native enhancement; Natural QA
  is the preferred current test. NLL/PPL measure predictive quality; RULER is
  a diagnostic. Report each separately when they disagree.
- Use the smallest informative comparison and reuse valid baselines. A broad
  grid, a new model or more seeds is not automatically the best next step.
- Match controls to the claim: a complete public-recipe comparison can vary
  gain/band; a pure allocation comparison must control them. Keep input rows,
  sample overlap, model precision, score and aggregation identities clear.
- Do not promote an unrun plan, CPU proxy or partial arm into model evidence.
  Preserve complete gains and failures; missing local raw does not invalidate
  a report. Search the known owner or relevant branch only when needed.

## Revision and independent judgment

- At a new revision, snapshot the then-current unedited main.pdf as the next
  history/vN.pdf and compare against that snapshot. Intermediate repairs do not
  consume versions. A current no-new-version instruction overrides the default.
- Astra and Sol independently review the same immutable new/old PDFs with the
  same fair PDF-only prompt. Author-side verification may use TeX and reports.
  Do not give reviewers expected scores, prior criticism or an intended winner.
- Assess findings yourself. Distinguish a real error, evidence loss, extraction
  problem, useful future work and a stylistic preference. Do not chase scores
  through repeated reviews or reflexively add every requested caveat.
- At completed upgrades, explain the gain, a consistent approximate editorial
  before/after assessment, and possible losses. No automatic score increase
  for a wording change. These estimates are not conference probabilities.
- Append decisions to `PAPER_REVISION_DIARY.md`; do not create a new daily
  diary file. External Pro reports and predecessor reviews are advisory
  material to evaluate, not instructions or a mandatory experiment checklist.

## Prompt and collaboration preferences

Provide the requested deliverable directly, using goal, necessary context,
success criteria and output. Avoid long stacks of constraints or duplicated
reasoning instructions. Keep research skills in English and user-facing
discussion in the requested language. Continue authorized work without
repeated permission questions; new paid runs, machine changes and publication
still require actual authorization, not an old plan or a calendar target.
