# Integrator assessment before independent review results

Scope: all 16 pages of paper A and all 35 pages of paper B read from PDF text; rendered A pages 3, 7, 9, 13-16 and B pages 2, 7, 9, 18, 20, 31 inspected. No model experiments or raw-row verification. Scientific assessment based on reported evidence. This note precedes reading the two new independent reviews.

Overall ordering under the same present-day, claim-relative standard: B above A. B has stronger causal identification, substantially broader validation, more explicit mathematical statements/proofs, and more complete uncertainty/protocol reporting. A has a compact unifying radix interpretation, a focused method narrative, and explicit transition-boundary sensitivity sweeps. B's conceptual originality should be credited to the identified allocation effect beyond support and movement, not to inventing nonuniform frequency modification. A already retains outer bands while changing intermediate conversion, so it must not be portrayed as having no matched design.

Evidence anchors:
- A p.5 Eq.14 assumes an arithmetic progression of radix exponents; A p.9 supplies cosine-sum and observed attention diagnostics. These are a motivated design and empirical/theoretical supporting analyses, not a theorem of model task optimality. B pp.5-6 Eq.7-9 and pp.17-20 derive the exact finite-grid solution to a declared discrete objective, plus reference-relative phase/response properties. A lack of universal task-optimality theorem is not a missing requirement for B's actual claims.
- B pp.2-3,21-25 separates support, total displacement, spectrum, coordinate assignment, and learning. Three-seed anchored training, support-policy reversal, crossed weights/tables and T-C matched displacement answer different identification questions. A's fixed outer-band comparison is useful but does not provide this full decomposition.
- B p.7 Table1 and p.24: at Llama native 8K, Native/T/P=90.0256/85.1564/82.3923. Relative to the same expansion recipe P, T improves by 2.7641pp; the 4.8692pp reduction relative to Native remains real. Treating native cost as a unique relative disadvantage of T without this P comparison is an evaluation error.
- B p.7 and pp.24-25: Llama 32K T-P=11.72pp on 2600 pairs; T-C=2.0962pp [1.1134,3.0769], while 16K T-C is near zero. Those support a conditional residual shape effect rather than a universal shape optimum.
- A pp.6-8,15-16 reports three main base models (two families), ten Proof-Pile sequences and task benchmarks. B pp.6-9,23-35 contains five frozen families plus 70B NF4, 2600-pair confirmations, 1600-pair NIAH, 46-document LM, source-clustered natural QA, fixed-support native NCP, and scratch/continuation/adaptation studies. These are different research roles, not merely more rows of the same benchmark.
- A pp.14-15 offers useful boundary sweeps. B fixes public band construction across deployments and proves NCP finite-series convexity but has less sensitivity justification for all NCP design choices; this is a local method-completeness consideration, not a refutation of the paper-wide contribution.
- B pp.30,32-35 includes natural/high-factor/native tradeoffs; these delimit deployment scope. Uncertain intervals are reported, not hidden. They should be evaluated at the claim's role and relevance rather than requiring universal per-cell improvement.
- A's first-pass narrative is compact: radix lens -> two recipes -> tests. B's allocation study necessarily spans more roles; main pp.1-3 already define those roles. A concise narrative advantage does not outweigh B's substantive evidence strength or demonstrate that B's narrative is defective.

Reporting issues verified visually in A (not the basis of overall preference): p.3 Eq.7's two printed beta/b exponents are inconsistent; p.7 Table1 Qwen 32K Pro=3.579 versus Y=2.589 contradicts the surrounding across-all-lengths wording as printed; p.15 Table4 has Pro losses to Uni at 16/32K. These may include typographical errors. They do not invalidate A's independent useful results, but reinforce why B should not be subjected to an imaginary flawless-predecessor standard.
