# Devil's Advocate report

## 1. Strongest Counter-Argument

The paper's headline causal claim survives only because the experiment forbids the baseline from doing the one thing the baseline is for. In §2.1 and Fig. 1c, "z is causally identified" means: at support pinned to the 256-token training window, a Cosh interior beats a uniform interior at 512/1K/2K. App. C.1 Table 8 then reveals that once each arm's support is retargeted to the evaluation length, **FMRoPE wins in 3/3 seeds at every OOD length** (+0.060/+0.227/+0.460 nats) — the sign of the paper's central causal claim is an artifact of the "one table serves all lengths" convention, and the words "target-matched"/"retarget" appear nowhere in the body. The same structure governs the mature headline (Table 1, p.6): the 0.56% comparator is uniform-in-exponent interpolation at a single chosen s=4 with no sweep over s, while the 60.47% arm is a non-uniform transport of the same pinned interval — i.e. the 2023 lesson that non-uniform beats plain interpolation, redescribed as discovery of a "third coordinate." Worse, the arm that is supposed to *be* the constructive witness loses to its own control: the movement-profile ramp scores 61.04 > 60.47, and derived-minus-ramp CIs contain zero on both models — and that ramp is defined by minimising MSE **to the derived table** (App. E.2), so its agreement is constructional, not independent. Meanwhile the pre-registered factorial never resolves the rule point (p=0.125, CI ∋ 0), the deformation-matched exponential is numerically better (p=0.071) and indistinguishable from Cosh (p=0.836), and the "zero-search" τ is used in none of the mature protocols (0.53×–2× deviations). The residual content is: non-uniform allocation helps when support is frozen; shape is undetermined; the paper's own diagnostic (r₂) predicts nothing, since the cell where r₂ doubles is the one with PPL 76.20.

**"So what?" test.** If a reviewer accepts everything, what changes tomorrow is: when you cannot or will not extend a RoPE table's support, redistribute its K pairs non-uniformly instead of log-uniformly, and diagnose redundancy with block-whitened c_{ων} instead of a cosine slice. That delta is real but narrow — it is a better-documented version of advice already in the NTK/YaRN/LongRoPE lineage, delivered via a construction (EVQ-Cosh) that the paper's own factorial cannot distinguish from a shape-matched exponential and that its own headline does not use. The actionable version of the headline is the routed Native/long policy of App. E.2, which reinstates exactly the support choice the paper presents as orthogonal. On the "so what" axis this is a well-controlled workshop result wearing a paradigm-signature title.

## 2. Issue list

**DA-1 — CRITICAL [LOGIC][CHERRY-PICKING]. Anchor:** §2.1 (p.3, lines 20–22: "z is a separately identifiable coordinate"), Fig. 1c caption (p.2), vs App. C.1 Table 8 / Fig. 4 (pp.22–23): target-matched contrasts +0.060±0.007 / +0.227±0.102 / +0.460±0.218, "FMRoPE is favoured by 3/3 seeds at every OOD length."
The identification study's effect direction is conditional on an evaluation convention the body never names. Table 8's own "Reading" paragraph admits the fixed column isolates allocation *when one table serves multiple lengths*, and that the two columns "do not identify a support-by-allocation interaction law." The abstract ("identifying z during co-adaptation") and contributions (i) ("causally identify its behavioural effect … fixing every scalar-base degree of freedom") carry no qualifier. This is not a nuance: it is the difference between "z is a controllable determinant" and "z is what you tune when you refuse to extend the window."
**Rebuttal evidence:** a support × allocation design with each arm swept over s and paired best-of comparisons; or evidence that the reversal is an artifact of the anchored grid rather than of allocation.

**DA-2 — CRITICAL [EVIDENCE][ALTERNATIVE-EXPLANATION]. Anchor:** Table 1 (p.6), §4.1 lines 17–30; §3.1 Eq. (2) (p.4); App. E.2 lines 88–105.
No control for scalar support in the frozen headline. "Same-support log-linear" is one point in the s-family; the lower block therefore measures "non-uniform vs uniform at s=4," and the abstract converts that into "+0.56% → 60.47% by changing only z." Compounding: (i) the ramp — explicitly "the discrete YaRN-family linear ramp" — *outperforms* the derived arm (61.04 vs 60.47) with derived-minus-ramp CIs containing zero on both models; (ii) that ramp is fit to the derived arm by minimising movement-profile MSE, so its success is guaranteed by construction and cannot corroborate it; (iii) the "+52.54 over official Transformers YaRN" is confounded by endpoint convention, since the paper's own YaRN-family member with pinned endpoints reaches 61.04.
**Rebuttal evidence:** log-linear and NTK-style (unpinned) arms swept over s with the derived advantage surviving a paired CI, plus an independent control not defined by reference to the treatment.

**DA-3 — CRITICAL [CONFIRMATION-BIAS][CHERRY-PICKING]. Anchor:** Table 7 (p.23) vs §2.1 body (p.5): "the exponential control rules out a generic 'any deformation works' explanation."
Pre-specified factorial: rule (1.00×) − Geo = −0.00988, CI [−0.021, 0.002], 7/12, **p=0.125**; matched-exponential − Geo = −0.01062, [−0.021,−0.001], 9/12, p=0.071; rule − exponential = **+0.00074**, p=0.836. Only the 1.25× multiplier resolves (p=0.027), unadjusted across four contrasts, and the effect is a ~1% equivalent-PPL shift (498.68 → 492.69). The body sentence asserts the opposite of its own table: a different deformation family reproduces the gain, so shape-specificity is unsupported. App. A.8 (p.18) honestly caps the operating rule at **7/9 configuration means, 18/27 seed pairs**, while the abstract elevates the construct to a "closed-form … witness" and a rule.
**Rebuttal evidence:** re-register the shape contrast (Cosh vs deformation-matched exponential) as primary and win it; otherwise drop "rule"/"witness."

**DA-4 — MAJOR [EVIDENCE][LOGIC]. Anchor:** §3.3 + Table 1 (p.5); §3.1 lines 61–62; §3.4 lines 197–202; App. A.5 Table 3 (p.15).
The load-bearing negative result anti-predicts behaviour: the swap where r₂ goes 4.57 → 12.54 is the PPL 7.14 → 76.20 cell, and the caption concedes "the worst cell is also the one whose static r₂ improves most." If r₂ is the budget, the budget is uncorrelated with the outcome the title promises to explain. Separately, the witness's declared validation runs under the cosine-feature kernel K_cos — the phase-fixing slice §3.1 discredits — because "the exact canonical-correlation objective is oscillatory." No link is ever established between surrogate and metric. And the validation's margins shrink with scale (−92% at L=128 → −24% at L=4096; video Shannon ranks 2.8→3.9, 2.0→2.5), i.e. largest exactly where extrapolation is not the problem.

**DA-5 — MAJOR [OVERGENERALIZATION]. Anchor:** Thm 1 + proof (App. A.1 lines 38–47); Prop 2 (p.5); Thm 3 uniqueness language (§3.4, App. A.4 line 199); Thm 4 obstruction (App. A.6); App. A.7 self-consistency.

## 3. Ignored alternative explanations / paths

- **(a) Effective-range shortening.** Everything is consistent with: what matters is usable wavelength range, and z is how you move range when range is forbidden. App. B.5 says so — EVQ "thins the slow band and shifts intermediate channels toward shorter wavelengths, which reduces mid-to-long distance aliasing" — with no test against a swept interpolation baseline. Table 8's target-matched column is direct evidence, unengaged in the body.
- **(b) Amplitude rescaling — partially controlled, gap remains.** The log-linear arm does share 1+0.1 ln 4 and scores 0.56, so amplitude alone cannot produce the headline; I withdraw the naive version of this attack. What is missing is Native-z-with-amplitude and derived-z-without-amplitude, which would separate "which pairs move" from "amplitude plus movement."
- **(c) Statistics-derived adaptation dressed as geometry.** m_k consumes the checkpoint's own residual uniqueness energies under an undefined distance measure; "geometry" here is per-checkpoint adaptive patching, closer to a learned-statistics correction than to the budget principle the title names.
- **(d) Synthetic-suite positional bias.** All headline endpoints are RULER/NIAH/Qasper-family, 20 rows per task, greedy decode.
- **(e) Undertrained-adaptation confound** (DA-9): §4.2's low-rank numbers may reflect recipe limits rather than persistence of z.
- **(f) Video DiT.** A 32→128-frame synthetic oscillation task is the setting where reducing slow-channel phase ambiguity trivially helps; one seed, τ chosen post hoc, no second architecture.
- **(g) Path not taken:** the manuscript never reports what LeRoPE's learned tables look like in z-coordinates. Given that §2 and §5 both concede learned tables explore the same coordinate, mapping published learned profiles onto Eq. (1) would have been the cheapest possible corroboration of the construct's existence outside the authors' own surrogate.

## 4. Missing stakeholder perspectives

- **Deployment engineers.** "Actionable after pretraining … no learned parameters or training tokens" is silent on the two costs that decide adoption: a 4× table at 16K implies ~4× KV cache and attention compute (inference cost is never mentioned anywhere in the manuscript), and a 30–45 RULER-point in-window tax (Table 9). Engineers installing the recommended fixed table without the App. E.2 router regress on their primary workload.
- **Reproduction community.** The 60.47% figure depends on an undefined "split rule" and "Native causal-distance measure," and on a τ partly chosen from another experiment's realised value.
- **Evaluation methodology.** 180 synthetic rows and 40/10-case retrieval counts license a claim about how RoPE fundamentally works; the less flattering suite-level numbers sit in App. D/Table 9, and the headline task set is labelled "confirmation-only."
- **Prior-work authors / novelty ledger.** LeRoPE is credited with the same design direction; frequency-design work in the bibliography goes undiscussed; main.tex lines 20–21 preserve an ICML-formatted fallback venue, indicating the "what prior work changes" taxonomy has been curated across at least one revision cycle without stating what was removed.
- **Readers of the AI-use statement (p.8).** Disclosure notes generative tools used to "develop conceptual frameworks and mathematical claims," "propose and refine hypotheses," and "suggest experimental parameters." Compliant and honest, but combined with a construct invented for the paper, a surrogate invented for the paper, and a validation performed under a metric the theory discredits, it raises the prior that the framing is narrative-shape rather than discovery-shape. Calibration input, not a defect.

## 5. Observations — non-defects

- **Statistical hygiene above venue norm.** Units declared per result; the exact-range matrix complete (180/180 + 12/12); sign-flip and bootstrap procedures stated; multiple-comparison exposure volunteered ("unadjusted across contrasts"); "the p-value is not the probability that the effect is zero." Few submissions undermine their own primary table this candidly.
- **Negative results that cut against the paper are kept and used.** Table 1 disciplines the geometry→behaviour story; Lemma 5 concedes full in-window rank coexists with total out-of-window aliasing; §3.4 states plainly that geometry and surrogate "play distinct roles."
- **Endpoint discipline.** NLL / PPL / token-F1 / official-scorer macro / strict AR exact / NLL-gap retrieval / MSE are never pooled; Table 4 maps each claim to its own intervention, unit and endpoint; App. C.1 explicitly refuses the interaction-law inference.
- **Grid-convention honesty** (§3.1 lines 24–30, App. C.1, Table 10): naming anchored vs midpoint grids as different support embeddings of one shape, instead of hiding the affine normalisation, is unusually careful.
- **Genuine technical contributions** independent of the framing: the phase-invariance proof for c_{ων}, the closed-form cross-Gram, and Prop. 2's softmax-metric refinement span{Δ − E_pΔ, Δ² − E_pΔ²}.
- The position-preserving attention-blocking ablation on 8B (−0.0095 vs +1.5055) and the bitwise-verified frozen-substrate controls in §4.2 are well-designed causal probes of the intended mechanism.

## 6. Falsification criteria

**DA-1.** Withdrawn if (i) each arm's support is swept and anchored EVQ beats each arm's own best geometric configuration in ≥2/3 seeds at ≥2/3 OOD lengths with paired CIs excluding zero, and (ii) the Table 8 reversal is shown not to be an artifact of the anchored grid (deployed midpoint Cosh interior at matched target support). Otherwise the body (abstract, §2.1, contributions, Fig. 1c) must state the direction's dependence on the single-table convention.

**DA-2.** Withdrawn if no uniform-log-linear or NTK-style (unpinned-fast-end) arm over s ∈ {2,4,8,16} reaches within the derived arm's paired CI on the same 180 rows, **and** the derived arm beats the ramp fitted to it on held-out rows — e.g. against an independently specified profile such as YaRN's own α/β ramp with endpoints pinned.

**DA-3.** Withdrawn if the shape-specific contrast (anchored Cosh rule vs deformation-matched exponential) is pre-registered as primary and wins with a CI excluding zero on a larger configuration population at the 1.00× multiplier, and either the 1.25× result is multiplicity-adjusted or dropped, and a rule-fixed-τ run matches the best per-protocol τ within CI.

**DA-4/DA-5.** DA-4's CRITICAL framing falls if either (a) a bound links C_app to the phase-invariant block-whitened Gram, or (b) r₂/c̄ predicts the sign of the trained contrast across the 12 factorial configurations with a CI excluding chance. If only the framing is at fault, both downgrade to MAJOR with a requested retitle; DA-5 falls if the identity in Thm 1 is restated as bookkeeping and the "23 pairs" count as arithmetic.

**DA-6.** Withdrawn when a written definition of the causal-distance measure and the split rule, plus the stated supplement generators, regenerate Table 1's 60.47/61.04 from the public checkpoint in a reviewer-run log.

**DA-8/DA-9.** DA-8 falls if any mature claim gains a second independently trained arm with seed as unit. DA-9 falls if frozen and adapted arms are reported on one shared table (same checkpoint, length, scorer) with the ~9× gap explained, or if the adaptation route is removed from the identification narrative.

**DA-10.** Withdrawn if the in-window tax appears in the abstract/§4.1 and the deployment recommendation names the two-table routing requirement as load-bearing.
