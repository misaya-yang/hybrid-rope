# Reviewer report — Seat EIC: Journal-Fit Reviewer

## 1. Paper summary (as this reviewer understands it)

The paper argues that a finite RoPE table is specified by three coordinates, not one: writing $x_k=-\log\omega_k=a+Rz_k$ (Eq. 1, §1, 01_intro.tex:8-14), the scalar base fixes only support $(a,R)$ under the geometric rule $z_k=k/(K-1)$, while the interior allocation $z$ — where the fixed $K=\drot/2$ channels sit inside the pinned interval — is an independent design coordinate. The motivating observation is geometric: at standard settings ($L{=}4096$, $b{=}5{\times}10^5$, $K{=}64$), 23 pairs with $\omega L\le 1$ occupy 46 nominal dimensions but have Rényi-2 effective rank 2.00, so over a third of the rotary budget buys nearly duplicate slow positional directions.

Three things are delivered. First, a causal identification: a paired three-seed 151.9M training control pins architecture, init, data order, optimiser, token budget, both sampled endpoints, and log-span, so only the $K{-}2=30$ interior frequencies move (anchored EVQ-Cosh at $\tau{=}4$ vs FMRoPE at training base 256); all seeds favour the reallocated table at every OOD length (§2.1, Table in App. A.5). Second, an exact phase-invariant geometry: a spectral-budget identity $r_2=2K/[1+(K{-}1)\bar c]$ (Thm 1), a fourth-order slow-collapse law (Prop 2), a co-adaptation crossing experiment showing the table is a training-time coordinate system (Table 1: swapping tables raises $r_2$ from 4.57 to 12.54 while PPL goes 7.14→76.20), and a transplant obstruction (Thm 3). Third, EVQ-Cosh: a closed-form, zero-learned-parameter allocation derived as the unique optimum of a stated convex surrogate (§3.4, Thm 4), deployed as a "witness" across three lifecycle routes — frozen checkpoint (OLMo 16K RULER 0.56%→60.47% at matched support), matched LoRA (1.485B, 8B), and from-training continuation up to 1.485B, plus a video DiT.

This is an analysis/mechanism paper with an attached construction, not a method paper: it repeatedly disclaims that geometry diagnoses while interventions establish behaviour, and positions EVQ-Cosh as a witness rather than the recommended product.

## 2. Strengths

- **S1 — A genuinely clarifying conceptual object.** The $(a,R,z)$ decomposition turns a scattered positional-encoding literature into a coordinate taxonomy, and the taxonomy is operational rather than rhetorical: related work actually re-reads FMRoPE as $(a,R)$ transport, LeRoPE/AdaRoPE as learned $z$, YaRN/LongRoPE as range transport, and DAPE/FIRE/RePo as leaving the family entirely (02_related.tex:4-58). The identity "a second table with the same support and different interior $z$ cannot be represented by a different scalar base" (03_theory.tex:29-32) is exact and does the work. This is a takeaway a broad ICLR audience can reuse.
- **S2 — Unusually disciplined causal design.** The 151.9M control enumerates and pins every scalar-base degree of freedom, including micro-batch/accumulation geometry per seed pair (02_identification.tex:8-15; a5_identification.tex:7-26), and the deployment-relevant condition (one table serving many lengths) is explicit. The 12-configuration factorial adds a deformation-matched exponential control that is the right way to test "non-geometric shape" as such (a5_identification.tex:122-135).
- **S3 — The paper polices its own strongest confusion.** Table 1 (table_coadapt.tex:14-24) is the most important exhibit in the paper: better static geometry plus wrong weights = catastrophic failure. Placing it in §3.3, before the experiments, prevents the "effective rank predicts performance" misreading, and the transplant-obstruction scope note ("fixed, position-independent, invertible compensation"; a1_proofs.tex:159-161) is appropriately narrow.
- **S4 — The exact theory is exactly right and checkable.** Theorem 1's identity (03_theory.tex:86-95) is a clean two-line result; the collapse law with the $\frac{19}{12600}(x^2-y^2)^2$ fourth-order deficit (03_theory.tex:113-124) and the Fourier-alias lemma separating rank from coverage (a1_proofs.tex:111-127) go beyond folklore about "low frequencies are slow."
- **S5 — Convergent evidence across the model lifecycle.** The zero-training frozen intervention (§4.1), selective Q/K LoRA and 8B causal source-use (§4.2), from-training 750M/1.485B crossover and K=16 MLA (§4.3), and the DiT all move on the same axis, with the recurring in-window/long-length crossover identified and then mechanistically explained for the one regime that breaks the pattern (05_discussion.tex:20-30).
- **S6 — Honest disclosure of non-wins.** The target-matched reversal (a5_identification.tex:94-117), the exponential indistinguishability (table_m4.tex:26: $+0.00074$, $p{=}0.836$), the ramp tying the derived table, and the CI conditioning caveats (tab:frozen-fixed-support caption; a6_mature_scale.tex:49-54) are all reported against interest. This raises my confidence in the positive claims.
- **S7 — Compliance and reproducibility posture.** Body fits the page limit with references exempt (main.tex:95-107); the reproducibility statement names grids, endpoint conventions, and per-result seed scope (07_reproducibility.tex), and the evidence-map table (a2_experiment_details.tex:18-39) is the kind of artifact ICLR replication committees wish every submission had.

## 3. Weaknesses

- **W1 [MAJOR] — The identification section's headline omits its own scope condition.** §2.1 states "Every training seed favours anchored EVQ at every OOD length" and concludes "$z$ is a separately identifiable coordinate" (02_identification.tex:16-22), and the abstract repeats the claim (00_abstract.tex:10-12). But App. A.5's second column shows that under the target-matched policy, FMRoPE is favoured by 3/3 seeds at every OOD length (+0.060/+0.227/+0.460 NLL; a5_identification.tex:84-109). The identification claim survives (either way, $z$ changes behaviour), but the *direction* of benefit is policy-conditional, and Fig. 1c — which a reader uses to anchor the whole paper — shows only panel (a). Fix: one sentence in §2.1 and a clause in the Fig. 1c caption stating that per-length support retargeting reverses the tested ordering; this preempts the appendix "gotcha" and sharpens the actual lesson (fixed-support $z$ matters most when one table serves many lengths, which is the deployment case).
- **W2 [MAJOR] — Contribution (iii) undercuts itself: the paper's own controls show the specific Cosh allocation is not behaviourally load-bearing.** The deformation-matched exponential ties the EVQ rule (table_m4.tex:26, p=0.836); the label-free movement-profile ramp ties or exceeds the derived table on both frozen models (04_experiments.tex:51-52; a6_mature_scale.tex:92-105 "leaves profile-specific ordering unresolved"). What survives is "some non-geometric interior allocation helps," for which closed-formity is an analytic convenience, not an empirical finding. As stated — "EVQ-Cosh as closed-form witness" — contribution (iii) is coherent with the negative results (the paper says "witness," not "winner"), but then the title-level "spectral budget" story needs the construction to do more work than "an example of $z$-movement." Fix: either (a) explicitly demote (iii) in the contributions list to "an analytic instantiation sufficient to demonstrate the axis," and delete the "deployment" framing in §4.1, or (b) provide one experiment where the closed form earns its keep — e.g., the $c{=}1$, $\tau=c\,\dhd/\sqrt{\Ltr}$ rule (a1_proofs.tex:346-371) predicting out-of-protocol $\tau$ choices better than a search of equal budget at 750M/1.485B scale.
- **W3 [MAJOR] — First-pass legibility of the 8-page body.** Three compounding problems: (i) the acronym EVQ is never expanded anywhere in the paper (00_abstract.tex:13, 03_theory.tex:230) — a reader cannot decode the method's name, which for a headline construction is a real presentation defect; (ii) §2 uses "anchored EVQ ($\tau=4$)", "Cosh", and "the deployed Cosh table's $z$" (02_identification.tex:11-15) before the Cosh density, $\tau$, and anchor/embedding machinery exist (defined only in §3.4 and App. A.5), and the related-work taxonomy that §1 points forward to (§1.3, 01_intro.tex:28-32) sits after §2; (iii) arm naming is dense without a glossary — Geo, Native, FMRoPE, anchored EVQ, derived, same-support log-linear, movement-profile ramp, long profile, session policy (04_experiments.tex, a6). Fix: expand the acronym at first use; move the four-family classification sentence from §2 Related to §1 (or a 3-line recap in §1); add a compact "arm dictionary" box or table; and add one forward-pointer sentence in §2.1 saying the Cosh family and anchoring are defined in §3.4.
- **W4 [MAJOR] — The zero-training "deployment" claim has one baseline.** §4.1's "Zero-training deployment" paragraph and the Qasper/2Wiki/natural-context numbers (04_experiments.tex:57-77) benchmark only against official Transformers YaRN, while the tuning-free extension literature is large (SelfExtend, PoSE, CLEX are in the bib list but never engaged in the body's positioning). The Qwen evidence is 4 development tasks × 20 rows (a6_mature_scale.tex:96-99). Fix: either relabel these rows as "controlled zero-training instantiations" and drop deployment language, or add at least two non-YaRN tuning-free baselines on RULER and one full-length non-synthetic benchmark. This matters for (g)-level positioning: a reviewer expecting a method will otherwise check the wrong baselines.
- **W5 [MINOR] — The abstract's marquee number invites the trivial-baseline reading.** "Raises nine-task 16K RULER from 0.56% to 60.47%" (00_abstract.tex:7-9) — but Native scores 0.00% and YaRN 7.94% at that length on the same 4K-window model (tab:frozen-fixed-support:45-48), so the comparison baseline is nearly broken by construction. The point is precisely that support-matched geometric spacing fails almost as hard as no extension, which is a finding — but the abstract should carry YaRN's 7.94% as the third anchor so the reader calibrates what "60.47%" is competing against.
- **W6 [MINOR] — Arithmetic presentation nits.** "Derived minus same-support log-linear is +59.92 points" (04_experiments.tex:23) vs the table's 60.47−0.56 = 59.91; "+52.54" vs 60.47−7.94 = 52.53. If computed from unrounded per-task macros, say so in the sentence.
- **W7 [MINOR] — The title's "budget" over-promises predictiveness relative to §3.3.** The budget identity is an availability statement ($\operatorname{tr}\Gamma=2K$ fixed; 03_theory.tex:96-102), and Table 1 shows $r_2$ can improve while perplexity collapses. §3 opens with this discipline (03_theory.tex:4-10), but the title plus the intro's budget metaphor (01_intro.tex:25) leaves two pages in which "spectral budget" reads as a performance predictor. Fix: add one clause to the intro ("the budget bounds what the basis can supply; behaviour is settled by training") at 01_intro.tex:26.
- **W8 [MINOR] — Scale claims rest on single matched pairs.** The evidence map itself records "one matched pair each" for 750M/1.485B, "one trained pair" for 8B, "1 training seed" for the DiT (a2_experiment_details.tex:27-38), yet §4.3 writes "repeat the in-window/long-length crossover through 1.485B" (04_experiments.tex:129-130). The hedge "in the tested protocols" appears in contributions (01_intro.tex:79-80) but not at §4.3's headline sentences. Fix: one compute-budget sentence (total GPU-hours or tokens) and "consistent direction" in place of "persistence" where n=1 pair.
- **W9 [MINOR] — §3.1 carries machinery for an experiment that arrives in §4.1.** The movement-profile construction (Eq. 2, 03_theory.tex:34-51) introduces $\widetilde U_k$ residual-energy definitions before the reader knows the derived/ramp arms live in the frozen OLMo experiment; the "second construction on the same $z$ coordinate" framing is load-bearing for the paper's honesty but hard to follow in theory-first presentation. Fix: move Eq. 2 and its two defining sentences into §4.1 (or label "instantiated in §4.1").

## 4. Questions for authors

- **Q1** — Does the target-matched reversal (App. A.5, panel b) have an analogue in the frozen OLMo experiment: if the same-support log-linear control is allowed per-length support retargeting at evaluation, does it close any of the 59.9-point gap, or is the reversal confined to the 256-token-training regime?
- **Q2** — Can you compute $r_2$ / $\bar c$ for the three matched-support frozen arms (log-linear, ramp, derived) at the evaluation length and show whether the static budget identity *orders* the three trained outcomes? That would convert Theorem 1 from diagnosis to (partial) prediction within one protocol, which the paper currently never demonstrates for any trained model.
- **Q3** — Given the ramp and the exponential both tie the Cosh allocation, what claim would survive if the deformation-matched exponential had been the construction throughout? Put differently: is there any tested protocol where EVQ-Cosh *beats* the label-free ramp with a CI excluding zero?
- **Q4** — The realised $\tau$ values span 4 (151.9M), 2.83/1.5 (DiT), 2 (OLMo-2), 1.5 (750M), 1.414 (MLA/LLaMA), with the $c{=}1$ rule applying only to "standard full-RoPE text" (a1_proofs.tex:346-355). How much of the cross-protocol effect is attributed to $\tau$ mis-specification risk, and can the 99-run study (a1_proofs.tex:384-397) be used to estimate a falsifiable band for $c$?
- **Q5** — What does "EVQ" stand for? Please expand it wherever the name first appears, including in the abstract.

## 5. Positioning and missing-comparison checks

The four-family classification (realised phases / support $(a,R)$ / interior $z$ / larger operator) is convincing against the families that matter: FMRoPE is handled both conceptually and *empirically as the control arm* (02_identification.tex:11-12) — the strongest possible separation for a rival coordinate story. LeRoPE/AdaRoPE (learned $z$), GRAPE/Selective-RoPE (operator generalisation), DAPE/FIRE/FoPE/RePo (learned operators), and the data-shapes theory (wu2026datashapes, correctly distinguished as selecting among supplied geometric channels; 02_related.tex:20-24) are all placed. The learned-table comparator exists in App. A.5 with an LR sweep (table_pe_dominant.tex). Two gaps at submission-time positioning: (i) LongRoPE-2/search-based non-geometric allocations are never compared at matched support, even though a search-derived allocation is the natural rival to a closed-form one given W2; (ii) the tuning-free deployment rows face only YaRN (W4). Neither gap changes the identification claim, but both change what a practitioner takes from the paper.

## 6. Claim-to-evidence fit assessment

Title claim: supported in the exact geometric sense ("a finite table has a budget," Theorem 1) and supported behaviourally by interventions, but the paper itself proves the budget is *not* a trained-behaviour predictor (Table 1), and the abstract/title do not carry that distinction — the body repairs what the front matter slightly overstates. Contribution (i) is well matched by the fixed-support control, modulo the W1 reversal disclosure. Contribution (ii) is over-delivered: four exact results, all scoped honestly. Contribution (iii) is the loose end: the witness role is clearly stated, but the paper's own ramp/exponential ties mean the evidence supports "interior allocation is a controllable coordinate" more strongly than it supports the Cosh construction — which the title and abstract implicitly elevate by naming EVQ-Cosh as the paper's constructive product. As three contributions, they read as one paper: identification of an axis, geometry of what the axis can do, construction on the axis — the coherence is real but depends on accepting "witness, not winner," which §4.1's "zero-training deployment" language occasionally forgets. The paper is consistently an analysis/mechanism paper; a reviewer primed by the abstract's 0.56→60.47 number to expect a method will be disappointed, and the discussion's closing "design object" paragraph (05_discussion.tex:38-44) is the right frame for the whole submission.

## 8. One-sentence recommendation to the area chair

Accept in the weak-accept direction contingent on the authors surfacing the support-retargeting reversal into §2.1, disambiguating the witness-not-winner status of EVQ-Cosh, and either demoting the "zero-training deployment" framing to a single-baseline control or adding two tuning-free baselines — because the identification of $z$ as a third finite-RoPE coordinate is a genuinely reusable contribution, but only if the paper's front matter makes the same careful scope distinctions its appendix already makes.
