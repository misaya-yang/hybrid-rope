# Reviewer report — Seat R1: Methodology Reviewer

## 1. Paper summary

The manuscript decomposes a finite RoPE table into support location $a$, log-span $R$, and interior allocation $z$ (Eq. 1), argues that geometric spacing wastes rotary pairs on a near-collinear slow subspace (exact Rényi-2 budget identity, Prop. collapse), and identifies $z$ as a causal design coordinate through three statistically distinct routes: (i) a paired three-seed 151.9M training control in which both arms pin sampled extrema and log-span so only the 30 interior frequencies move; (ii) frozen-checkpoint transplant experiments on OLMo/Qwen in which same-support log-linear, derived-residual-energy, and movement-profile-ramp tables share checkpoint, endpoints, amplitude, rows, and decoder; and (iii) matched low-rank and from-training protocols at 750M–8B plus a single-seed video DiT. EVQ-Cosh is offered as a closed-form constructive witness (inverse-CDF quantiles of a convex surrogate, Thm. ODE), with an operating rule $\tau = c\,d_{\rm head}/\sqrt{L_{\rm train}}$, $c=1$ declared as a pre-fixed zero-search convention.

Methodologically, the paper is unusually disciplined about endpoint separation (NLL vs. PPL vs. task-F1 vs. exact-match are kept disjoint; App. evidence-map table assigns unit and endpoint per experiment) and about what each bootstrap conditions on (e.g., "intervals condition on the fixed checkpoint and task set"). The weaknesses I identify are concentrated in (a) the scope language of the headline three-seed identification relative to its own appendix's reversal result, (b) τ-selection and pre-specification in the mature protocols versus the "zero-search" narrative, (c) the non-significant formula-point contrast in the factorial, and (d) evaluation-subset selection and small eval samples behind the frozen-checkpoint headline.

## 2. Strengths

- **S1.** Correct and explicit choice of statistical unit in the factorial: the configuration is the unit, seed is collapsed within configuration before a 12-unit bootstrap and an exact $2^{12}$ sign-flip test, and the caption states p-values are unadjusted across contrasts (App. Sec. identification-details, "Metric" ¶; Table `table_m4` caption, lines 8–14). This is the honest design and it is disclosed.
- **S2.** Conditioning language on bootstrap intervals is explicit and, as far as I can verify, never exceeded: frozen-table caption ("paired intervals condition on the fixed checkpoint and task set, not model- or task-population uncertainty", Sec. 4.1 Table 2), OLMo RULER (App. mature-scale lines 52–54), and the 1.485B from-init figure caption (App. mature lines 74–77) all state what the intervals do not cover.
- **S3.** All internal arithmetic I could reconstruct checks out: 151.9M budget $7{,}629\times256\times256 = 499{,}974{,}144$ tokens (App. identification lines 18–22); the five factorial Δ values and rule−exponential difference match the absolute NLLs to 5 decimals; equivalent-PPL conversions match $\exp(\bar N)$; the co-adapt main effects $+0.599/-0.597$ and interaction $-3.537$ reproduce exactly from the log-PPL cells of Table `table_coadapt`; the 23-pair slow count at $L{=}4096,b{=}5{\times}10^5,K{=}64$ is correct ($k\ge 41$); DiT percentage deltas match Table `table_dit-h2h` cells; and $138.8\to95.6$ is $-31.1\%$.
- **S4.** PPL conventions are explicitly defined ("$\exp(\mathrm{mean\ NLL})$, never an arithmetic mean of raw PPL", App. identification, Metric ¶) and the weighted OOD NLL formula is fully specified.
- **S5.** The target-matched reversal is reported against the authors' own interest in the appendix ("FMRoPE is favoured by 3/3 seeds at every OOD length", App. identification lines 106–108), together with an explicit statement that the columns do not identify a support×allocation interaction law (lines 111–117).
- **S6.** Provenance/pinning: pinned FineWeb-Edu and tokenizer revisions (App. identification lines 30–36), generator seeds recorded (App. mature lines 34–36), curated manifest and runner script named for the 99-run study (App. proofs, λ-CV section lines 392–395).
- **S7.** Design-stage controls in the mature studies are genuinely matched: bitwise-verified frozen V/O inheritance, identical trainable parameter counts, identical 1:1:2 phase-exposure mixture (App. mature lines 28–44), and the "physical-memory cap, not phase-free 4K training" disclosure (lines 17–19) is exactly the kind of honesty reviewers should reward.

## 3. Weaknesses

**W1. [MAJOR] The headline identification claim is conditional on a deployment policy, and the reversal is disclosed only in the appendix.** The abstract ("improves every tested OOD length in every seed", lines 10–12), intro (Sec. 1 "The anchored EVQ minus FMRoPE…" ¶; Fig. 1c caption "moving only 30 interiors improves every tested OOD length in all three seeds"), and Sec. 2 all state the per-seed win without the qualifier that under the *target-matched* policy — retargeting each arm's support to the evaluation length, which is precisely what deployed range-extension methods do — FMRoPE wins in 3/3 seeds at every OOD length, with larger magnitudes (+0.46±0.22 at 2K) than the fixed-range wins (App. Table 4 right block; App. identification lines 106–117). "Fixed-support" in the abstract technically carries the condition, but a reader of the body will take the identification as "interior allocation helps OOD," when the evidence establishes the narrower claim "with one table serving all lengths, allocation matters; when you retarget support to the test length, support dominates and the ordering reverses." *Fix:* state the reversal in Sec. 2 in one sentence and mirror it in the abstract (e.g., "at pinned training support"), or provide at least one protocol (the 1.485B LoRA or frozen routes partially serve here) that shows the effect survives support retargeting to the eval length.

**W2. [MAJOR] Several mature protocols use τ values that are not outputs of the stated zero-search rule, and the rule's own $L$ is ambiguous for phase-exposed training.** Eq. (5) with $c=1$ gives τ=4 for the 151.9M control ($64/\sqrt{256}$), consistent. But: the 750M continuation uses "midpoint quantisation with τ=1.5" (App. experiment-details lines 86–87) where the rule gives 1.0 at a 4K window (and 1.41 at a 2K parent); OLMo-2 1.485B arms use "endpoint grid $u_k=k/K$ with τ=2" (App. mature line 9) — neither grid nor value matches the deployed midpoint rule at any stated $L$; LLaMA uses τ=1.414 (line 129), which equals $64/\sqrt{2048}$, though the protocol's physical cap is 8K with target-16K phase exposure. The reproducibility statement carves out only "the separately fixed MLA and video settings" (lines 7–8), implicitly claiming the others follow the rule. There is also an under-specified degree of freedom: what is $L_{\rm train}$ when training is physically capped but explicitly position-ID-phase-exposed? *Fix:* give, per protocol, the $(c, L, u_k)$ triple and whether τ was rule-generated or separately fixed; state the tie-break for $L$ under phase-exposure protocols.

**W3. [MAJOR] The pre-specified formula-point contrast is not significant, and the only significant factorial arm is the off-rule 1.25× multiplier.** Table `table_m4`: EVQ rule − Geo = −0.00988, CI [−0.021, 0.002] (includes 0), p=0.125, 7/12 configurations; only 1.25× (p=0.027, unadjusted) clears 0.05; boundary-τ arms point inward at both extremes (App. identification lines 154–157), which is consistent with the formula sitting near a boundary of its own basin. With 5 contrasts and no adjustment, 0.027 is marginal even before multiplicity. The body's language is mostly disciplined ("direction", "useful zero-search prior"), but the aggregate narrative — a uniquely optimal closed-form witness whose downstream protocols all use formula-family τ and all favor EVQ — outruns what the identification-grade factorial shows for the formula point. *Fix/evidence:* either report a pre-specified contrast family (which of the five contrasts is confirmatory vs. exploratory, and an adjusted p for the claim-bearing one), or soften the remaining places where the rule point is the referent of "EVQ beats Geo" (e.g., the 99-run "beats … in 7/9 configuration means", App. proofs lines 384–390, has no test at all).

**W4. [MAJOR] The frozen-checkpoint headline rests on an undocumented nine-task subset, a single extension factor, and an unmapped derived→movement transform.** Sec. 4.1 and Table 2 report "nine confirmation-only tasks" / "unseen-nine" at 16K (0.56% → 60.47%), and Qwen "core-four". The manuscript never lists the nine tasks, states what the other four RULER families scored, or documents the selection rule that made nine "confirmation-only" (App. mature lines 96–99). A +59.9-point headline with CI [54.88, 64.80] is not fragile, but task-subset selection is a real risk when the selection documentation is absent — especially since the Qwen derived-minus-log-linear interval [0.25, 17.50] barely clears zero. Similarly, the movement map $m_k=(1-\widetilde U_k)^2$ (Sec. 3.1 lines 46–49) is one of infinitely many monotone maps, with no sensitivity analysis, and the ramp is fit to the derived table, so derived vs. ramp is corroboration of the movement profile, not an independent allocation (the paper concedes the latter, lines 104–105, which is good). *Fix:* add the task list, per-task scores for all 13/4 families, and the freeze artifact (date/hash) supporting "frozen before evaluation"; report at least one alternative exponent in the movement map or one alternative $s\in\{2,4,8\}$.

**W5. [MINOR] The 151.9M control's evaluation sample is very small and carries no uncertainty interval.** The metric is "paired final-128-token teacher-forced NLL over the 32 frozen anchors" (App. identification lines 61–62) — 4,096 evaluated tokens per length per arm — with only across-seed SD reported and no interval at all in the body. With n=3 seeds, 3/3 agreement is p=0.25 by sign; the claim leans on magnitude, which is defensible, but document/anchor-level uncertainty is simply absent. Also, seed 42 runs micro-batch 64×4 while seeds 137/256 run 128×2 (lines 21–23): paired geometry is identical, but cross-seed SD conflates seed with execution geometry. *Fix:* report an anchor/document-level bootstrap within seed (or the SD of anchor differences) and one run swapping micro-batch geometry to show the seed effect is geometry-free.

**W6. [MINOR] Rounding reconciliation needed at three points.** Sec. 4.1 reports derived-minus-log-linear "+59.92" and derived-minus-YaRN "+52.54", while Table 2's rounded cells give 59.91 and 52.53; the bootstrap-CI centers (59.84; 52.47) sit slightly off the quoted point estimates, suggesting percentile intervals of unrounded statistics — fine, but say so. App. supporting (lines 49–50): "8K PPL changes by +0.9%" vs. Table 3 cells 35.4→35.8 (+1.13%); quote unrounded means. *Fix:* one footnote: "differences computed from unrounded values; CIs are percentile intervals."

**W7. [MINOR] Co-adaptation interaction CI names an unclear unit.** "Configuration-level bootstrap CI [−5.17, −3.04]" (Sec. 3.3 lines 158–161), but the described diagnostic is one configuration (50M, L=512, two seed-42 weights, 1,920 head–query observations). Is the bootstrap over observations, heads, or layers? As written, "configuration-level" over a single configuration is vacuous. *Fix:* name the resampling unit (I suspect head–query observation) and state it.

**W8. [MINOR] Reproducibility gaps for full re-execution.** (i) The 50.9M factorial never enumerates its grid values (2 bases — presumably {256, 500K}? 2 training lengths? 3 head dimensions?) anywhere in the manuscript; (ii) the video DiT table gives optimizer, RoPE base, activation but not LR, batch, steps, or the 3D axis partition, and it is the "zero-search reference" sweep whose τ=2.83 formula is quoted; (iii) 8B LoRA LR/dropout and the step counts (300 vs. 516) are partially specified; (iv) evaluation corpora (RULER generators, 2Wiki, Qasper, PG-19 subsets) are not revision-pinned, though the 20260728/29 generator seeds help; (v) no compute/hardware budget appears anywhere in paper-2027 despite ~2.6B tokens of 750M/1.485B-class training. The claim "Seed scope is stated with every result" (Reproducibility statement, line 21) is true of tables and appendix but not of body sentences: e.g., Sec. 4.3's 750M sentence omits that it is seed-42-only. *Fix:* add a design-values table per protocol and a compute appendix entry.

**W9. [MINOR] Submission-source hygiene: a dangling experiment.** `tables/table_evq_ramp.tex` (454M, 3-seed, PK@8K 100±0%) exists in the submission tree but is never `\input` by main.tex, and its caption cross-references App. `\ref{sec:ramp-scaler}`, which is defined nowhere in the compiled paper. Likewise `appendix/a4_supporting_experiments.tex` is not included. If the compiled PDF is the submission, this table is dead weight that a GC of the supplement will surface as an unexplained artifact; if it was meant to be evidence, it is missing from the paper. *Fix:* include it (with the ramp-operator definition and correct statistical unit) or delete it from the archive.

**W10. [MINOR] Training sufficiency and single-trajectory weight in the scale ladder.** The 151.9M control trains 3.3 tokens/param and the 432M MLA 1.16 tokens/param — far below compute-optimal — so the fixed-support win is an early-trajectory phenomenon; the from-init 1.485B (2.097B tokens, ~1.4 tok/param, still short) is the only scale companion, and it is a single trajectory. The crossover narrative in Sec. 5 ("the comparisons repeat a within-protocol crossover") aggregates 750M, MLA, 8B, and 1.485B, of which three are single-seed; the pooled *impression* of the ladder is stronger evidence than any ladder rung individually provides. The paper mostly scopes this correctly ("in the tested protocols", "the tested ordering for this architecture and protocol"), and I acknowledge that, but I would still like one multi-seed replication at ≥750M for a headline OOD number. *Fix/evidence:* a second seed on either the 750M continuation or the MLA 16K PPL would settle most of this.

## 4. Questions for authors

- **Q1.** For the 151.9M control: can you report a bootstrap or anchor-level interval for the mean fixed-range contrasts (e.g., −0.281±0.192), given that 32×128 = 4K evaluated tokens dominate the per-seed noise?
- **Q2.** Why does the target-matched ordering reverse, and what prediction does the spectral-budget theory make about *when* allocation beats retargeting? As stated (App. identification "Reading" ¶), the paper's own appendix frames the fixed-range win as policy-conditional; would the authors expect a YaRN-style transport-plus-reallocated-interior arm to dominate both current arms at 2K?
- **Q3.** For 750M (τ=1.5), OLMo-2 1.485B (τ=2, endpoint grid), and 8B (τ=1.414): were these τ values rule-generated (under what $L_{\rm train}$) or chosen, and were they fixed before results were inspected? (See W2.)
- **Q4.** Which nine RULER tasks are the "unseen-nine", what do the other four families score under Native/log-linear/derived, and what artifact documents that the table/amplitude/split were frozen before evaluation?
- **Q5.** In the factorial, the deformation-matched exponential ties the EVQ rule (+0.00074, p=0.836). Do the authors agree this means the *identity of the shape* (Cosh vs. any non-uniform deformation) is unidentified by the factorial, and the Cosh family's privileged status comes only from the static surrogate?
- **Q6.** What is the resampling unit of the co-adapt interaction CI [−5.17, −3.04], and does it use all 1,920 head–query observations or per-model aggregates?
- **Q7.** For the 1.485B selective Q/K protocol: both arms train only 8.39M parameters for 300 steps; is there evidence the ordering is not an artifact of one step-count sweet spot (e.g., one alternative step count per arm)?
- **Q8.** Sensitivity of the derived arm to the squaring in $m_k=(1-\widetilde U_k)^2$ and to $s$: what happens at $m_k = 1-\widetilde U_k$ or $s=2,8$ on OLMo?

## 7. One-sentence recommendation to the area chair

Accept path exists and the core identification is real, but only if the authors bring the fixed-range conditionality into the body, document τ-selection and task-subset freezing in the mature protocols, and reconcile the non-significant formula-point contrast with the zero-search narrative — I recommend a conditional accept contingent on those clarifications in the author response.
