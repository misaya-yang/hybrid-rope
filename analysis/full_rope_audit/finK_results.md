## Verdict

Highest causal whitened effrank at L (per config):
- K16: **uniform** (erank_whit(L) = 31.9720); least L->4L relative loss: **geometric** (loss = -39.4225%)
- K32: **uniform** (erank_whit(L) = 63.9514); least L->4L relative loss: **geometric** (loss = -50.7023%)
- K64: **uniform** (erank_whit(L) = 127.7829); least L->4L relative loss: **geometric** (loss = -63.0496%)
- K64_b1e4: **uniform** (erank_whit(L) = 127.7829); least L->4L relative loss: **geometric** (loss = -74.3961%)
- K32_L1024: **uniform** (erank_whit(L) = 63.6186); least L->4L relative loss: **geometric** (loss = -54.1120%)

Aggregate over 5 configs — highest erank_whit at L: uniform 5/5.
Aggregate — least relative erank_whit loss from L to 4L: geometric 5/5.

Interpretation (read the two aggregates with care):
- **uniform** wins the effrank ceiling *by construction*: it places no channel below 2*pi/L
  (n(wL<=1)=0 at every length), so it is the "healthy zone only" allocation and saturates effrank
  at ~2K. It pays with zero low-frequency coverage — a different axis than collision quality.
- **full-opt and logdet-opt are numerically the same allocation** (tables agree to 4 decimals at all
  lengths): erank_whit reaches 124.3/128 = 97.3% of the uniform ceiling while retaining the pinned
  low-frequency endpoint (w_min = 1/b, nlow = 1), with the lowest C_full (~1.09 vs 516.7 geometric)
  and essentially zero L->4L drift (loss = -0.13%, i.e. a slight gain). cos-opt is a close variant
  (C_cos 1.0005 vs 1.0005; erank_whit ~2.7% lower, C_full 2.98 at L).
- **geometric's "least relative loss" is an artifact**: its losses are strongly *negative*
  (e.g. -63.0% for K=64) because it starts with 24/64 channels in the collapsed zone (wL<=1) and
  effrank *grows* as channels exit collapse with length. Negative loss = improvement from a
  collapsed baseline, not extrapolation stability. The genuinely stable families (loss <= 0.2%
  in magnitude) are full-opt, logdet-opt and uniform; cos-opt drifts ~2.7% (all upward).
- The three objectives (min C_cos, min C_full, max logdet) converge to near-identical optima on
  the causal training grid (C_cos floor ~1.0005, C_full floor ~1.094 for K=64, b=5e5), and all
  restarts of full/logdet find the same basin — the collision-optimal configuration appears
  essentially unique, and it coincides with near-maximal whitened effrank.
